Final Instructions - added 12th March

The Final Instructions for Competitors are now available as well as the Indemnity Form. Please share the instructions with all your fellow team members and be sure to bring a copy of the Indemnity Form to the event as you will not be registered until you lodge an Indemnity completed by all team members.

More pictures - added 12th March

Have a look at pictures of the general terrain.

15hr downtime - added 10th March

People in the 15hr event may head back to Blenheim overnight if they wish.

Where to stay? - added 6th March

For people trying to make accommodation decisions for Friday and Sunday nights Blenheim is recommended. There is no advantage in staying north or south of the town.

Control Collection Entry refund - added 4th March

Anyone who collects 12 or more controls (as identified by Mondo) after the event (during the week or following weekend) will have their individual entry refunded.

Bus from Picton - added 4th March

Alan Wilson will run a bus from Picton and return. See the details.

Entries now open

Juniors - U23

For the New Zealand Championships the Junior category is for people under 23 years of age on the first day of competition.
