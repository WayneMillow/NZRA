WHY ENTER A CHAMPIONSHIP ROGAINE?

Because they are fun - and that's what rogaining is all about.

We only call it a Championship because it has to meet certain quality standards to be eligible as a World Championship pre-qualifying event. This means that you get a well organised event to have fun in.

You have done a 6 or 7 hour rogaine, maybe even a 12 hour one. Can you do 24 hours? Probably not, you say. Well, don't be put off. It's easier than you think, and definitely not as hard as two 12-hour rogaines. Here's why:

  • Firstly, you go slower because you know you have to last longer.
  • Secondly, you go much slower at night because of the extra navigational challenge. It is also cooler, so instead of sweating and chafing, you are cool and dry and really enjoy the walk.
  • Thirdly, you can return to the hash-house for a few hours feed and sleep - many people do.
  • Fourthly, it's really great walking under a full moon. If you haven't ever done it, you must.

So for you, this is not a "Championship" - it is a rare occasion to enjoy a great occasion on excellent country. Come along and enjoy.
