Calendar of Events 2001
1st - 2nd December 2001
Duration: 18hrs (4pm - 10am)
Location: Awatere Valley		 Merinos & Matagouri by Moonlight
This is mixed country with extensive areas of rolling hills punctuated by steep mountain sides and impassable steep-walled streams. The map is 1:50,000. Organisers: Alan Holdaway and Susan King. See the Results.
