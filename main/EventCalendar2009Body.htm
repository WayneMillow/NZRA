Sat 2nd - Sun 3rd May
Duration: 24hrs
Location: Canterbury
 T'Walk 2009
This will be the 43rd running of this event, organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club, the pioneer of long distance foot navigation events in NZ. It differs from a Rogaine in that teams follow a fixed route. A "not to be missed" event. Detailed information and entry form are available on the T'Walk website. Entries close 22nd April.
Sunday 17th May
Duration: 3hr & 6hr options
Location: Wellington		 Metlink City Safari
The City Safari allows you to use Public Transport: bus, train, cablecar, ferry. You can use your head instead of your legs to get partway to the checkpoints. For more information see the event website www.citysafari.org.nz.
