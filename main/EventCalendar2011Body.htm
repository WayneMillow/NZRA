Calendar of Events 2011

Weekend
22nd- 23rd January
Duration: 24hrs
Location: Tasmania		 2011 Australian Rogaining Championships
To be hosted by the Rogaining Tasmania in the northern midlands of Tasmania. The course will be set by experienced rogainers John Brock, Darryl Smith, Karen Pedley and Nigel Davies. The terrain will be a mixture of open plains (farmland) and steeper lightly wooded hills. See the event website.
Midweek Series
beginning 2nd March
Duration: 3hrs
Location: Port Hills,
Canterbury		 R&R Sport Taylors Mistake Rogaine Series
Postponed due to ChCh Earthquake
Nora Audra and Ian Edmond are organising the popular R&R Sport Taylors Mistake Rogaine Series again. The series starts on March 2nd and will run for 5 successive Wednesdays visiting a different part of the Port Hills each week. The events will be three hours long. For more information and an entry form visit http://www.madeventz.co.nz/tmrogaine.
Sat-Sun 19-20 March
Duration: 24hr & 15 in 24hr
Location: Marlborough		 2011 New Zealand Championships
The 2011 NZ Champs will be in Marlborough with Mondo Kopua as chief planner. The date has been changed from the original and is now confirmed as weekend 19-20 March. For further information see the website:
http://rogaine.org.nz/nzchamps2011/ .
There is a shorter less stressful 15 in 24hr associated event as well as the 24hr championship.
Sunday 20th March
Duration: 4hr
Location: Waitakere City		 Eco-city Challenge
4 hour urban navigation challenge on the extensive Track & Park network in Waitakere City. Teams of 2-5 people run, walk or take the bus. Event website at http://www.lacticturkey.co.nz/
Sat 14th - Sun 15th May
Duration: 24hrs
Location: Canterbury
 T'Walk 2011
This will be the 45th running of this event, organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club, the pioneer of long distance foot navigation events in NZ. It differs from a Rogaine in that teams follow a fixed route. It is great fun and a "not to be missed" event. For full details and entry information see the T'Walk website. Entries are now open
Sat 14th & Sun 15th May
Duration: 3hr
Location: Living Springs, Canterbury
 Wicked Weekend incorporating an R&R Sport Foot Rogaine
Nora Audra and Ian Edmond are organising an R&R Sport Rogaine in conjunction with the inaugural Wicked Weekend. There will be a 1hr Family Rogaine and various MTB events as well as the 3hr Rogaine. For details and entry information consult the websites www.madeventz.co.nz/tmrogaine and www.huxster.org.nz.
Sunday 15th May
Duration: 3hr & 6hr options
Location: Wellington		 Metlink City Safari
The City Safari allows you to use Public Transport: bus, train, cablecar, ferry. You can use your head instead of your legs to get partway to the checkpoints. For more information see the event website www.citysafari.org.nz.
Saturday 28th May
Duration: 12 & 6 hrs
Location: Wanaka region		 Highland Events Wanaka Rogaine
Based at the Ardgour Station Woolshed, Thomson Gorge Rd, Tarras. For event details including a map of the general area of the course see the website http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/rogaine. Entries are now open. The cryptic clues used in the past are to be replaced this year by a more traditional Rogaining format focusing on navigation and strategy.
Saturday 18th June
Duration: 12hr & 6hr
Location: Amuri, Canterbury		 Heights of Winter 2011
Peninsula and Plains Orienteers are the organisers this year. Chief organiser is Stuart Payne assisted by Ian Edmond and Nora Audra. There will be 12hr and 6hr rogaines in the Amuri area, North Canterbury. The terrain is attractive rolling hill country west of Culverden: photo1, photo2, photo3. A great event is assured so mark it in your calendar. See the PAPO website for information and online or paper entry. Entries close 6th June.
Fri 8th/Sat 9th July
Duration: 12hr
Location: West Auckland		 Bethells 12-hour Rogaine
Registration at Bethells Beach reserve next to the carpark from 10:30pm Friday 8th July, maps given out at 11:30pm, start at midnight. Finish Saturday at noon. Controls located no further north than Goldie's bush, and no further south than Piha. Hot food and tents will be set up at Bethells Reserve HQ. Teams of 3-5. Either enter as a team, or as an individual and a team will be arranged for you. Email alastairmcdowell@hotmail.com to register. $10 entry fee. Entries close 3rd July.
Sunday 10th July
Duration: 6 & 3hr
Location: Dunedin		 Hamel’s Haunts Rogaine
Registration and entry on the day at Halfway Bush School, Dunedin from 9:30am, start 11:00am. Entry fee of $25 includes light meal and prizes. Teams of 2 to 5 people. Contact is Matt Bixley, matt.bixley@agresearch.co.nz or phone 021 0469255 or 03 4763869. This is a fundraising event for Halfway Bush School & Commonwealth 24 Hour Championships.

8th European Championships
Sat-Sun 23-24 July 2011, 24hrs, Latvia
Located in the north part of Vidzeme highlands known as Mezoles hills, the terrain is partly coniferous and mixed forests of different ages, partly timber felling sites, and partly open fields and meadows. There is a semi-developed network of local and forest roads, but some areas are without any roads. See the event website.
Sunday 14th August
Duration: 4 & 3hrs
Location: Springfield,
      Canterbury		 Annavale Attack and Amble
A navigational adventure in the Springfield region of the Canterbury foothills in support of Darfield HS Rural College. Mark it in your calendar. The 4-hour rogaine (Attack) is for the competitive and the 3-hour rogaine (Amble) is intended to be more recreational. For information and an entry form see the Darfield High School website. Entries close Tuesday 9th August.
Sunday 21st August
Duration: 3hr
Location: Wellington area		 25th Annual Winter Classic - Auld Land Syne
The Winter Classic is an endurance, long distance orienteering event that is both physically and mentally demanding. There is a 3hr Challenge Rogaine run in conjunction with the event. See the Classic website for details. Entries close 14th August.
Sunday 21st August
Duration: 3hrs
Location: Auckland		 Lactic Turkey Riverhead Rogaine
A 3hr event in the Riverhead Forest which is at the head of the Waitemata Harbour, north-west of Auckland. Information and entry form are available on the Lactic Turkey Events website. Entries close 17th August.
Beginning Sat 27th August
Duration: 4½ & 3hrs
Location: Canterbury		 Canterbury Rogaine Series
The Canterbury Series consists of three events planned for 27th August, 17th September and 8th October. The events are suitable for all ages and abilities but the school competition, open to all high school students (free entry), is a special feature. Organisers Tim Farrant, Jess Collingwood and Rowan Nugteren will be running training workshops at local schools leading up to the rogaines. Email Jess at info@canterburyrogaine.com to nominate your school.
     Proceeds from the events will fund travel grants for Canterbury juniors to compete at domestic and international rogaining events. For more information about the series, and to enter online, see the website http://www.canterburyrogaine.com/
Saturday 17th September

Location: Earnscleugh Station		 Mt. Outdoors Rogaine
Organised by Ed Stevens and Terry Davis in the Clyde region of Otago. See the website http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/rogaine for more information. Enter before Monday 12th September to avoid a late fee.
Sunday 25th September

Sunday 4th December
   (originally Saturday)
Duration: 7hrs
Location: St. James, Hanmer		 Office Hours 2011
Join NZRA for a pre-Christmas treat - rogaine in the Clarence and Waiau basins and ranges beyond Hanmer Springs. This mid-duration event in the high country will suit newcomers and trampers as well as regular rogainers. Make a weekend of it – we'll be based near the recently opened St James National MTB Cycle Trail (http://nzbybike.com/st-james-cycle-trail/) and you can camp for free at the old St James homestead.
     See the Information sheet for further details. Note that participants are limited to 200 to ensure we go lightly on the extensive natural wetlands in the area. Enter on-line now and have a look at the List of Entrants. Entries close 5pm Wednesday 23rd November; late entries at the organiser’s discretion and for an additional fee of $15 per person. For enquiries contact either Chrys Horn (chrys.horn@xtra.co.nz), Ph (03) 338 0313 or Grant Hunter (g.hunter@clear.net.nz), Ph (03) 351-6369.
