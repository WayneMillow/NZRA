Saturday, 20th December

Duration: 4hrs
Location: Eastbourne,
        Wellington		 Christmas Party Rogaine
A 4 hour Saturday afternoon rogaine in an area never used before, 25 mins from Wellington city, followed by a beach BBQ. Organised by Mike Sheridan, it is a unique event that can be done on MTB and/or foot. The event starts with a short bike ride (15-30 mins depending on wind and fitness) to the Lakes, with a few controls available along the way. On arrival at the Lakes you are given a second detailed map of the Lakes area. This section you can choose to do on MTB or on foot. (Foot rogainers receive a 25% bonus). Bikes can be left at a designated area, complete with a security guard. You will need to allow time to ride back along the coast road to Burdens Gate to arrive prior to 6pm.

The best thing about the Christmas Party Rogaine is that its free to enter but there will be a $10 charge for the BBQ after the event. Registration and maps from 1pm, start 2pm, finish 6pm, followed by the BBQ on the beach.

Refer to the event website for further information.
