In October 2002 the first AGM of the NZRA was held. Present were four people who have been contributing to rogaining even before then and continue. They are life members of NZRA.

Peter Squires

A multiple winner of age group events both in New Zealand and worldwide

Bill Kennedy

A multiple winner of age group events both in New Zealand and worldwide

Grant Hunter

A multiple winner of age group events

Bob Cunninghame

Bob Cunningham has been instrumental in the development and rogaining in New Zealand. As the Otago regional representative of the NZRA, Bob has hosted and supported numerous New Zealand Championships, including Hummock Station (2009), Mahingarangi (2012), El Dorado (2016) and Waikaia (2017).
