2022 Results

  • Australasian Rogaining Championship 2022 - From Saturday 08 October To Sunday 09 October - Avoca, Victoria, Australia
    The course was on Dja Dja Wurrung land in the Pyrenees Ranges, approximately 220km and 2.5hrs drive northwest of Melbourne. Associated with the championship event was a 6 hour rogaine. See the Event Website Australasian Rogaining Championship 2022 for results.

  • Wily Weka Rogaine - Sunday Sep 11th - 4 hrs - St Arnaud, Nelson
    Wily Weka has been a major event on the Nelson calendar for many years now and attracted many entrants who found out why. See NOC Results for results.

  • Cromwell Orchard Fun Rogaine - Sunday Sep 11th - 3 hours - Cromwell, Central Otago
    Great fun was had with a lot of family teams and lots of controls. See Orchard Rogaine for results.

  • Central Otago Orienteers Winter Rogaine - Sunday Aug 28th - 4hr or 2hr - Alexandra, Central Otago
    It was a fantastic day at Goulburn Station for the Central Otago Orienteers fundraising rogaine. Results have been emailed out to participants.

  • 17th World Rogaining Championship 2022 - From Friday 26 August To Saturday 27 August - Czech Republic
    Unfortuately there was only one New Zealand team this year at the World Championships. Matt and Monty certainly provided entertainment for the "dot watchers" back home. See the Event Website World Rogaining Championship 2022 for details.

  • Wicked Rogaines - Winter Night Series - Wednesdays 8, 15, 22 Jun, 10, 17, 24 Aug - 1 & 2 hours - Port Hills Christchurch
    Although the end of this series brings promises of spring, it also ends the enjoyment of a Wednesday night in the darkness running around the treets and hills. See Winter Series Results for results.

  • Port Hills Spring Rogaine - Sunday Aug 21st - 6hr or 3hr - Tai Tapu, western Port Hills
    No matter how many events that are run in the Port Hills, there always seems to be somewhere that you haven't been before. And it is always fun not matter what the conditions. Six Hour Results Three Hour Results

  • Wicked Bog Roy Rogaine - Sunday Aug 14th - 6, 4, 2hr & family event - Bog Roy Station, Lake Benmore
    This location provided perfect rogaining country and after weeks of rain and cold weather, the day of the event dawned cold but clear and still. There was something for everyone, young and old, experienced and newbie with magnificent vistas visible in every direction. See Bog Roy Results for results.

  • NOCturnal Rogaine Series - Wednesdays 15 Jun, 13 Jul, 12 Aug - 1 or 2hr - Nelson area
    It is over for another year and people are already looking forward to next year. Check out the results at Nelson Orienteering Club Results.

  • Wicked Winter 6 hour Rogaine - Sunday Jul 31st - 6, 4, 2 hour & family events - Weka Pass Waikari
    It was winter but blue sky came out at the end so everyone was able to enjoy this unique area. See Weka Pass Results for results.

  • Highland Events Snowgaine - Saturday 30th Jul - 6 & 3 hour options - Snowfarm, Cardrona Valley
    The previous days' weather put off a lot of people but those who made the effort were not disappointed. This is a unique event that should not be missed See Highland Events for results.

  • Great Forest Rogaine - Saturday Jul 23rd - 3 and 6 hour events - Whakarewarewa , Rotorua
    This event has been going for many years and gets better every year as evidenced by the lucky people who were there. See Orienteering Bay of Plenty for results.

    For results go to www.noc.org.nz/event/nocturnal-rogaine-series.

  • Heights of Winter - Saturday Jun 11th - 12 & 6 hour events - Woodbury, near Geraldine
    The weather bomb that dropped snow over a lot of the South Island missed the course area so although there were remnants of previous snow and a cold wind, the weather was otherwise OK. A full field in both the 12- and 6-hour events enjoyed a great course that encompassed a wide variety of terrain with areas of native bush and open paddocks, and from flat to steep. The description of what to do if cornered by a bull in the final information caused some consternation among some competitors but the event was enjoyed by all competitors without major incident.
    Results with route analysis at Heights of Winter - Results
    Also NZRA Results and NZRA Results (PDF).

    Results will be at Results - TWALK.

  • Highland Events Autumn Rogaine - Saturday April 23rd - 3, 6 & 12 hour events - Lindis Valley
    The conditions couldn't have been better for an outstanding course. There was climb for those that love it but it was not too steep and could mostly be avoided and the travelling was generally easy. Snow the previous week added and early chill but with no wind and clear skys competitors were able to enjoy the course and the vistas that it provided.
