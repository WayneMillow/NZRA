Calendar of Events 2003

18th - 19th January 2003
Duration: 24hrs
Location: Tasmania
Entries close: 13th December		 24th Australian Rogaining Championships
To be held in Tasmania near the town of Ross which is 80km south of Launceston and 120km north of Hobart. Organiser: Nick Bowden, Email: Nick.Bowden@dpiwe.tas.gov.au. More details and entry form are available on the Rogaining Tasmania website. See the reports by Annie Sanderson and Alan Holdaway.
15th - 16th February 2003
Duration: 2 days (8hrs + 4hrs)
Location: Central Otago
Entries close: 31st January		 The Pedal E.P.I.C.
A mountainbike navigational challenge for teams of 2 - 4. Cryptic clues. Pick your own course navigating along roads and 4WD tracks. Enjoy the fascinating landscape and stunning views. Organiser: Bruce McLeod, Email: bmcleod@es.co.nz or Ph 03 441 4447. Results and comment are available on the EPIC website.
15th - 16th March 2003
Duration: 24hrs
Location: Roxburgh, Otago
Entries close: 20th February		 The Roxburgh Ramble – NZ Championships
This is a classic 24hr event. Mark it in your calendar. Don't be fooled by the "Championship" label — the event is open to everybody. See Event Details for further information and to download an entry form. See the planners report and pictures of the course. Results, organiser's report, and event pictures are available.

Wednesday 22nd October
Duration: 3hrs after-work
Location: Port Hills, Christchurch		 Port Hills Twilight Rogaine
Organised by Chris Forne and helpers. Start/finish at Rapaki valley carpark at the end of Hillsborough Road. Registration from 5:30pm to 6:30pm, with maps given out at 6:30pm. Everyone will then have half an hour to plan their route before a mass start at 7.00pm. After finishing at 10pm there will be a brief prize giving and supper. Cost is $12 per competitor. For further information and entry form see the event website. Results are now available.
