20th - 21st May
Duration: 24hrs
Location: Canterbury
 T'Walk
This will be the 40th running of this event, organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club, the pioneer of long distance foot navigation events in NZ. It differs from a Rogaine in that teams follow a fixed route. A "not to be missed" event. An accurate and fair course is assured with Chris Forne as chief setter. Consult the T'Walk website for further information and entry form. Contact person is: Emily Wall (emily.wall@gmail.com)

Saturday 25th November
Duration: 7hrs
Location: Canterbury		 'Heights of High Country' - Office Hours Rogaine
Treat yourself to a 7 hour rogaine before Christmas sets in. This event is in the Canterbury eastern high country between Mt Somers and Lakes Clearwater and Heron. It’s located between the Rangitata and Ashburton Rivers, where you’ve probably never been before. Modelled on a similar event at Lake Lyndon in 2003, it will be suitable for trampers and others to try rogaining and to test and develop map reading skills. There are no grade and gender classes, all teams being created equal. Details and entry form are available. Entries close 15th November. Enquiries to Grant Hunter, g.hunter@clear.net.nz. If you are looking for a partner see Partners Wanted. Also, see the list of Entries. And now Instructions to Competitors.
Saturday 9th December
Duration: 12/8/4hrs
Location: Napier area		 December Dash
The December Dash will be held on 85 sq km of Hawkes Bay farmland (65%) and pine forest of varying maturity (35%). More information on the event website. Entries close Monday 27th November.
