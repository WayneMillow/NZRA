Location: East Otago		 Never Look Back

Duration: 24hrs
Location: North Finland		 13th World Rogaining Championships
The 13th World Rogaining Championships will be run in North Finland. The event centre in the Saariselkä region will host the first ever World Rogaining Championships to be held above the Arctic Circle. At about 68 degrees north, the competition area in the Urho Kekkosen National Park with its navigationally difficult wetlands and challenging hills covered by open tundra, are sure to be a thorough test of the world’s best rogaining teams. Saturday 28th November
Duration: 4 and 7 hrs
Location: Eastbourne near Wellington		 Big Trig 2015
The 3rd Big Trig organised by O Hutt Valley is planned for 28th November near Wellington. OHV ran NZ's first rogaine in 1991. Since then we've worked up to the championship 24-hour rogaine (Tararua in 2006, Moonshine in 2010) and we've worked DOWN to afterwork 3-hour and even 1-hour mini rogaines. But each year we like to put on a "middle-sized" rogaine that appeals to all groups - tough adventurer racers to weekend warriors to family groups. It's usually 8-10-12 hours long. Of course you can sample the area with a short option. "The Big Trig" is our name for for it. Big means its bigger than our regular rogaines. Trig means there's some hills. The subtitle usually indicates where it is. We've got a huge amount of terrain all around us - the start is usually within an hour of downtown Wellington. For further information see the event website.
