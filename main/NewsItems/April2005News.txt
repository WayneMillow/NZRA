Other events that are not too far off are -

The B-Hynd Rogaine (Marlborough) - Saturday 28th May
====================================================
A Marlborough special planned by Jut Bishop and Lachy Hynd for the Marlborough
Orienteering Club. 3 & 6hr events - a good warmup for the 12hr Heights of Winter
(Canterbury 18th June). Enjoy the challenging terrain that Marlborough has on offer.
See the event details on the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz . Entries close
24th May.


Lactic Turkey Piha Rogaine - Saturday 11th June
================================================
A popular annual event in Auckland, with 3hr and 6hr options, organised by Lactic
Turkeys (Phil Wood and Shaun Collins). See the Lactic Turkey Events website,
http://www.lacticturkey.co.nz/ , for more information and entry form. Entries close
6th June.


Heights of Winter - Saturday 18th June
========================================
This 12hr event in Canterbury is being organised this year by Peninsula and Plains
Orienteers. Bruce Meder and John Howard are the course planners. More details should
be available soon - keep your eye on the NZRA website.
