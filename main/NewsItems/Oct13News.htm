Events coming up

Canterbury Series "Hogs Back" Rogaine
Castle Hill Village, Canterbury, Saturday 19th October
          Tim Farrant and team are putting together a series of events with the first on October 19th based at the Castle Hill Village community centre. There will be 3hr and 6hr options. Visit the event website, http://canterburyrogaine.com/, for further information and entry. Entries close 17th October, or when full, so get yours in and don’t miss out.


Karekare Gladefest
Karekare, West Auckland, Saturday 16th November
          This is yet another rogaine assembled by Shaun Collins and is in support of the Lone Kauri Community School. Based at the Pohutukawa Glade the course takes advantage of beach and bush with event durations of 90 minutes or 3hrs. Full details and entry are available from the Lactic Turkey website. Post-rogaine activity includes live music from local bands, face painting and lolly scrambles for children, and hot food and coffee for all.


The Golden Road Rogaine
Otago, Saturday 23rd November
           The Golden Road Rogaine is based at the El Dorado Woolshed on El Dorado Road. Organised by Bob Cunninghame and team, there will be a 13-hour “Classic”, a 6-hour “Introduction” and a 3-hour “Family Special”. The terrain, west of Waikouaiti and about an hour’s drive from Dunedin, is “interesting country” – relatively open farmland and tussock with rocks and scrub in places, plus some forest blocks, and even several distinct volcanic cones. Some valleys are steep sided but reasonable routes that minimise the ups and downs are possible. Download the event flyer. Full event information and on-line entry will be available on the NZRA website, http://rogaine.org.nz/ from mid-October.


Belmont Rogaine
Belmont Regional Park, Wellington, Saturday 30th November
         Belmont Regional Park is a 3500ha triangle from the top to the bottom of Lower Hutt, and across to Cannons Creek, Porirua. It features farmland up to 456m with views to the South Island, 60 unusual WW2 ammunition bunkers, extensive areas of native bush, and more. There will be 10hr and 6hr options. The event is being organised by Michael Wood under the Orienteering Hutt Valley banner. Download a flyer and check the event website for all the detail.

New Plymouth Urban Afterwork Series, 30th October to 4th December

Recent Event Results

Mt Outdoors Bannockburn Rogaine, Central Otago, 14th September

Fortunately the threatened snow showers didn’t eventuate and 365 people enjoyed fine weather but brisk temperatures at Bannockburn. Organisers Terry Davis and Ed Stevens comment:

“The Highland Events Mt Outdoors Bannockburn Rogaine,was a resounding success from an organisers’ perspective and hopefully from a participants view as well. We had wonderful weather and a near capacity field (400) with some of the top navigators in the world participating, with the likes of Greg Hamilton, Tim Farrant, and Phil Wood all handing in very impressive score cards. Chris Forne won the 6hr mountain bike section; not competing on foot due to injury. Scores and placings can be seen on the Highland Events website http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/rogaine .

There were two standout aspects to this event from Highland Events viewpoint. First was the increase in the number of 12hr competitors. We had a quality field of 112 which is a huge increase for us and very rewarding as it does take a lot of effort to put a 12hr course together. Secondly, we had 107 entrants in the 3hr family event which indicates that the sport is set to grow and develop in our region.

The six hour event was also up on other years with 146 competitors.  The top score of 1760 to a local team of adventure racers, Simon Bowden, Bob McLachan and Kris Mayen, was very impressive.

Lastly, Highland Events would like to thank all the competitors for coming along and making this event very memorable.  Many of you travelled a great distance to compete. Terry and I hope you enjoyed competing as much as we enjoyed putting the event on”.

 And from Greig Hamilton, 12hr winner:
“I thought the map and distribution of the controls was really good, it made it hard to find a good route. I don't think our route was very good especially compared to Tim's (Farrant).  We had more climb and their route flowed much better so Tim did well on the planning.  Emily (Wall) and Katie (Bolt) also had a much better route than ours even though it was quite different to Tim's in the first section.

We were really impressed with the quality of event that Terry and Ed put on. Good area, good control placements, hard to plan a route, no chance of getting all the controls, just all round good fun”.

The next Highland Events rogaine is pencilled in for Saturday 3rd May 2014. The likely venue is in the Lindis Valley on the Shirimar, Geordie Hills and Morven Hills Stations, subject to final landowner approval.  Mark it in your calendar.


Welly Afterwork, Tuesday 10th September
          The 6th event in the 2013 Welly Afterwork Series was run on Tuesday 10th September. Despite a grim weather forecast 92 runners and walkers turned up for the event which was shortened to 2.5 hrs given the impending wetness. A report by organiser Ian Harrison and the results may be found at http://www.mapsport.co.nz/wellyrog/awrog13res.html.



11th World Rogaining Championships, Russia, 26th - 27th July          
     

Vivienne Prince’s report on her experiences in the Russian event took a bit longer than expected to make it onto the website so you may have missed it. Take a look now.

Viv and team mate Richard Robinson (Australia) may be disappointed with their “24 hours of aimless wanderings in the swamp” but they really did quite well and are credited with being 16th (amongst 314 teams) in the most efficient planners stakes.




12th World Rogaining Championships – registration opens 1st November
Black Hills, South Dakota, 16 & 17 August, 2014
           If you are keen to participate in these championships you should register within 14 days of registrations opening to maximise your chances of gaining entry. There are a total of 1000 places available. Some of these are reserved for top performers in previous WRCs, regional championships and national championships but many non-prequalifying people should get in. For further explanation of the entry process see the note on registration on the NZRA website. Also keep up to date via the event website, http://peakassurance.net/, and the event blog, http://peakassurance.net/Blog/Blog.html.
