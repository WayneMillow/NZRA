Events coming up

Next Weekend – Karekare 12 Hour Rogaine – Next Weekend
Waitakere Ranges, Auckland, midnight Friday 13th to noon Saturday 14th July

Alastair McDowell advises that the Midnight 12 Hour Rogaine is ON for its second year. The event base will be at Karekare Beach, Waitakere Ranges. Assemble at the beach carpark on Friday night to register from 10:30pm, event briefing at 11:20pm, maps given out at 11:30pm, and you'll be off at the Crack of Midnight. So if you are keen for 12 hours of cryptic clues & midwinter midnight madness in the bush sign up now at: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheet/viewform?formkey=dFFHMUpvdkQtc2VjQ3RGMEozbVM0dnc6MQ

The organisers are Alastair McDowell, Leighton Watson & Sylvie Admore of the Auckland University Tramping Club. If you have any questions just email Alastair at alastairmcdowell@hotmail.com.


Ayrdale Attack and Amble, Springfield, Canterbury, Sunday 19th August
           The 3 & 4 hour Annavale A & A has morphed into the Ayrdale A & A for this year to reflect the dominant region of the map. The registration and event centre is still at Annavale - people will recall how cosy that woolshed was as snow fell outside during the meal and prize-giving last year. For information and an entry form see the Darfield High School website, http://www.darfield.school.nz/rural. The organisers are once again Carol Fowler & Stuart Payne for the Darfield HS Rural College. Entries close Wednesday 15th August.


Mt Outdoors Wanaka Rogaine, Central Otago, Saturday 15th September
             Ed Stevens and Terry Davis of Highland Events are planning their Spring Rogaine again this year with 12hr and 6 hr events in inspiring Central Otago landscapes. Keep your eye on the Highland Events website, http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/rogaine, for more information as it comes to hand.


2012 New Zealand Championships, Otago, weekend 24th - 25th November
Organisation of the New Zealand Championships, Moonlight on Mahinerangi, is proceeding well. Organiser-in-chief is Bob Cunninghame. Full information will be available on the NZRA website soon.

Even though the event is billed as a “championship” remember it caters for all tastes from top-class super-fit competitors to the strictly social rogainers who may return to the hash house for food and rest at any time. In addition to the 24hr event there will be a shorter 7hr non-championship event on the Saturday afternoon.


Wellington Afterwork Series, April to November
    This series of 3hr afterwork Rogaines in the Wellington area under the auspices of O Hutt Valley continues at monthly intervals through to November. The next event on 2nd August is in the Wrights Hill/Makara Peak region and planner is Rob Vanderpoel. See the Maptalk calendar, http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine, for details.

Recent Events

Hamel’s Haunts, Dunedin, Sunday 17th June
          “Snow all week didn’t seem to deter the nearly 70 people who turned up to the Rogaine on Sunday, nor did the -5 degrees at Leith Saddle around 8am” – they do breed them tough down south!! Organiser Matt Bixley was out the evening before the event digging controls out of the snow although he gave one away when he realised it was a waist-deep wade to it.

For Matt’s thoughts on his event see his blog at http://runlongergeek.wordpress.com/. (Skip past his Heights of Winter blog which, nevertheless, you are likely to find interesting too).


Heights of Winter, North Canterbury, Saturday 30th June
            The annual HOW saw a record number of people (400+) enjoy an excellent day out running helter-skelter over a brace of North Canterbury farms in brilliant weather. Led by Dave Laurie and Pete Squires, the organising team did a magnificent job ensuring that the event went off without a hitch. Congratulations to all those who took part but especially to Chris Forne and Greig Hamilton who, in winning the 12hr event, well and truly spanked the opposition by collecting all the checkpoints with an hour and a half to spare. There were some notable performances in the 6hr event too and it was pleasing to see so many Junior teams taking part.

Thanks to all competitors for turning out, particularly the considerable number who travelled from the North Island and from outside Canterbury in the South Is. Good numbers makes the huge effort put in by organisers all worthwhile.

Take a look at http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/Results/HOW2012Results.htm where you will find full results and links to (i) a map showing the winning route, (ii) reflections by Dave Laurie on behalf of the organising team, and (iii) an extensive gallery of pictures thanks to Mark Petty.

If you lost something at Heights of Winter see if it is listed at the top of the results sheet.
