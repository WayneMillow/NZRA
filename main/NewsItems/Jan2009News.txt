              New Zealand Championships Entries Open
              =======================================

Entries are now open for the 2009 New Zealand Championships to be run in Eastern Otago
weekend 7th/8th March. There is an 8hr event in addition to the 24hr rogaine. You can
enter online at http://www.rogaine.org.nz or download and post an entry form. Buses,
provided from Christchurch and Dunedin, can be booked via the entry form.

See the NZRA website for complete information. With the next World Championships being
in NZ in November 2010 this event is an opportunity to gain some experience. While the
World Champs seems a long way off remember there are not many 24hr events in New
Zealand for you to practice on. To encourage people we are offering a free entry in
the World Champs to one lucky entrant in the 24hr event at these NZ Champs - see the
event info for this announcement.

If you are wondering what to expect of the course read the Planners' report by Pete
Squires and available on the website. There are some photos of the course there too.

Remember that although the event is billed as the NZ Champs it is a standard rogaine
that can be enjoyed by all-comers. You don't have to stay out in the field for the
full 24hrs. In fact, an attraction of the event is the continuous provision of food at
the hashhouse so you can come in, rest, have a feed and maybe a sleep, and then head
out again.

And, for the tertiary sudents, there is a "University Challenge".


The Tim Farrant School of Rogaining
===================================
The following announcement is from Tim Farrant, current Junior World Champion.


   "A newly established under 23 group will be running a series of public trainings
(open to all ages) around the Port Hills during the weeks leading up to this year's
New Zealand Rogaine Champs. The series will cover a number of Rogaine specific skills
that should prove handy for novices and the experienced too!

The trainings will run at 6:30pm on Tuesday evenings, starting Tuesday 3rd February,
at Sign of the Kiwi. Cost will be $3 for a map. Please register your interest with Tim
Farrant, tjf35@student.canterbury.ac.nz

Also:
Peninsula and Plains Orienteering club will be running a series of street score events
on Wed nights, starting on the 21st of Jan. The first event will be held at Hansens
Park in St Martins. Check out www.papo.org.nz for more details."


If other "schools" are planned elsewhere in NZ please let us know so we can advertise
them.
