New Zealand Rogaining Association (Inc.)
3rd July 2011
 

Dear Rogainer,

Two new Rogaines in Auckland and Dunedin are scheduled for next weekend.


Bethells 12-hour Rogaine, Auckland, Friday 8th/Saturday 9th July
        Registration at Bethells Beach reserve next to the carpark from 10:30pm Friday 8th July, maps given out at 11:30pm, start at midnight. Finish Saturday at noon. Controls located no further north than Goldie's bush, and no further south than Piha. Hot food and tents will be set up at Bethells Reserve HQ. Teams of 3-5. Either enter as a team, or as an individual and a team will be arranged for you. Email alastairmcdowell@hotmail.com to register. $10 entry fee. Entries close 3rd July, i.e. today so HURRY.


Hamel’s Haunts Rogaine, 3 & 6hr, Dunedin, Sunday 10th July
      Registration and entry on the day at Halfway Bush School from 9:30am, start 11:00am. Entry fee of $25 includes light meal and prizes. Teams of 2 to 5 people. Contact is Matt Bixley, matt.bixley@agresearch.co.nz or phone 021 0469255 or 03 4763869.
     
     
And don't forget the Annavale Attack and Amble  and the Winter Classic (Wellington) both coming up in August.
