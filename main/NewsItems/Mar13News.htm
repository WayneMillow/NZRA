Events coming up

R&R Sport Wicked Rogaine Autumn Series, Port Hills, Christchurch
          This mid-week Autumn series of three events will be run on successive Wednesdays beginning 3rd April. Critical localised areas of the Port Hills are still not available due to the earthquakes so the rogaines will be limited to 2hr events again this year. Registration and maps are expected to be available from 5pm with a mass start at 6pm. Consult the Wicked Facebook page for further information including the location of each event.

Welly Afterwork Series, April to November
          A series of 3hr afterwork Rogaines at monthly intervals in the Wellington region under the auspices of O Hutt Valley. The first event is on 17th April. See the Maptalk calendar, http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine, for details.

Highland Events Autumn Rogaine, Lindis Valley, Central Otago, Sat 11th May, 12 & 6hrs
        The Highland Events 12hr and 6hr Autumn Rogaines are happening again this year. Ed Stevens and Terry Davis have found some amazing country in the Lindis Valley. The terrain is well tracked typical Central Otago country with mainly open native grasses and some scrubby gullies. Altitude range is between 400m and 800m. Further information will be on the Highland Events website shortly.

T'Walk (24hr Walk), Canterbury, Sat/Sun 18th/19th May
         It is on yet again!! This will be the 47th running of this annual iconic event, organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club, the pioneer of long distance foot navigation events in NZ. T’Walk differs from a Rogaine in that teams navigate their way around pre-determined routes. T’Walk is a must-do if you're at all into orienteering/rogaining, getting lost in the backcountry, reading maps, wearing silly costumes, adventure racing, matagouri, food, hanging out in woolsheds, failing at reading maps... and all things in between! The emphasis is on having fun. See the T'Walk website, http://www.cutc.org.nz/twalk/

Events just past

Kings Heist Rogaine, Marlborough, Saturday 23rd February
            42 people turned out for the Marlborough Orienteering Club’s rogaine organised by President Peter Hamill in the Waihopai Valley in late February. Read the article by Mondo Kopua on the rogaine in which he relates his experience in introducing his 6-yr old son, Angus, to rogaining. For results and map showing the winning route see http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/Results/KingsHeist2013Results.htm

Taranaki Secondary Schools Rogaine, Wednesday 6th March
              Once again the annual Taranaki Secondary Schools Rogaine broke all records with 737 students (up 30% on last year) competing at Lake Mangamahoe in three age categories and the three gender categories of girls, boys and mixed. The event ran from 6pm to 9pm giving the students dark zone experience and the chance to play with their torches. The top senior boys team was the only team to clear the course expertly set by Liam Paterson.

Are there any other regions interested in such an event? Nick Collins outlines his thoughts and gives hints on organising a Secondary Schools Rogaine in a report at http://rogaine.org.nz/main/NewsItems/TSSSA_2013.pdf. The report includes links to pictures, map, and results.

NZRA sponsors this event by providing the Navlight scoring system.

Congratulations to all those who participated and particularly to the winning Seagate team of Chris Forne, Nathan Fa’avae, Sophie Hart and Trevor Voyce. Annual General Meeting and Presidents Report
        
The minutes of the 2013 AGM, which include the President’s Annual Report, are available at http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/NewsItems/AGM2013_minutes.pdf

The President’s report highlighted
  • the importance of the annual Heights of Winter Rogaine as the premier event on the calendar,
  • the challenges we face in NZ in delivering a 24 hour championship event annually through limited organisational resources,
  • while short events (e.g. 3 to 6 hour ) are flourishing in many regions, it continues to be challenging to muster resources for 12-24 hour durations,
  • the need to attract younger people to leadership and organisational roles.

Of interest in the minutes is the discussion on the provision of the NZ Champs in general and the 2013 event in particular. Lines of enquiry are still being followed but it is increasingly likely that there will not be a Championship in 2013.

The list of NZRA Office Bearers is available at http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/Contacts.htm. Of note is that Alan Holdaway asked to step down from his role as one of our representatives on the International Rogaining Federation (IRF) and has been replaced by Emma de Lacey. We thank Alan for his great work on our behalf while he has held this position since 2008. Most recently he has been active in the technical working group.

If you have any contribution to, or comments on, anything in the minutes (or anything else for that matter) please contact the Secretary, Grant Hunter at g.hunter@clear.net.nz or President, Peter Squires at petersquires@ihug.co.nz.


Championship News

1st Australasian Championships, North Queensland, Sat-Sun 22-23 June 2013

Rogaining North Queensland will host the 1st Australasian Rogaine Championships, Sat-Sun 22nd & 23rd June 2013, on the Atherton Tableland, a 40 minute drive from Cairns International airport. On-line registration is now open on the event website http://adventuresportnq.info/arc2013 where further information is available.



11th World Rogaining Championships, Russia, 26th - 27th July 2013
             The 11th WRC will be based in Alol village in the Pustoshka district of the Pskov region, 500km west of Moscow and 400km south of St. Petersburg. The event website opened on 5th December and 602 people have registered to date. The ceiling on numbers is 1200 people so there are many places still available. If you need an excuse to visit Russia then this WRC could be it.

Vivienne Prince continues to be the only New Zealand rogainer registered.
