Mainly Tramping Wanaka Rogaine - Saturday, 14th November
========================================================
    There will be 6hr and 12hr options at the Snow Farm on the Pisa Range, accessed from the
Cardrona Valley. Entries close on Monday 9th November, ie 5 days before the event, although
late entries will be taken (for a fee) up to 30mins before the start. See the event website
http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/ for details and online entry (or entry form).


9th World Championships 2010 - Entries pouring in
==================================================
     Entries for the 9th WRC are pouring in fast. As of 4th November (4 days after entries
opened) we have received 400. There is an upper limit of 550 people so if you wish to enter
REGISTER NOW.

There are entry criteria defined by the International Rogaining Federation (IRF) that will
guarantee entry for some teams who have a history of good performance in 24hr Rogaines. For
others the order of application will be used in determining priority. Consult the website,
http://wrc2010.org.nz/registration.htm for an explanation of the registration process.

Most importantly, to be considered you must get your entry in. There are bound to be
withdrawals so don't be put off by the entry cap.

The entry fee for the World Champs is $NZ180. NZRA is encouraging Juniors to enter by
offering a $NZ90 subsidy. (Juniors will need to pay the $180 up front and then claim the
subsidy from the NZRA Treasurer).


Groundeffect CyclicSaga, Weekend 28th - 29th November, Awatere Valley
======================================================================
      The 2 day Cyclic Saga has been rescheduled to before Christmas and will be located in
the Awatere Valley. This popular MTB Rogaine will have a shorter option, "The Son of Saga",
each day. For complete information see the Saga website, http://www.cyclicsaga.co.nz/.


2010 New Zealand Championships, Wellington, 17-18 April 2010
============================================================
         The "Moonshine24" 2010 New Zealand Championships, are to be hosted by Orienteering
Hutt Valley in the Akatarawas, near Wellington. Chief organiser is Mike Sheridan so a
challenging event is assured. The event website is now open with information and pictures,
http://www.moonshine24.co.nz/.


2010 Australian Championships, Snowy Mountains, 27-28 March 2010
================================================================
         The 2010 Australian rogaining championships will be held in the magnificent Snowy
Mountains and hosted by the ACT Rogaining Association. The course will be set by national
rogaining champions, David Baldwin and Julie Quinn, and will provide excellent navigational
challenges in a beautiful environment.

Terrain will vary from alpine ash forests, through snow gum woodlands and granite
boulderfields, to open plains. Autumn in Kosciuszko typically provides superb clear days
with cool evenings. The Hash House will offer ideal camping and there is caravan park and
lodge accommodation within 5 minutes drive.
