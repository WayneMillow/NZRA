Silver Peaks Rogaine 2008, Saturday 18th October
================================================
     The Silver Peaks Rogaine 2008 will be held on Saturday 18th October (postponed from
27th September) in the Silver Peaks area about 25 km northwest of Dunedin. There will be
12 hour and 6 hour options for teams of two to five members.

     Much of the area is pine plantation or native bush with some areas of beech forest.
There are numerous, well-defined, tramping tracks as well as forest roads through the
area, which provide good access throughout. In the southeast and west/northwest areas
there is open tussock country with some regenerating native scrub - dracophyllum, hebe and
manuka.  In the latter region there are several, prominent rock-outcrops and cliffs and
some notable caves. This is steep ridge and spur country with well-defined valley systems.
The altitude range is from 150 to 780 m. Careful planning and route-choice will help to
minimise the climbing and ensure an enjoyable day out!

For more information and an entry form consult the NZRA website http://www.rogaine.org.nz



MACPAC Control 90 Multi-sport Rogaine, Saturday 18th October
============================================================
Three rogaines - kayak, bike, and hike - each of 4hrs make up this 12hr event with a
compulsory 2hr stop between disciplines. The event is for 2-person teams. There will be 1
map per person and the Navlight electronic punch system will be used to record your
progress. Promoter is Nathan Fa'avae. See the website http://www.control90.co.nz for more
information.



Wairewa Office Hours, Sunday 30th November
==========================================
Subject to land access permissions the annual ‘Office Hours’ will be held near Little
River on Banks Peninsula. We’ll be collaborating once again with the Wairewa Runanga on a
Maori themed event similar to the rogaine held over Maori language week, July 2006. Mark
this event in your calendar. We will confirm in early October.



Helping North Canterbury farmers clean up after flood damage
=============================================================
Several rogainers are keen to help with clean-up on farms affected by recent floods, which
is a nice response to the support they give us to hold events on farms. Any Canterbury
rogainers willing to help for a day or two (weekdays or weekends), please call Grant on
g.hunter@clear.net.nz or 351-6369. It's a matter of filling a car and going to farms as
directed by the coordinator at Hurunui DC.



World Rogaining Championships 2008
==================================
The World Champs will be run 13th/14th September in Estonia. Kiwis Chris Forne and Denis
de Monchy will be defending their title won in the Warrumbungles, NSW, Australia in 2006.
A total of 30 Kiwis will be competing. Look for the results on the event website
http://8wrc2008.rogain.ee/ shortly after the event.
