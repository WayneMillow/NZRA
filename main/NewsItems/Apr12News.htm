Events coming up

Wellington Afterwork Series, April to November
         A series of 3hr afterwork Rogaines at monthly intervals from April to November in the Wellington area and under the auspices of O Hutt Valley. The next event is on 7th May. See the Maptalk calendar, http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine, for details.

Auckland Bivouac Rogaine Series, each Sunday of May
         Organised by North West Orienteering, Auckland Orienteering, and Counties-Manukau Orienteering Clubs, this series of 3 hour and 90 minute Rogaines will be held at a different location each Sunday in May with the first event on the 6th May. Consult the website, http://www.rogaineseries.co.nz/ for complete information and entry.

Taylors Mistake Autumn Rogaine Afterwork Series, Port Hills
          Cancelled due to ongoing earthquake risk and consequential limited land access.

T'Walk (24hr Walk), Canterbury, Sat/Sun 5/6 May
          This will be the 46th running of this iconic event, organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club, the pioneer of long distance foot navigation events in NZ. It differs from a Rogaine in that teams navigate their way around up to 5 pre-determined routes returning to the HH at the end of each loop for sustenance (the students are great cooks) and rest. Emphasis is on fun - Twalk is low-key and as such is a magical introduction to 24hr Rogaining. The event is fully explained on the T'Walk website, http://www.cutc.org.nz/twalk/

Navquest Series, Sunday 6th May through Sunday 24th June
          The NavQuest is a series of four 3hr rogaines aimed at secondary school students, their families and teachers. Members of the public are more than welcome to join in by entering in the open category. Go to http://www.opc.org.nz for more information and to register a team online. The NavQuest  is organised as a partnership between The Sir Edmund Hillary Outdoor Pursuits Centre of New Zealand (OPC) and NI orienteering clubs in Rotorua, Auckland, Wellington and Taupo. Coordinating organiser is Darren Ashmore.

Highland Events Autumn Rogaine, Lowburn Hall, Cromwell, Saturday 12th May
           Ed Stevens and Terry Davis are again organising one of the few regular 12hr Rogaines in NZ and as usual it is in historical Central Otago. There will be a 6hr option as well. So get your team together for what will be a great day in the Otago tussocks (and rocks). After Ed and Terry’s last Rogaine president Peter Squires wrote:
“The Highland Events 12hr Rogaine on 17th September was a classic. For anyone who has memories of “hidden checkpoints” or “cryptic clues” or “matagouri bash”, forget it. This event was as good as it gets”. For more of what Pete had to say see http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/Results/EarnscleughRogaine.htm.
See the Highland Events website, http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/rogaine  for complete event information, some pictures, and online entry.

Heights of Winter, Canterbury, Saturday 30th June
          Prepare yourselves for some mid-winter fun. Information on the annual Heights of Winter in North Canterbury is now available on the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz. The date coincides with a full moon. There are 12hr and 6hr options. The terrain is varied ranging from gentle hills to steep and rugged hill-country with an elevation range of 500m. Vegetation is a mix of clean open grassland, scattered scrub, open native forest, and some plantations. An enthusiastic organising team is out planning the course in North Canterbury as we speak. On-line entries will commence in mid-May.

Recent Events

Taranaki Secondary Schools Rogaine ...  despite very wet conditions 571 students enjoyed the annual Taranaki Secondary Schools Rogaine on Wednesday 21st March. There are results and photos of the many happy people at http://bubbles.school.nz/Nick

Annavale Equestrian Rogaine
            NZ’s first equestrian Rogaine on Sunday 1st April (an auspicious date) attracted a field of 96 riders and 96 horses. Carol Fowler, as a horse rider herself, came up with the idea and co-opted Stuart Payne to assist. By all accounts it was a very successful event enjoyed by all, including the horses we hope, in beautifully fine weather. For results and organisers’ comments see http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/Results/EquestrianRogaineResults.pdf


Bits and Pieces
1.    Tim Farrant, well-known NZRA Committee member, event organiser and rogainer extraordinaire, has been selected as NZOF Rogainer of the Year for 2011. Congratulations Tim, well deserved.

2.    Many well-known Rogainers featured in the Godzone Adventure Race held in the Southern Lakes region of the South Island from 7th to 14th April with first place going to the team of Nathan Fa’ave, Sophie Hart, Chris Forne and Trevor Voyce. See the website http://www.godzoneadventure.com/the-adventure/ where you can retrospectively follow the route, and the woes and tribulations, of each team using the slider at the bottom of the live tracking page.

3.    There are 16 Kiwis entered in the 2012 Australian Championships to be held 5th and 6th May in the ruggedly beautiful Flinders Range on the edge of the South Australian desert. We wish them every success for the event as they navigate their way through steep sided gorges, sheer drop offs and old mines, stands of native pine, open mallee scrub, the odd gum tree and ever-present rocks. Website: http://rogaine.asn.au/arc2012.

We hope to see you all at a Rogaine soon.
