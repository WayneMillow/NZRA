Dear Rogainer,


The next two events on the Calendar are:-

Korowai Office Hours, Canterbury high country, Sunday 2nd December
===================================================================
Come and join the New Zealand Rogaining Association for a pre-Christmas rogaine on the
fringe of the Canterbury high country. You’ll get to visit your Korowai-Torlesse
Conservation Park. This is a seven-hour event, just like a short day at the office. There
are no age or gender grades – all teams are created equal. If you can’t get a leave pass so
close to Christmas, make up a family team and bring them all along. Further details and
entry form are available on the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz . Entries close 23rd
November.

Marlborough Christmas Rogaine, near Picton, Sunday 2nd December
===============================================================
Organised by the Marlborough Orienteering Club there will be 2 and 4 hr options followed by
a meal. For further information contact Janette Holdaway at holdaway@xtra.co.nz .
