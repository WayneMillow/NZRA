Events in the near future

R&R Sport Taylors Mistake Series, Christchurch, starts Wednesday 9th November
           Nora Audra and Ian Edmond are organising the popular R&R Sport Taylors Mistake Rogaine Series again. The three 3hr afterwork events are on consecutive Wednesdays beginning 9th November. There is also a 1hr family rogaine for the youngsters. For more information and an entry form see http://www.madeventz.co.nz/tmrogaine.


The Great Forest Rogaine, Rotorua, Saturday 12th November
         Three and six hour foot and MTB Rogaines utilising Rotorua's wonderful and right up-to-date Whakarewarewa Forest Map. See the Orienteering Bay of Plenty website, http://www.obop.org.nz/events.html, for details and entry. Entries close 5th November.


Office Hours, North Canterbury, Sunday 4th December
        This 7hr rogaine in the Clarence and Waiau basins and ranges beyond Hanmer Springs will suit newcomers and trampers as well as regular rogainers. The principal organiser is Grant Hunter assisted by two newcomers to rogaine event organisation, Chrys Horn and Linda Lilburne. See the information on the NZRA website http://www.rogaine.org.nz for more details and online entry. Entries close 23rd November.


Further events in the calendar

Afterwork Rogaines in November
         There are a raft of afterwork rogaines of various durations in Hamilton, New Plymouth and Wellington during November. Consult the Maptalk calendar, http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine, for details.


Christmas Twilight Rogaines
         A Christmas Twilight is scheduled for 21st December in Wellington (http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine) and another is planned for Christchurch although we don’t have a date yet. Keep your eye on the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz for an announcement.

 
MTBO Carnival, Christchurch and Central Otago, 7th – 14th January
         This MTBO carnival organised by PAPO, and incorporating the 2012 Australia - New Zealand MTBO Challenge, is to be held in historic Central Otago (4 events) with 2 warm-up events in Christchurch. All participants welcome - you don't have to be part of the Challenge to partake. See the official website (http://otagomtbo2012.co.nz/) for details. Entries close on Friday 16th December.


Waitangi 21, Wellington region, 4th – 6th February
        This is a 21-hour multi-stage rogaine spread over Waitangi weekend in the Wellington area as a celebration of 21 years of Rogaining in New Zealand. See an event flyer (40KB PDF) and the event website http://waitangi21.ohv.org.nz/.

Registration for 2012 World Champs expected to open 1st December
          The entry criteria and registration process for the 10th World Rogaining Championships in Czech Republic, August 2012, are currently under vote within the IRF and should be announced about 12th November. We will publish the criteria document on the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz, as soon as it is available. Any change to the process indicated below will also be notified via the website.

It is expected that registration will open 1st December on the event website http://www.rogaining.cz/wrc2012/. Entries are limited to 1000 people. Some people will pre-qualify for entry by reason of performance in past WRC and regional championships and to exercise this right of entry their registration must be in by 14th December. (The qualifying events will be specified in the mid-November announcement).

There should be a significant pool of places available for non-prequalifying teams which may be subjected to a ballot. To be considered in the ballot non-prequalifying people must register by this same date (14th December). Teams registering after this will have a diminished chance of gaining entry.

Each IRF member country is allocated 6 wildcard teams for people who record a significant performance not accounted for in the criteria, for people who are playing a major role in the development of the sport and/or the organisation of rogaines, or for people who miss out on a place via the routes above. Subject to confirmation, the New Zealand wildcard selection panel and selection process will be announced by mid-February 2012 and the last date for wildcard nominations is likely to be 1st March. Note that to be eligible for a wildcard, teams must register for WRC2012 in the normal way as above.

The principal message to note is that the first phase of the entry process is expected to follow a tight timetable. People registering after 14th December, whether they prequalify or not, will have a diminished chance of gaining entry.

Please pass this message on to all your Rogaining mates and contacts who may be interested in WRC2012 and keep your eye on the NZRA website for the mid-November announcement.
