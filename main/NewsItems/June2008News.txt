       Heights of Winter 2008 (Canterbury) – Saturday 14th June
       ========================================================

Entries close next week, Wednesday 4th June, so get yours in without delay.

This is the usual 12 hour event, with a 6hr option too, to be held in North Canterbury
on terrain of rolling land and hills (up to about 400 m) with mostly clean open
grassland, a little scattered scrub, and some native bush in the gullies. A very
comfortable Hash House has been arranged.

See the NZRA website http://www.rogaine.org.nz for details and an entry form. Online
entry is available there.



        Lactic Turkey Piha Rogaine (Auckland) – Saturday, 7th June
        ==========================================================

Entries close next week, Thursday 5th June.

There are 6hr and 3hr options in this popular annual event. See the event website
http://www.lacticturkey.co.nz/ for more information and an entry form.



        2008 New Zealand Championships
        ==============================

The 2008 NZ Rogaine Championship, co-organised by the Taupo and Rotorua Orienteering
clubs, was run in Taupo last weekend on a picture-perfect course. The championship was
won by a team of top adventure racers - Brent Edwards, Stu Lynch, and Rhys Burns. They
cleaned the course in under 13hrs and were over three and a half hours ahead of the
second placed team of Dennis de Monchy and Robert Jarvis. Third were the Forne
brothers, Chris and John. Eight teams completed the whole course.

In the 12 hour event, run as two 6 hour sections with a 12 hour break in between, the
father and son team of Wayne and Matthew Smith overcame blistered feet to hold their
half time lead over Darren Ashmore and Rebecca Smith. Juniors Duncan Morrison and Rory
Hart were third.

Full results are available on the NZRA website http://www.rogaine.org.nz .
