              R&R Sport Taylors Mistake Rogaine Series (Christchurch)
              =======================================================
The first in this 5 event series is only one week away so get your entries in. An entry
form and information is available on the event website at
http://www.madeventz.co.nz/tmrogaine .

The 3rd event on the 19th March, at a mystery location, will be organised by Georgia
Whitla and Tim Farrant to raise funds for their tilt at the Junior title at the World
Champs in Estonia. So turn up and give them your support.

New Zealand Championships
=========================

The 2008 New Zealand Championships, weekend 24th/25th May, will be hosted jointly by
Rotorua and Taupo Orienteering clubs on the North Island Central Plateau with Mark
Copeland as controller and Lance Eccles as planner. The event site is near Taupo. Entries
are now open. There is a non-championship 12hr "Game of Two Halves" (2 x 6hr) event as
well.

Newsletters #1 and #2, event information, and an entry form can be found at
http://www.rogaine.org.nz .


Eastbourne results
==================

Organised by HVOC, the results for this 12 or 6 out of 18 hour rogaine are available at
http://www.mapsport.co.nz/hvoc/ebrog.html . First place went to the father/daughter
combination of Ross and Hazel Bidmead.
