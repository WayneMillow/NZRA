
New Zealand Rogaining Association (Inc.)
21st July 2009


Dear Rogainer,

Two rogaines in August -

  Winter Classic 3hr Challenge, Sunday 2nd August, Wellington region
  ==================================================================
       The Winter Classic, and Wayne Cretney Memorial, is normally solely an
orienteering event but this year the 23rd Classic includes a 3hr Challenge Rogaine.
Bryan Teahan is the organiser. See the Classic website,
http://www.winterclassic.co.nz/ , for details. Entries close 24th July so get yours in
pronto.


  Annavale Amble and Attack, 3hr and 4hr, Sunday 16th August, Canterbury
  ======================================================================
        This navigational adventure in the Canterbury foothills is being organised by
Stuart Payne and Carol Fowler as a fundraiser for Darfield HS Rural College. The
Attack is for the competitive and is 4hrs while the Amble is 3hrs and intended to be
more recreational. Information and an entry form is available on the Darfield High
School website, http://www.darfield.school.nz/rural-college . Entries close 10th
August.
			
			
Coming up in September and October are:

(1) The Katharine Eustace Fundraising Rogaine, Wanaka, Saturday 26th September, 6 &
3hr options. See the Highland Events website, http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/ .

(2) Control 90 Multi-sport Rogaine, Tekapo, Saturday 10th October, 12hrs. The Control
90 consists of three rogaines - kayak, bike, and hike - each of 4hrs duration. For
details see the website, http://www.control90.co.nz/ .


  2010 New Zealand Championships announced, 17-18 April 2010
  ==========================================================
         The 2010 New Zealand Championships are to be hosted by Orienteering Hutt
Valley in the Akatarawas, near Wellington. Chief organiser is Mike Sheridan so a
challenging but fair event is assured. This will probably be the only 24hr rogaine in
New Zealand leading up to the World Championships to be held in Canterbury in November
2010 so mark it in your calendar as a "must attend" event. Further information will be
available when the event website opens.
