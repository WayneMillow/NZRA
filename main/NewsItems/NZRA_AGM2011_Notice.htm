Notice of
Annual General Meeting

21st February 2011

The NZRA AGM will be held Monday 21st February, 7.30 pm at 6 Harrods Court, Christchurch.

Agenda will include:
  1. minutes from AGM 2010 (available below) and matters arising
  2. financial report
  3. election of officers
  4. transitioning to a more truly national organisation (recap of findings at committee meeting held 7 February)
  5. other general business

For those living outside of Christchurch and having a compelling interest in attending this meeting, NZRA will consider helping with travel costs. Please raise this with the Secretary, g.hunter@clear.net.nz.

If anyone has items to add to the agenda please send them to the secretary, Grant Hunter, at g.hunter@clear.net.nz
