Never Look Back
Eastern Otago, Saturday 14th March
Bob Cunninghame and team have been working hard to provide an unforgettable Rogaine on terrain adjacent to that used for the Golden Road rogaine in July last year. But where are all the entrants? To date only 28 have people signed up. Entries close on Saturday so get yours in now. (Late entries may be accepted at the organiser’s discretion). Event details and online entry are available on the NZRA website.

The terrain, inland from Palmerston and Waikouaiti, is relatively open farmland and tussock with schist and basalt outcrops, patchy scrub, plus some forest. There will be 13, 6 and 3hr courses, with the latter catering particularly for families and novices.

Message from Bob:
“With regard to the number of entrants we shall run the event come what may. Note there is a total fire ban over the whole area but the farmers are happy to have us. It is a bit greener now, but still very dry underfoot and streams are very low. Camping is available at the event headquarters on Saturday night. No gas/petrol etc stoves permitted but there will be hot water for drinks available in the woolshed on Sunday morning and the toilets will still be there”.


More events in March and April
NavQuest, various North Island locations, 15th March thru 7th June
        The NavQuest is a collection of four 3hr rogaines in Taupo, Auckland, Wellington, and Rotorua. It is aimed at secondary school students, their families and teachers but members of the public are more than welcome to join in by entering in the open category. Go to www.hillaryoutdoors.co.nz/navquest/ for more information and to register a team online. The first event is on Sunday 15th March. Organiser is Darren Ashmore. 
Wicked Autumn Series, Christchurch, from 18th March
          The Wicked series of 2 hr afterwork Rogaines, organised by Nora Audra and Ian Edmond, is back with three events on the Port Hills, Christchurch. The first event is on Wednesday 18th March. Keep your eye on the Wicked Facebook for further information.
Wellington Afterwork Series from 22nd April
        This popular annual 3hr afterwork series at various locations in the Wellington region will run monthly 22nd April to 22nd September. See the full list of Welly Rogaines.
Taranaki Sec School Champs, Wednesday 18th March, 3hrs
      The Taranaki Secondary School Champs takes place on Wednesday 18th March. Up to 1000 students are expected to take part in this 3hr event organised by Annie Sanderson and Nick Collins, amongst others. NZRA supports this event by providing the Navlight scoring system. We’ll report on the event in the next newsletter.

For more Rogaines in the months ahead, particularly the significant events in May, refer to either
  • the NZRA event calendar at http://www.rogaine.org.nz/ or
  • the OrienteeringNZ calendar.

Tarawera Ultramarathon Warmup
         Read the nice story about Rogaining in one of the World’s biggest running magazines – Trail Runner! This 1hr event was organised as an ice-breaker prior to the Tarawera Ultramarathon in February.


Championship Events
13th World Rogaining Championships, 22nd - 23rd August 2015
          There are now 700 entrants in WRC2015 to be held in Lapland. Entries are limited to 1000 people and 700 have entered so far, of which 9 are Kiwis, so get your entry in if you are planning on competing.

Bulletins 1&2 and course setter notes, including advice on weather, terrain, and map, are available on the WRC2015 website. Keep up to date via the event Facebook and Twitter.
3rd Australasian Championships, 10th – 11th October 2015
       The 2015 Australasian championships are to be held in the beautiful Capertee National Park about 3½ hrs west of Sydney. This wonderful rogaining area offers open bushland, scenic creeks, river flats, magical vistas, rolling hills and one or two steeper hills. See the website for up-to-date information.
15th New Zealand Championships, 31st October 2015
              Subject to confirmation the 2015 New Zealand Championships will be in Marlborough on the weekend of 31st October. There will be shorter non-championship events in addition to the 24hr championship. Chief organiser is the experienced rogainer Mondo Kopua. There will be more information on the NZRA website in due course.

That is it for now. Keep your eye on the event calendars and we hope to see you at an event soon.
