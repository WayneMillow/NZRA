Annual General Meeting, 27th February, Christchurch
        The NZRA AGM will be held Monday 27th February, 7.30 pm at 6 Harrods Court, Christchurch. The Agenda will include:
  1. Minutes of the 2011 AGM and matters arising
  2. President’s Annual Report
  3. Financial report
  4. Election of officers
  5. Other business
If anyone has items to add to the agenda please send them to the secretary, Grant Hunter, g.hunter@clear.net.nz. The final agenda will be available from the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz, a week before the AGM.

For those living outside of Christchurch and having a compelling interest in attending this meeting, NZRA will consider helping with travel costs. Alternatively we may be able to arrange an electronic link. Please raise this with the Secretary, g.hunter@clear.net.nz.

The minutes of the 2011 AGM are available at http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/NewsItems/AGM2011_minutes.pdf

2012 New Zealand Championships moved to late Spring
          We draw your attention that, in a break from tradition, the 2012 New Zealand Championships has been moved from Autumn to late Spring. It will be held in Otago within 100km of Dunedin on the weekend of 24th - 25th November. Organiser-in-chief is Bob Cunninghame. Details will be available on the NZRA website later in the year.


2012 Australian Championships, South Australia, 5th and 6th May
          Entries are now open. Kiwis have a proud history of good performances in the Ozzie Champs so we hope to see many of you there. Website: http://rogaine.asn.au/arc2012.

These Championships will be a qualifying event for the 11th World Championships in Russia, 27 – 28 July 2013. (Note that future World Champs are to be run annually). The entry criteria for Russia are still being determined but they are expected to be similar to those for the 10th WRC in Czech Republic later this year (http://www.rogaining.cz/wrc2012/files/entry_criteria.pdf).

Waitangi 21, Wellington region, 4th – 6th February has been run
          Nine teams completed all 5 sections of this 21-hour multi-stage rogaine spread over Waitangi holiday weekend in the Wellington area. The event was organised by Michael Wood as a celebration of 21 years of Rogaining in New Zealand. The stages in the rogaine for a total of 21hrs were
  • Saturday morning – 1hr urban rogaine in downtown Wellington
  • Saturday afternoon –  3hr rogaine on Wellington western skyline
  • Overnight Saturday/Sunday – 7hr rogaine (8pm to 3am) in East Harbour Regional Park
  • Sunday afternoon – 6hr in Battle Hill Regional Park (MTB option as well)
  • Monday morning – 4hr in Belmont Regional Park where the original rogaine was run. (MTB option as well)
The team of Erin Roberts (Rotorua) and Pete Swanson (Auckland) was the overall winner, with the womens team of Debbie Mansfield and Julie Gordon second. Third place went to Liam Drew and Barryn Westfield who were in the lead until the final event where they returned late.

Many other teams completed various sections of the event. For full results see the event website http://waitangi21.ohv.org.nz/.
