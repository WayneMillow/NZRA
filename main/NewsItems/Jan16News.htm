Stoneyard Staggers
24hr (NZ Champs) and 7hr Rogaines
Eastern Otago, 27th – 28th February
           The 15th New Zealand Championships will be run in Otago north of Dunedin and inland from Waikouaiti the last weekend of February. This Rogaine will be suitable for everyone, not just experienced and elite rogainers. There will be a 7 hour non-championship event or you can test yourself over the full 24 hours championships. Remember you do not need to stay out for all of the 24 hours - you may make multiple trips back to the "hash house" where hot food, socialising, rest, and perhaps a bed, will await you.
 
This championship will be a great opportunity for the 68 NZ Rogainers heading to Alice Springs in July for the World Championships to trim up their technique. Entries are now open. See the event website for all the details and on-line entry.


Other events coming up
Christchurch Rogaine Series, Port Hills, Wednesdays 27th Jan, 3rd & 10th Feb
           Team Next Generation are hosting a new series of three after-work rogaine adventures this summer, commencing Wednesday 27th January. Each event will be of two hours duration, from 6:30 - 8:30pm. For more detail see the Canterbury Rogaine website.


Akatarawa Attack MTB/Foot Rogaine, Sunday 31st January, 4 & 8 hrs
          This is a traditionally rugged MTB rogaine, in the wilds of the Akatarawas between Upper Hutt and Kapiti. Under new rules you can use your bike for all, some or none of the controls, which makes for more route choice, and also more teams. The start is at Whareroa Farm near Paekakariki, with controls between Orange Hut and the Kapiti Coast. For details see http://akattack.org.nz/. Entries are due by 21 January.

 
MTB Rogaine Series, Rotorua, February/March, 90 minutes
            The annual series of 90 minute MTB rogaines, organised by Orienteering Bay of Plenty, will be run on Sundays 14th & 28th February and 20th March, with an additional novelty event on Thursday 18th February as part of the Rotorua Bike Festival. These are fun, family friendly events. All the information you need is on the Orienteering Bay of Plenty website. Entries close 11th February for the series and 15th February for the novelty event.


Kaikoura Inland Rogaine, Saturday 19th March, 3 & 6 hrs
           Kaikoura Suburban School, local farmers, and the NZ Rogaining Association are collaborating on an event to be held on the 'Inland Road' between Mt. Lyford and Kaikoura, North Canterbury - a 2.5 hour drive from Christchurch and 2hrs from Blenheim. There will be 3 and 6 hour options. Expect stunning landscapes and an excellent mix of terrain for rogaining. Overnight camping will be available on-site. Details will be on the NZRA website soon.

WRC2016 positions still available
There are still positions available in the 14th World Rogaining Championships (WRC2016) to be run near Alice Springs. Join the 68 Kiwis already entered in what will be a challenging Rogaine in very interesting terrain: semi-arid, rocky and technical, with complex gully systems. All the entry conditions have been relaxed so now is your chance to partake in a world champs close to home. Consult the event website for full information.

Agenda items and nominations for the positions of President, Treasurer, Secretary, and committee members should be lodged with the Secretary (g.hunter@clear.net.nz) at least five days before the meeting.
