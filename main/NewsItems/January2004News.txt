There will be the usual range of events in 2004. Many are in the planning
stage and will be announced in due course. See the Event Calendar at

added as details come to hand. The following Rogaines are of immediate

Green Coast New Zealand Rogaine Champs 2004
======================================
March 6 & 7th, Wairamarama, Auckland South.

      These Champs promise to be a special event in the superb rolling
farmland and bush terrain of the Wairamarama/Limestone Downs area. The
Championship Rogaine will be for the standard 24 Hour duration (1.00pm
Saturday to 1.00pm Sunday). Detailed event information and an entry form
are available on the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz . Entries
close Monday 1st March.

     There are not many 24hr events available so this is an opportunity for
you to indulge. However, for those who prefer shorter events there are
non-Championship options - a 16 Hour option (9.00pm Saturday to 1.00pm
Sunday) and a 6 Hour option (7.00am to 1.00pm Sunday). The unique 16 Hour
event will allow participants to experience the thrill of night-time
rogaining without the exertion of a full 24 Hour event.


Naseby Rogaine – Saturday 13th March, 6 and 12 hrs
==========================================
       This event at Naseby in historic Central Otago is to be run by YODA
(Youth Orienteers Dunedin Association) with Bob Cunninghame as the chief
organiser. Entries close 28th February. See the website, 
http://www.rogaine.org.nz for preliminary information. More detail will be
posted there as it comes to hand.


6th World Rogaining Championships
=============================
May 8 and 9th, Arizona, USA

While this is officially a World Championship it is open to everybody.
Don't be put off by the usual image of Arizona as excessively hot and dry.
The location for the event is the forests of east-central Arizona at a
cooling altitude of 2500m where water is abundant and the expected
temperature is in the range 12 to 18 degrees.

New Zealand has a history of participation in the World Champs - there were
Kiwis in 8 teams at the last champs in Czech Republic, 2002. Plan a
business trip or vacation around this event. For more information visit our
website http://www.rogaine.org.nz or the event website 
http://rogaine.tucsonorienteering.org/ .
