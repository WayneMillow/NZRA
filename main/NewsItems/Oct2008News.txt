Wanaka Mainly Tramping Rogaine
==============================
The Mainly Tramping Wanaka Rogaine will be held on 22 November 2008. There will be 12hr
and 6hr options along the same format as the June event. It is going to be held on the
Snow Farm, Cardrona Valley which will make for awesome navigation and a great venue.
Event detail and online entry can be found at the web page
http://www.wanakahomestead.co.nz/page_147.html or just google "Wanaka Rogaine". If you
would like to receive details on Wanaka events then email stay@wanakahomestead.co.nz to
be put on the email list.



North Canterbury farmers helped to clean up after flood damage
==============================================================
NZRA members contributed 8 person-days to help farmers clean up after the winter floods
on farms at Blythe Valley and Omihi, North Canterbury. The work, mainly clearing flood
debris off fences, was very much appreciated by the farmers and the district council
coordinator. It was pleasing to be able to put this effort back into a farming community
that has supported our events. Thanks to Grant Hunter for organising this.



World Rogaining Championships 2008
==================================
The Kiwi contingent performed notably at the World Champs, 13th/14th September in
Estonia, with New Zealand being the best performed nation outside of the "home" countries
of Estonia, Russia, and Latvia. Chris Forne and Denis de Monchy with 300 points were
narrowly beaten by 4 points into 2nd place by a Russian pairing. Gold medals were won by
Tim Farrant and Georgia Whitla in the mixed junior division (186 points), and by the
experienced trio of Pete Squires, Anne Kennedy, and Bill Kennedy in the mixed supervets
(166 points). Greg Barbour, competing with Australian Dave Rowlands, won silver in the
mens veteran section with 267 points. Other NZ performances of note were -

    Viv Prince (with Ozzie Richard Robinson), 6th mixed vets, 229 points
    Liam Drew and Rachel Ockelford, 28th mixed open, 192 points
    Julian Maclaren and Emily Wall, 30th mixed open, 190 points
    Dave Laurie and Bruce Meder, 5th mens supervets, 176 points

Note that the European scoring system allocates 1 point for our usual 10 points. Full
results are available on the event website http://8wrc2008.rogain.ee/ .
