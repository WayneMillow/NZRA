Dear Rogainer,

                   Silver Peaks - Saturday September 23rd, Dunedin
                 ===================================================

Entries for the Silver Peaks rogaine close in 5 days, Tuesday 12th September, so get your's
in now. This is a 12hr event with a 6hr option. Chief organiser is Bob Cunninghame.

Make a weekend of it by joining in the Dunedin Orienteering Club event near Henley on the
Sunday. There is a discount of $4 awaiting those who compete in both events. Enquire at the
Rogaine on the Saturday.

For the rogaine entry form and further details see the NZRA website,
http://www.rogaine.org.nz



CPIT Spring Mystery Madness, Sat 7th October, Port Hills, Christchurch.
======================================================================

This urban fringe rogaine, organised by Christchurch Polytech, ran for the first time last
year and was a great success. The course provides 12 hr, 6 hr, and 3 hr options. There is
also a special option for families who wish to get out and explore the Port Hills. The event
is based at the Cashmere Primary School.

This will be a question/answer rogaine and will incorporate a mystery element which involves
the participants in putting together a cryptic clue gleaned from a range of checkpoint answers in
order to find a hidden prize.

For details and entry form see the CPIT Recreation website at
http://www.cpit.ac.nz/subjects/recreation/news.asp

=====================================================
