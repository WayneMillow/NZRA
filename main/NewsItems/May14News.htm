Upcoming Events
              Never mind that winter is fast approaching (and may even be here), there are lots of rogaines up and down the country to warm you, make your winter more tolerable, and to take you through into spring. So get out there and rattle through the events that are provided for you. The more the merrier.

This month, in addition to the normal list of events, we highlight the expansion of secondary-schools rogaining beyond the long-standing Taranaki Schools and Navquest events. We also flag 24-hour championship events in New Zealand and Australia, and the World Championships in South Dakota.


T'Walk 2014, Canterbury, weekend 17th -18th May
           Entries are open for this unique and iconic 24hr event, organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club. Join the students for a weekend of exercise and fun. T'Walk is two days after the full moon so will be magic if the night is clear and just a little frosty. You don’t have to “twalk” for the whole 24hrs and if you're the type that requires sleep there is a heap of space for tents and there is a HUGE woolshed. Nourishing food will be provided semi-continuously. See the T'Walk website for all the details and get your entry in now.

Metlink City Safari, Wellington, Sunday 18th May
             In the City Safari teams will race by foot, train, bus, cable car or ferry to find checkpoints spread at unique and interesting spots throughout the Wellington region. The course has been designed to reward smart use of public transport which adds a whole new level of strategy. Live scoring will enable you to know how you are doing during the event (using your smartphone), and friends and family will be able to watch your progress from a big screen at the start finish line. There are 3hr and 6hr options. For more detail see www.citysafari.org.nz. Entries are open (late fee after 11th May).

Heights of Winter, North Canterbury, Saturday 14th June
           See the information sheet on the NZRA website for details of the annual Heights of Winter Rogaine. Entries open on 15th May and are limited to 220 people in each of the 12hr and 6hr events so get your entry in punctually. Direct any general enquiries to Grant Hunter at g.hunter@clear.net.nz. Grant would welcome any further offers of assistance ranging from field work to help on the day.

“Summer's on its Way”, Whakatane, Sunday 22nd June
           Urban and a little bit rural with 6 and 3 hour options, 10am and 1pm start times respectively, and maps available 1/2hr before the start. The start/finish is at Trident High School, Whakatane. The event is a fundraiser for a Year 10 class heading to OPC Great Barrier. The more the merrier; there will be some very nice scenery.... an active volcano, dormant volcanoes, expansive sandy beaches, an historic pa, several cafes and footpaths. Entry fee is $15. Contact is Alister Coulter.

The Golden Road Rogaine, Eastern Otago, Saturday 12th July
              There will be a 13-hour “Classic”, a 6-hour “Introduction” and a 3-hour “Family Special”. The terrain, west of Waikouaiti and a one-hour drive from Dunedin, is relatively open farmland and tussock with rocks and scrub in places, plus some forest blocks. Further details will be available on the NZRA website soon.
General Enquiries:  Bob Cunninghame, signalhill@xtra.co.nz, ph. (03) 473 8330.

Secondary School Rogaining

NZ Secondary School Rogaine Championships, Saturday 9th August
        The inaugural NZ Secondary School Rogaine Championships, officially sanctioned by the NZ Secondary School Sports Council and by Orienteering NZ, will be hosted by Orienteering Bay of Plenty (OBoP).  There will be Senior 4 hour and Junior 3 hour events in Mixed, Male and Female categories. More information and the entry form can be found at http://www.orienteering.org.nz/events/new-zealand-secondary-school-rogaine-champs/ .  If you know of a school wishing to partake the person to contact is Suzanne Scott at suebrook08@gmail.com or phone 021 233 9078.


School events leading up to the NZ Secondary School Championships
         If you are looking to prepare a school team for the New Zealand Championships there are school rogaines scheduled in Rotorua, Taupo, Wellington, Auckland, and Christchurch:
 
1.    Navquest Series, 25th May to 22nd June - Rotorua, Taupo, Wellington and Auckland
          Four 3hr rogaines aimed at secondary school students, their families and teachers. Members of the public are more than welcome to join in by entering in the open category. Go to http://www.opc.org.nz/navquest/ for more information and to register a team online. The NavQuest is organised as a partnership between the Sir Edmund Hillary Outdoor Pursuits Centre of New Zealand (OPC) and orienteering clubs in Rotorua, Taupo, Wellington and Auckland

2.    Canterbury Secondary Schools Rogaine, Christchurch Port Hills, Sunday 6th July
          The Peninsula and Plains Orienteers are organising a Canterbury Secondary School Rogaine on Sunday 6th July based at Victoria Park. Planner is Tim Farrant. There will be a three and four hour options following the same format as the NZ Secondary Schools Rogaine in Rotorua on 9th August. The event will be open to anyone, and be a fund raiser to assist the top senior and junior teams to attend the National championships. Contact is Martin Etherington, martin@etherington.co.nz  or ph. 027 433 1388


Championships coming up

12th World Rogaining Championships
Black Hills, South Dakota, 16th-17th August 2014
As noted in previous newsletters there are many places still available. The current total of 419 is well short of the 1000 limit. To date 32 Kiwis have registered their interest but we expect a few more once they get themselves sorted. If you are wondering what is in store for you take a look at the article by Bud Laird on Rogaining in S. Dakota. The event website and blog is at http://peakassurance.net/. See the list of entrants.

Australasian Championships (Goldfields Bonanza)
Victoria, weekend 11th-12th October 2014
           Set in an old goldfields area in the Castlemaine region, the terrain is a complex and challenging mixture of spurs and gullies only 1.5hrs drive from Melbourne. There will be a 6hr event as well as the championship 24hr Rogaine. The website is at http://vra.rogaine.asn.au/arc14/.

New Zealand Championships 2014
Nevis Valley, Otago, weekend 6th-7th December
          The 2014 New Zealand Champs will be based in the lower Nevis Valley with its unique landscapes and historic remnants of the gold mining era. This interesting valley is quite remote but is readily accessible in summer; Google “Nevis Valley” for a glimpse of what the area offers. Chief organisers are the experienced Highland Events team of Ed Stevens and Terry Davis who are well known for their twice yearly Wanaka Rogaines. Mark the date in your calendars and look for more information on the NZRA website further down the track.

Events just past
 
Mt Outdoors Autumn Rogaine, Lindis Valley, Saturday 26th April
The Highland Events autumn Rogaine was based at the Shirlmar Station woolshed and took in the Shirlmar, Morven Hills and Geordie Hills fine wool merino sheep stations. There were 12hr, 6hr and family 3hr events, and a 6hr MTB option in glorious Central Otago weather. Organiser Ed Stevens comments:

          “What a fantastic weekend with some great scores coming out of this event with Greig Hamilton and Seamus Meikle scoring 3910 out of a possible 4370; how good is that! But wait there is more: I had to give away slabs of Speights to three teams who got all five 100 point controls – the Lucas boys (George and Tom), Tim Farrant and Georgia Whitla, and of course Greig and Seamus.  I would never have thought it was possible.  WELL DONE.

We had great reports about the course. There were 75 controls ranging from 400m to 1100m above sea level with a good balance of flat and hills with heaps of choices, even for the elites. There were many comments in planning on how hard it was to pick the best route.  That is what our goal is in course setting.  The controls ranged from 750m to 1.2k apart.

We had an awesome dinner put on by the friends of the Tarras School and I think everybody went home with a prize.  The trophies we had made by Ocean Shell went to first place in the open mixed, open male and open female. I’m sure they will be treasured by the winners; they looked great.

 A couple of hard luck stories - Jo Williams and Adrian Camm had to pull out after 9 hrs with Adrian getting sick; nevertheless they were 3rd in the Mixed Open. In the 6hr event the Google Maps girls were having so much fun they lost track of time and were disqualified for coming home 40mins late”.

For full results see the Highland Events website.
