Entries close on Friday
The Charwell Challenge
3hr and 6hr Rogaines
Inland Road (North Canterbury), Saturday 19th March

Co-organiser Grant Hunter says that in many years of organising rogaines in Canterbury, 'Charwell' has the most outstanding landscape of all, with the Seaward Kaikouras just over the back. Overnight campers are very welcome and the farmyard setting is superb.

The course planners have worked hard on a navigational challenge worthy of this setting, on terrain of rolling land, hills and valleys, within an elevation range of 300m. Vegetation is a mix of open grassland, native scrub and some forest. See the information sheet for more detail. Enter now; entries close next Friday, 11th March. (Late entries at discretion of the organisers).

Recent Results

Stoneyard Staggers
24hr (NZ Champs) and 7hr Rogaines, Eastern Otago, 27th – 28th February
Congratulations to the 95 people in 39 teams who took up the challenge of the 24hr NZ Championships run on a challenging course in Eastern Otago inland from Palmerston and Waikouaiti. The team of Chris Forne and Greig Hamilton ran out the winners with a score of 3590 out of a possible 3810, travelling 115km and climbing over 6000m. Second on 3010 was Ryan Thomson and Tim Farrant, closely followed by Georgia Whitla and Lara Prince only 10 points further back. Take a look at the map showing the winning team’s route. (They went to check point 20 first).

 Full results and a report are available. The Glenomaru Valley Bush Bandits (Simon Cullen, Dan Cullen) took out the 7hr companion event with a score of 810.

An event such as this involves a lot of preparatory work. Special thanks to overall organiser and course setter/vetter Pete Squires and to Bob Cunninghame for landowner liaison, course planning and catering. Others involved in setting and vetting were Laurie Kennedy, Jim Cotter, Dave Laurie, and Andy Buchanan. And, of course, thanks to those who helped on the day.

The 2017 NZ Championships will be run the weekend of 11th – 12th February in Waikaia Valley, Southland. Since this event will also serve as the 5th Australasian Champs we expect to see a large contingent of Aussies competing. Pencil it in your diary. Come to enjoy the atmosphere – you don’t have to do the lot.
