Recent Events
Over 300 people in 61 teams turned out for the annual T’Walk on the weekend of 9th May. Results are up on the T’Walk website. Next year will see the 50th running of this iconic event, the pioneer of 24hr navigation events in New Zealand, started by Aussie Greg Martin while studying at the University of Canterbury.

The unique Urban Safari on the 17th May attracted 660 people who used various modes of transport, as well as some walking and running, to visit checkpoints distributed within the Wellington urban area. Results can be seen at www.citysafari.org.nz.

There were some high scores in the Autumn Muster run in the Geraldine area, South Canterbury, on Sunday 17th May. With 3hr and 6hr options and a range of categories there was an event for all tastes and ages, including family groups. See the results which feature some of the top names in Rogaining.

The first Tutira Rogaine in the Hawkes Bay region on Saturday 9th May was a little light on participants but was enjoyed by all those who took part. Options on offer were 3, 6, and 9hrs with the latter starting in the dark. The Rogaine was organised by well-known multi-sporter George Christison in support of the small Tutira primary school. Results are available on the website and take a look at the pictures on Facebook.

On Sunday 24th May the Marlborough Orienteering Club hosted a 3hr Rogaine in classic South Marlborough rolling farmland. Mostly the results show only team names so are meaningful only to “those in the know”; but nevertheless there are some recognisable names in the list.

The monthly Welly afterwork on Wednesday 13th May attracted 49 people who didn’t believe the bad weather forecast and who were rewarded by a pleasant outing in fine weather. The event was run in the Porirua region. See the Welly website for results.


Championship Events
The date for the 2015 New Zealand 24hr Championships has been confirmed as the weekend of 31st October/1st November in the Marlborough region. There will be a shorter non-championship event as well. Chief organiser is NZRA President Mondo Kopua. More information will be up on on the NZRA website before too long.

3rd Australasian Championships, 10th – 11th October 2015
        Entries are open for the 3rd Australasian Championships to be held in the Capertee National Park about 3½ hrs west of Sydney on the weekend 10th – 11th October. This is a wonderful rogaining area with mainly rolling hills, river flats and open bushland providing easy travel. See the website for full information. Entries are open.

WRC2016 pictures
           Course setting for the World Rogaining Championships in July 2016 in the MacDonnell Ranges east of Alice Springs has begun. Reports are that the terrain is amazing and the new map that was surveyed just for this event is fantastic. For a selection of photos taken during the course reconnoitre see https://www.facebook.com/DavidBaldwin900/posts/10153336767504490. If you are thinking of partaking be sure to read the advice on qualification from the organisers.
