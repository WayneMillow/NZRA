Dear Rogainer,

(1)  Results and route choices for the Multisport Bikes Piha 3 and 6 hr rogaine, which

(2)  Note the following upcoming events:

The Half-EPIC – 26th July, 6hrs
--------------------------------
         Bannockburn, Otago. Organised by Bruce McLeod at short notice. Entries CLOSE
tomorrow so get your's in today. Details and entry form are available on Bruce's website
-  http://www.epicnavigation.com

Multisport Bikes Cascades Rogaine – 9th August, 3 and 6 hrs
--------------------------------------------------------
        Waitakere Ranges, Auckland. Organised by Phil Wood and Shaun Collins. Their last
event on the 28th June was enjoyed immensely by all those who participated. The
organisers say "We've got a beauty this time! A dense network of tracks with a bit of
everything - waterfalls, tram-lines, huge kauri, tunnels and fast running tracks."
Entries close 4th August so don't delay, get them in. For details and entry form see the
Lactic Turkey website, http://www.lacticturkey.co.nz

The Rock and Pillar EPIC – 6th September, 12hrs
------------------------------------------------
         Middlemarch, Otago. Another Bruce McLeod special. Details and entry form are
available on Bruce's website - http://www.epicnavigation.com. Entries close 22nd August
and numbers are likely to be limited. Get your entry in now.

NO Queenstown EPIC this year
----------------------------
         For those who have been wondering, there will not be a Queenstown EPIC this
year. Organiser, Bruce McLeod, tells us that there is overload in the district with the
Southern Traverse and the South Island Orienteering champs down that way. The next EPIC
planned after Sept will be a pedal epic in February.

Wellington and Hutt Valley
--------------------------
        For people in the lower North Island, note that a series of 3 hour shoestrings is
planned by the Hutt Valley Orienteering Club for October-November. Also it is hoped that
the postponed Colonial can take place in the November/December timeframe. Keep your eye
on the NZOF website http://www.mapsport.co.nz/rog/rogevents.html and/or the NZRA website
for details.
