Events coming up

Highland Events Wanaka Rogaine, Lindis Valley, Central Otago, Sat 11th May, 12 & 6hrs
          Once again Ed Stevens and Terry Davis of Highland Events have secured two fantastic Central Otago fine wool properties, Timburn and Long Acre Stations in the Lindis Valley, for their Autumn Rogaine on 11th May.  The topography is awesome ranging from 400m to 800m with heaps of interesting features. The terrain is well tracked Central Otago country with mainly open native grasses and some scrubby gullies. Again, there will be a 3 hr family Rogaine which proved very popular in the Tarras Spring Rogaine.

This will be a traditional style of Rogaine, not cryptic, with controls identified by an A5 sheet of weather proof material with a code to be recorded. See the Highland Events website, http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/rogaine, for event details and online entry. Entries close 6th May.


Auckland Rogaine Series, North-West Auckland, 12th May to 9th June
         The Auckland Series of 4 x 90min events begins Sunday, 12th May. See the series website, http://rogaineseries.co.nz/ for complete information and online entry.


T'Walk (24hr Walk), Canterbury, Sat/Sun 18th - 19th May
           It is on yet again and entries are open. This will be the 47th running of this iconic annual event, organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club, the pioneer of long distance foot navigation events in NZ.

T’Walk differs from a Rogaine in that teams navigate their way around pre-determined routes. It is a must-do event if you're at all into orienteering/rogaining, getting lost in the backcountry, reading maps, wearing silly costumes, adventure racing, matagouri, food, hanging out in woolsheds, failing at reading maps... and all things in between! The emphasis is on having fun. See the T'Walk website, http://www.cutc.org.nz/twalk/, for online entries and full information. Entries close 15th May.

 
Heights of Winter 2013, South Canterbury, Saturday 8th June
           In a break from tradition the annual Heights of Winter rogaine heads south of Christchurch to Woodbury, near Geraldine. This year there will be a 10hr option (replacing the usual 12hr event), a 6hr option, and a 2.5hr event for local schools.

The organising/planning team (Stuart Payne, Martin Etherington, Mike Harding and Dave Laurie) have found some fantastic country. The terrain includes rolling farmland to more rugged hills with streams and a small river. The height variation over the course is approx 450m. Vegetation is variously open farmland, tussock country, and some wooded areas. Entries are now open and close 29th May. See the Peninsula and Plains Orienteers (PAPO) website http://www.papo.org.nz/ for complete information and online entry.


Navquest Rogaines, various North Island locations, 9th to 30th June
          The NavQuest is a set of four 3hr rogaines, each in a different location in the North Island, aimed at secondary school students, their families and teachers. Members of the public are more than welcome to join in by entering in the open category. Go to http://www.opc.org.nz/navquest/ for more information and to register a team online. The NavQuest is organised as a partnership between the Sir Edmund Hillary Outdoor Pursuits Centre of New Zealand (OPC) and orienteering clubs in Rotorua, Auckland, and Wellington. Coordinating organiser is Darren Ashmore.

Etiquette – Rogaining Rules and Code of Conduct
 
           As the Rogaining season gets into full swing people are reminded of the rules and etiquette under which Rogaines are run. The general principle is that Rogaining is an amateur sport and should be fun but fair. Official Rules of Competition are applied rigorously to championship events but are usually relaxed somewhat for non-championship rogaines. The general tenor of the rules still applies however. In particular, in fairness to all teams there is no relaxation of the rule that ALL members of a team must visit a checkpoint together in every event:
R10. Members of a team shall remain within unaided verbal contact of one another at all times whilst on the course. A team shall demonstrate compliance with this requirement to any event official or other team on request.

R16. All team members shall simultaneously approach to within 5 metres of each checkpoint for which points are claimed.
Further, our sport relies on the goodwill of landowners who are usually more than happy to host us on their land. To maintain this goodwill all participants are asked to read and follow the NZRA Code of Conduct.

R&R Sport Wicked Rogaine Autumn Series, Port Hills, Christchurch
          This mid-week Autumn series, organised by Nora Audra and Ian Edmond, was reduced to two events of 2hrs each because of the continuing restricted access to the Port Hills due to the rock-fall risk following the earthquakes. The first event, which finished in light rain, was based at Taylors Mistake and the second in Hansens Park at the foot of Mt. Vernon. A great time was had by all. For results consult the Wicked Facebook.
