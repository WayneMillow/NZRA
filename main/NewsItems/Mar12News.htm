Wellington Afterwork Series, April to November
                A series of 3hr afterwork Rogaines at monthly intervals in the Wellington area and under the auspices of O Hutt Valley. The first event is on 4th April. See the Maptalk calendar, http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine, for details.


T'Walk (24hr Walk), Canterbury, Sat/Sun 5/6 May
               This will be the 46th running of this iconic event, organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club, the pioneer of long distance foot navigation events in NZ. It differs from a Rogaine in that teams navigate their way around pre-determined routes. Emphasis is on fun. Twalk is a must-do if you're at all into orienteering/rogaining, getting lost in the backcountry, reading maps, wearing silly costumes, adventure racing, matagouri, food, hanging out in woolsheds, failing at reading maps... and all things in between! See the T'Walk website, http://www.cutc.org.nz/twalk/


Navquest Series, Sunday 6th May through Sunday 24th June
             The NavQuest is a series of four 3hr rogaines aimed at secondary school students, their families and teachers. Members of the public are more than welcome to join in by entering in the open category. Go to http://www.opc.org.nz for more information and to register a team online. The NavQuest  is organised as a partnership between The Sir Edmund Hillary Outdoor Pursuits Centre of New Zealand (OPC) and NI orienteering clubs in Rotorua, Auckland, Wellington and Taupo. Coordinating organiser is Darren Ashmore.


Heights of Winter, Canterbury, Saturday 30th June
                The date of HOW 2012 has been set to coincide with a full moon. Note the date in your calendars. More information will be available in due course on the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz. There will be 12hr and 6hr options. If you can assist with course planning and setting, or admin on the day, please contact Dave Laurie, lauriefam@xtra.co.nz or Pete Squires, petersquires@ihug.co.nz .


2012 Australian Championships, South Australia, 5th and 6th May
                The 2012 Australian Championships are to be held in the ruggedly beautiful Flinders Range. Regular entries close 6th April with an absolute late entry cut-off of 20th April. Kiwis have a proud history of good performances in the Ozzie Champs so we hope to see many of you there. Website: http://rogaine.asn.au/arc2012.

These championships will be a qualifying event for the 11th World Championships in Russia, 27 – 28 July 2013. (Note that future World Champs are to be run annually). The entry criteria for Russia are still being determined but they are expected to be similar to those for the 10th WRC in Czech Republic later this year (http://www.rogaining.cz/wrc2012/files/entry_criteria.pdf).

Annual General Meeting, 27th February, Christchurch
            The NZRA AGM was held on Monday 27th February. The minutes of the meeting and the Presidents report are available on the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/NewsItems/AGM2012_minutes.pdf

Of interest is the proposed “national series” of four or six rogaines, of six hours or longer, and with an equal number in the North and South islands. Initially most of the series will be based as much as possible on currently running events rather than new ones. While exact details are yet to be finalised, the plan is that individual scores will be tracked, and the person with the highest total from all but one event will be the winner. It is anticipated that this series will improve the participation rate and quality of larger rogaines.

Be sure to read both the Presidents report and the Minutes to gain a little understanding of the behind the scenes administration of your Association.


Recent Events

Little River Rogaine Festival, Banks Peninsula, Saturday 3rd March

Organiser Nora Audra reports -
“The R&R Sport Little River Rogaine Festival was planned to be a day full of action and with options for all tastes, from the 1.5hrs family event to an extreme 12hrs event. It was all going to plan. We got the entries, set some challenging courses, got the local school involved.... it looked like we were set for an awesome weekend... BUT the only thing we didn't have control of was the weather. The forecast during the week wasn't promising and sure enough the horizontal rain and the strong winds turned what was planned to be a nice day out into an extreme adventure.  Lucky we weren't holding the event in the North Island where the weather was even worse!

For safety reasons we decided to shorten the 12hrs course to make sure that all participants were off the cloud covered high peaks before dark. It made the course even more challenging, as they had to choose carefully which controls to miss out. We had some disappointed people at the start, but we are sure that once they were out there, exposed to the conditions, they were relieved that hot food and the comfort of the hash house was two hours closer.

Over the day we had also a 6hr, a 3hr and 1.5hr events. It was very rewarding to see local kids having a go in the 1.5hr family event which we offered free for all locals. Most of them had never done a rogaine before and when they returned all we could hear was laughs of excitement and great stories.

Fortuitously the weather improved a bit during the day and everybody made back home safely apart from one team that needed to be rescued (thanks to Little River Fire Brigade) because of a flared-up old knee injury. The concluding food fest was hotly appreciated by everybody, again thanks to Little River School for the amazing food.

It was the first 12hr event we have organised and we were really happy with the outcome. From experienced rogainers to total newbies, all we heard was positive feedback and tall tales of high adventures, which for us is the best reward.  It takes a while to put the course together and without the local land owners generosity we wouldn't be able to do so. We are looking forward to organising more events in the future.”

See the results at http://www.madeventz.co.nz/tmrogaine
