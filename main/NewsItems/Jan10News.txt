Waitangi Rogaine, Port Hills, Christchurch - Saturday, 6th February
======================================================================
     Joel Smith, Elisha Nuttall and Jess Collingwood are organising a Rogaine on
Waitangi day in the Godley Head area with the start/finish at Taylors Mistake car park.
It will run from 6pm - 9pm with registration from 5pm. Proceeds will be used to fund
the Middleton Grange Outdoor Pursuits team to compete at the Hillary Challenge in
Turangi. For further information and an entry form see the event website
http://www.waitangirogaine.110mb.com/.

2010 Australian Championships, Snowy Mountains, 27-28 March 2010
================================================================
       The 2010 Australian Rogaining Championships will be hosted by the ACT Rogaining
Association in the magnificent Snowy Mountains. The course will be set by Australian
rogaining champions, David Baldwin and Julie Quinn, and will provide excellent
navigational challenges in a beautiful environment. Terrain will vary from alpine ash
forests, through snow gum woodlands and granite boulder fields, to open plains. Autumn
in Kosciuszko typically provides superb clear days with cool evenings. The Hash House
will offer ideal camping and there is caravan park and lodge accommodation within 5
minutes drive.

Numbers are limited to 550 people so get your entry in via the website,
http://www.act.rogaine.asn.au/arc2010. This is a great opportunity for people to build
their 24hr experience in preparation for the World Champs in New Zealand in November.


2010 New Zealand Championships, Wellington, 17-18 April 2010
=============================================================
       This is yet another opportunity to enhance your 24hr Rogaine experience in
preparation for the World Champs next November - a great chance to optimise your effort
over 24hrs, test your concentration, and learn the idiosyncrasies of your team mates
under the stress of a 24hr Rogaine.

The "Moonshine24" New Zealand Championships, will be hosted by Orienteering Hutt Valley
in the Akatarawa Ranges, near Wellington. Chief organiser is Mike Sheridan so a
challenging event is assured. Course planning is complete and is all ready to go.

Entry forms are now available. See the event website, http://www.moonshine24.co.nz/ ,
for in-depth information, pictures, blog, and entry form. There will be 3hr, 6hr and
12hr non-championship events in addition to the 24hr championship.
