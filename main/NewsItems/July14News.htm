Dear Rogainer,

The Golden Road Rogaine
El Dorado Station, Northeast Otago, Saturday 12th July

Entries close next Saturday 5th July. The Golden Road offers 13-hour “Bakers Dozen”, 6-hour “Introduction”, and 3-hour “Family Special” rogaines. The terrain, northwest of Waikouaiti and a one-hour drive from Dunedin, is relatively open farmland and tussock with rocks and scrub, plus some forest. Details and pictures are available on the NZRA website. Enter now. Note that the organisers are offering a $5 discount to people who entered the cancelled Heights of Winter. Take a look at the List of Entrants.
General Enquiries: Bob Cunninghame, signalhill@xtra.co.nz, ph. (03) 473 8330.


More Rogaines

Victoria Park Rogaine (incorporating the Canterbury Secondary School Rogaine Champs)
Christchurch Port Hills, Sunday 6th July
           PAPO is organising a 3hr Rogaine incorporating the Canterbury Secondary School Rogaine Champs, and a 1 hour event aimed at families. It is at Victoria Park this Sunday 6th July. This event is open to everyone and is a fund raiser to assist the top Senior and Junior School teams to attend the National championships in Rotorua, 9th August. Planner is Tim Farrant. See the PAPO website.

Navquest, Taupo, Sunday 6th July
           The final event in the 2014 Navquest series is near Taupo on 6th July.  This 3hr rogaine is aimed at secondary school students, their families and teachers but anybody is welcome to join in. For more information and entry see http://www.opc.org.nz/navquest/. Hurry, entries close tomorrow 2nd July.

Welly Afterwork Rogaine Series, next event 23rd July
          The next 3hr afterwork Rogaine is on 23rd July in the streets and bush reserves from Island Bay to the Windmill. For more information see the Welly Rogaine website.

NZ Secondary School Rogaine Championships, Saturday 9th August
           The inaugural NZ Secondary School Rogaine Championships, officially sanctioned by the NZ Secondary School Sports Council and by Orienteering NZ, will be hosted by Orienteering Bay of Plenty (OBoP) in conjunction with the Great Forest Rogaine (see below). There will be Senior 4 hour and Junior 3 hour events in Mixed, Male and Female categories. Full details and the entry form are now available at http://www.orienteering.org.nz/nzss-rogaine-champs/. If you know of a school wishing to partake the person to contact is Suzanne Scott at suebrook08@gmail.com or phone 021 233 9078. Entries close 25th July.

Great Forest Rogaine, Whakarewarewa Forest, Rotorua, Saturday 9th August
         There are 3hr and 6hr, foot or MTBO, rogaine options in the Great Forest Rogaine organised by Orienteering Bay of Plenty (OBOP). For more information and to enter check out www.obop.org.nz or email orienteeringbop@gmail.com

28th Winter Classic, Wellington, Sunday 17th August
          A 3hr Rogaine is included in the Winter Classic orienteering event organised by Bryan Teahan and Wellington Orienteering club. All are welcome as individuals or teams, and the Rogaine Team Explorer event includes categories for school teams or corporate. Bryan claims:
“This year’s area is another cracker on a new map and area which can only add to the legend of the Winter Classic. Beautiful open pine forest in steep to rolling terrain, sprinkled with some open native bush gullies with rock and cliff features. As a navigator / explorer who craves the ultimate challenge, this year is definitely one not to miss.”
For information and entry details see the website.

Events just past

Heights of Winter 2014
           The annual Heights of Winter was victim to heavy rains in North Canterbury leading to saturated ground and flooded streams, and cancelled for the first time in 15 years. This was very disappointing for both the competitors and the organisers, who had all other aspects of the event well-covered.

Hamels Haunts V, Dunedin, Sunday 29th June
            Matt Bixley’s entry-level three hour event designed to be training for Spring Challenge, and other longer navigation events, attracted 115 people in glorious Dunedin weather on Sunday just past. For results and comments see Matt's blog.

Auckland Rogaine Series, Sunday 8th June
            A record number of 300 people individually and in teams took part in the final 90min rogaine in the South Woodhill forest on 8th June. Results and pictures from the series, organised by the North West Orienteering Club, are available on the series website.
 

Championship Rogaines

12th World Rogaining Championships
Black Hills, South Dakota, 16th-17th August 2014
           Not long to the Worlds now. The latest Kiwis to register are Greig Hamilton and Matt Scott; that brings the total Kiwi contingent to 34. We wish all our representatives well and hope to see some significant performances. The event website and blog is at http://peakassurance.net/. An article by Bud Laird on Rogaining in S. Dakota gives participants an introduction to what to expect. See the list of entrants.

Australasian Championships (Goldfields Bonanza)
Victoria, weekend 11th-12th October 2014
           Set in an old goldfields area in the Castlemaine region, the terrain is a complex and challenging mixture of spurs and gullies handy to Melbourne. There will be a 6hr event as well as the championship 24hr. Details at  http://vra.rogaine.asn.au/arc14/.

New Zealand Championships 2014
Otago, weekend 6th-7th December
          The 2014 New Zealand Champs will be based in the lower Nevis Valley with its unique landscapes and historic remnants of the gold mining era. This interesting valley is quite remote but is readily accessible in summer; Google “Nevis Valley” for a glimpse of what the area offers. Chief organisers are the experienced Highland Events team of Ed Stevens and Terry Davis who are well known for their twice yearly Wanaka Rogaines. Look out for more information on the NZRA website.
