New Zealand Rogaining Association (Inc.)
25th May 2004


Dear Rogainer,

This is a short note to alert you to the following events scheduled over the next
couple of months.


Canterbury Rogaine – Sunday 30th May, 3 and 4 hr options, Waikari area, North
Canterbury.
========================================
     This event is being organised by Stuart Payne for PAPO. Enter on the day. The
map will be 1:25000. Start time is 10-30am with registration beginning at 8-45am.
Allow for a 70min drive from Christchurch. See the PAPO website,
http://www.geocities.com/paponz/, for further information.


Multisport Bikes Piha Rogaine – Saturday 12th June, 3hr & 6hr options, Piha
Auckland.
=================================================
     See the Lactic Turkey website, http://www.lacticturkey.co.nz/, for more
information.


Heights of Winter – Saturday 26th June, 12hr & 6hr events, Cheviot area, North
Canterbury.
===========================================
     Course planning and organisation is well under way for this popular annual
event. Go to the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz, for an information sheet,
entry form, and to see the current list of entrants.

Aussie Champs
==============
     There are already 3 NZ teams entered in the Australian Championships to be
held north of Adelaide, South Australia on July 3-4, http://sa.rogaine.asn.au/.



RESULTS
========
Results for, and comment on, the following events that have been run over the last
month are accessible via the NZRA website, www.rogaine.org.nz.

    (1) Hawke's Bay "Nevis Ay Die" (17th April)

    (2) Hopefull Rogaine, Marlborough (24th April)

    (3) T'Walk, Canterbury (8/9th May)

    (4) 6th World Rogaining Champs, Arizona, USA (8/9th May)
            The 11 Kiwis in 6 teams competed with some distinction at these World
Champs. See the NZRA website for a report by Pete Squires. The next World Champs
will be held on October 13/14, 2006 on our doorstep in Australia near Sydney. Maybe
YOU should be there!!

Regards,
NZRA.
