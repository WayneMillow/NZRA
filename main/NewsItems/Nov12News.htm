Dear Rogainer,

Moonlight on Mahinerangi 24hr & 7hr Rogaines
Otago, 24th & 25th November

Not long until entries close – next Saturday 10th November
Get your entry in now

The organisers and planners have a great event in store for us. The course is currently being finalised and all other arrangements are in hand. Reports are that the landscape will be challenging with intricate drainage patterns in largely open farm land and tussock country, woodlots and plantations. The event venue is centred on a woolshed with plenty of camping and, while we cannot control the weather, the moon will be 81% illuminated.

Get your entry in now. See the event website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz/nzchamps2012/, for further details and online entry. Transport is available from Dunedin city and Dunedin airport. Final instructions, including directions to the event site, will be emailed to entrants one week before the Rogaine.

We hope to see you there wandering around the country-side
under a glorious moon.



Other events not far off

Great Green Meadowbank Challenge, East Auckland, Saturday 10th November
           This must be a first – a 2 hr Rogaine organised by 9 and 10yr old pupils (Year 5) at Meadowbank School as part of their curriculum under advice from Shaun Collins of Lactic Turkey Events. The children invite all-comers to join in the fun on Saturday 10th November. There is more information on the event website, http://www.meadowbankrogaine.wikispaces.com/. Entries close 6th November (tomorrow) so don’t delay.


R&R Sport Wicked Rogaine Spring Series, Port Hills, Christchurch
          The Spring series is back. Organised by Nora Audra and Ian Edmond the series will run on Wednesday 14th, 21st and 28th November at 6pm. (Registration and maps from 5pm). Due to the earthquakes large expanses of the Port Hills are still not available to operate a 3hr event so this year there will be three 2hr events. Consult the Wicked Facebook page for further information including the location of each event.
