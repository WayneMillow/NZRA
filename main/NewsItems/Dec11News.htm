Christchurch Christmas Twilight, Wednesday 21st December
            Emily Wall and Chris Forne are back in Christchurch after an extended OE. As the following message shows they are quickly back in the saddle organising the Christmas Twilight.

Ho Ho Ho the Christmas Rogaine is ON!

Hi everyone! We are excited to present the 2011 Christmas Rogaine! More details will follow shortly; we are working hard to sort things out with the landowners and will provide more information as soon as we get it. In the mean time we can tell you it will take place on an exciting and brand new map at the southern end of the Port Hills.

DATE: Wednesday 21st December
TIME: 6pm to 8.30 pm
8.30 - 9pm Christmas Party

So the event will be a slightly unusual length - 2.5 hours. The last 1/2 hour of a 3 hour rogaine is usually the hardest bit, so we thought we would substitute that for a bit of eating and post-rogaine chat instead. We will have some Christmas goodies to give out as prizes and some little eats for everyone to enjoy afterwards. The cost for entry is $20 pp, $15 for student or unwaged people and free if you‘re under 10.

Entries will be taken on the night but we will be printing the maps on Tuesday so if you want to be guaranteed a map you need to email christmas.rogaine@gmail.com before Monday 19th at 22.00

Please visit http://www.christmas-rogaine.blogspot.com/ for all the latest information

Emily and Chris

Office Hours, North Canterbury, Sunday 4th December has been run
       Congratulations to everyone who participated in the Office Hours rogaine in the Clarence and Waiau basins and ranges beyond Hanmer. After a harrowing weather forecast we were blessed with mild temperatures and winds from the north-west, with showers only setting in for the last hour. For seven hours 48 teams (115 people) worked their way around their chosen share of the 40 check points.

Big thanks to Grant Hunter, Chrys Horn and Linda Lilburne for a very pleasant and well organised event.  Results are posted on the NZRA website http://www.rogaine.org.nz/.


WRC2012, Aug/Sept, Czech Republic – registration and NZ wildcard entry
        Phase 1 of the registration process for WRC2012 closes on 14th December. People who prequalify by reason of past performance must register by 14th December to take advantage of their prequal position. The particular criteria for this event have lead to Kiwis being on the prequal list who probably didn’t expect to be there. This may alter your desire to attend. Check out the list of prequals, and the entry criteria and process, on the event website http://www.rogaining.cz/wrc2012/en/

Teams registering after 14th December, whether they prequalify or not, will have a diminished chance of gaining entry. Since entries opened on 1st December nearly 600 applications for the 1000 available places have been lodged.

For WRC2012 there is provision for selection by individual countries of up to 6 wildcard teams per country. These wildcard entries are intended for deserving people who fail to gain entry via the other routes in the entry criteria. A document explaining the New Zealand wildcard selection criteria and process is available on the homepage of the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz/.
