
New Zealand Rogaining Association (Inc.)
6th July 2008


Dear Rogainer,

The following rogaines scheduled within the next 3 months may interest you. As usual
consult the indicated event websites for further information.


Lactic Turkey Huia Rogaine - Auckland, 6hr & 3hr options, Saturday 12th July
==========================
Entries close this Wednesday, 9th July. Consult the Lactic Turkey Events website,
http://www.lacticturkey.co.nz/ , for details and online entry.


Marlborough Rogaine - Marlborough, 6hr & 3hr options, Sunday 27th July
===================
Organised by the Marlborough Orienteering Club. In the 3hr you may choose either an
urban or rural course. The 6hr event encompasses both courses. Download detailed
information and entry form from the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz . Entries
close Tuesday 22nd July.


Silver Peaks 2008 - Otago, 12hr & 6hr options, Saturday 27th September
=================
In the Silver Peaks area west of Dunedin, organised by Bob Cunninghame and team. There
are extensive areas of native bush and scrub, some pine forest, and open tussock areas.
Travel will be mostly on the numerous tramping and forestry tracks. Altitude ranges
between 50 and 780 metres. Details and an entry form will be available on the NZRA
website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz , closer to the event.


TrustPower Adventure Quest - Queenstown area, Saturday 4th October
==========================
The next TrustPower Adventure Quest is set to be held on the 4th October this year, mark
your calendar. Consult the event website,
http://www.southerntraverse.com/AdventureQuest/ .


MACPAC Control 90 Multi-sport Rogaine - Lake Brunner, 12hrs, Saturday 18th October
=====================================
Three rogaines - kayak, bike, and hike - each of 4hrs make up this 12hr event with a
compulsory 2hr stop between disciplines. The event is for 2-person teams. Promoter is
Nathan Fa'avae. See the website http://www.control90.co.nz for more information.



    -------------------
    CHAMPIONSHIP EVENTS
    -------------------

2008 Australian Championships - Inverell, NSW, Friday/Saturday, 15th/16th August
=============================
To be held in the Copeton Waters State Recreation Area near Inverell in northern NSW.
There is an open invitation for Kiwis to partake. For more information and entry
particulars see the New South Wales Rogaining Association website,
http://www.nswrogaining.org . Buses will be provided from both Sydney and Brisbane to
the event.


2008 World Rogaining Championships - Estonia, 13th/14th September
==================================
There are 30 Kiwis among the 800 entrants, in 358 teams, in the World Champs. Chris
Forne and Dennis de Monchy will be defending their title on what is expected to be a
predominantly forested and flat course - at least compared to what we expect in NZ. The
event website is at http://8wrc2008.rogain.ee/ . A full list of teams is provided on
that website so have a look there to see the people who will be representing NZ.

Partner Wanted for World Champs
-------------------------------
Michael Wood is looking for a partner, or team willing to accept an extra person, in the
World Champs. Unfortunately the partner he had arranged now cannot go. Michael is a
well-performed male supervet but would be willing to compete in a younger class. See the
NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz for Michael's contact details.




Heights of Winter Trophy
========================
    If anyone happens to know where the trophy for the Mixed Open winning team is then
please contact Dave Laurie at lauriefam@xtra.co.nz .
    330 people in 129 teams enjoyed a balmy day for the annual Heights of Winter run in
the Cheviot area, North Canterbury. There were 205 people in the 12 hour event. For a
report and results see the NZRA website.
