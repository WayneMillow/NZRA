T'Walk 2011, Canterbury, weekend 14th & 15th May, 24hrs
========================================================
      Entries are now open for this classic event, the longest running 24hr 
"Rogaine" in the world.  Organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club, 
the pioneer of long distance foot navigation events in NZ, this will be the 45th 
running of T'Walk (Twenty-four Hour Walk). While the format is different to a true 
Rogaine it does have all the navigational and endurance challenges of a full scale 
24hr Rogaine. Come, join in the fun. Full details and entry are available on the 
T'Walk website, http://www.cutc.org.nz/twalk/index.php. Entries close 11th May.


R&R Sport Rogaine, Christchurch, Saturday 14th May, 3hrs
=========================================================
      Nora and Ian have arranged this consolation event to keep your interest up 
after the Autumn Taylors Series was cancelled as a result of the Christchurch 
earthquake. There is a 1hr Family category again. It is based at Living Springs so 
will be on a part of the Port Hills not used previously. See 
http://www.madeventz.co.nz/tmrogaine/ for information and entry form.


City Safari, Wellington, Sunday 15th May, 6hrs and 3hrs
========================================================
     The City Safari allows you to use Public Transport: bus, train, cablecar, 
ferry. You can use your head instead of your legs to get partway to the checkpoints. 
For more information about this event, organised by Michael Wood, see the website 
http://www.citysafari.org.nz .


Mitre 10 Ardgour Rogaine, Wanaka, Saturday 28th May, 12hrs and 6hrs
=====================================================================
     Ed Stevens and Terry Davis are organising their usual late autumn/early winter 
rogaine again. It will  be run on the Central Otago merino country of Ardgour and 
Bendigo sheep stations. Ed tells us that this year he will be replacing the cryptic 
clues with a more standard checkpoint format. Mark this event in your calendar - it 
will be good preparation for Heights of Winter 3 weeks later. Entries are now open 
via the website http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/rogaine.


Heights of Winter, Canterbury, Saturday 18th June, 12hrs and 6hrs
===================================================================
      Peninsula and Plains Orienteers are the organisers this year. Chief organiser 
is Stuart Payne assisted by Ian Edmond and Nora Audra. There will be 12hr and 6hr 
rogaines in the Amuri area, North Canterbury. A great event is assured so mark it in 
your calendar. More information will be posted in due course on the NZRA website, 
http://www.rogaine.org.nz/.

2011 New Zealand Championships, Marlborough 19th - 20th March
==============================================================
      The Eve of the Harvest championships were run in perfect weather in the hills 
not far out of Blenheim. Mondo Kopua set an interesting course which challenged all 
of us. The mens open (Jamie Stewart and Greig Hamilton) and womens (Debbie Mansfield 
and Julie Gordon) winners were unchanged from last year. The mixed category was a 
tight race with Emma and Guy de Lacey triumphing over Vivienne Prince and ozzy 
Richard Robinson by a mere 20 points.

Thanks to Mondo and his team for all the work that he put into the event. It all 
went like clockwork.

Full results, maps, and thoughts from Mondo can be found at 
http://rogaine.org.nz/nzchamps2011/results.htm.



Taranaki Secondary Schools Rogaine
===================================
      This 3hr Rogaine on the 30th March, sponsored and supported by NZRA, attracted 
457 entries and is destined to become a top-line regular event on the rogaining 
calendar. Taranaki organisers headed by Nick Collins and Annie Sanderson are to be 
congratulated for this initiative. Nick has provided a report at 
http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/NewsItems/TSS_2011.pdf. Of particular interest is a 
texting interchange used to guide lost students back to base. 
	  Full results are available at 
http://bubbles.school.nz/tssrogaine2011/Results.htm.
