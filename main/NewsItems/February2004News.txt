Dear Rogainer,

(1) Note that entries close shortly for the New Zealand Championship and Naseby
YODA Rogaines. More detail below.

(3) Partner Wanted for NZ Champs.


Green Coast New Zealand Rogaine Champs - March 6 & 7th, Wairamarama, Auckland South.
===============================================================================
      These Champs promise to be a special event in the superb rolling farmland
and bush terrain of the Wairamarama/Limestone Downs area. The Championship
Rogaine will be for the standard 24 Hour duration (1.00pm Saturday to 1.00pm
Sunday). Detailed event information and an entry form are available on the NZRA
website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz . Entries close Monday 1st March.

      There are not many 24hr events available so this is an opportunity for you
to indulge. However, for those who prefer shorter events there are
non-Championship options - a 16 Hour option (9.00pm Saturday to 1.00pm Sunday)
and a 6 Hour option (7.00am to 1.00pm Sunday). The unique 16 Hour event will
allow participants to experience the thrill
of night-time rogaining without the exertion of a full 24 Hour event.


YODA Naseby Hillbilly Rogaine – Saturday 13th March, 6 and 12 hrs
==================================================================
       This event at Naseby in historic Central Otago is to be run by YODA
(Youth Orienteers Dunedin Association) with Bob Cunninghame as the chief
organiser. Entries close 28th February. See the Dunedin Orienteers' website,
http://www.dunedinorienteering.com/ for information and entry form.


Port Hills Twilight Rogaine (Christchurch) – Sunday 14th March, 3hrs (6pm - 9pm)
===============================================================================
       Chief organiser is Dave Laurie. Entries close Wednesday 10th March. An
entry form is available at http://www.rogaine.org.nz .


Hopefull Rogaine (near Blenheim) – Saturday, 24th April, 3hr & 6hr events.
=========================================================================
          This will be another Barry Hope special organised for the Marlborough
Orienteering Club. Barry will find yet another new piece of Marlborough
countryside to use. More information will be available at
http://www.rogaine.org.nz as it becomes available.
