Events in the next couple of months
Great Forest Rogaine, Rotorua, Saturday 25th July, 3hr & 6hr options, foot and MTB
           The annual event organised in the Whakarewarewa Forest area by Orienteering Bay of Plenty provides the usual 3hr or 6hr, foot or MTB Rogaine options. This is a great fun team event and is suitable for all ages and abilities. See the OBOP website for information and online entry.

Kaiwara Rogaine, near Culverden, Saturday 25th July, 3 & 6hr options
           This mid-winter event in the Canterbury Rogaine Series is scheduled for Saturday, 25 July. The course will be predominantly in open rolling hill country, but will also include a few blocks of native and exotic forest. See the Canterbury Rogaine Series website for online entries and further information.

NZ Secondary School Championships, Saturday 8th August
            Orienteering Hutt Valley (OHV) will be hosting the NZ Secondary School Rogaine Champs in the Waiterere region on Saturday 8th August. More information may be found at http://nzssrogaine.org.nz/. For any queries email Andrew Riddle or phone 021 161 2637. Entries close Sunday 26th July. There will also be 3 & 6hr public Rogaines on the same course on the same day.

Hamel's Haunt VI, Dunedin, Sunday 9th August, 3hrs
          The 6th event in a series organised by Matt Bixley is based at Laing's Road, Dunedin. See facebook or Matt's blog.

Lowburn 3hr Rogaine, Lake Dunstan, Sunday 16th August
           This event is a Highland Events/Epic Navigation joint venture. Phil Wood, Australasian Champ, will be taking a navigation training session pre-event. A great fun family day out and an ideal introduction to Rogaining is promised. See the website for details and entry.

Wellington Afterwork Rogaine Series, Wellington, next event 30th July
           See the Welly website for information on the next event in the Welly series which is on Thursday 30th July in the region of Belmont Park.

Deborah Turner Memorial Rogaine, Sunday 8th November, 3hr relay
           This 3hr team relay, organised by the Hawke's Bay Orienteering Club in Hastings, was to be on 26th July but is now rescheduled to 8th November. See the HBOC website for details.

Recent Events
Auckland Rogaine Series, final event Sunday 7th June
             The Northwest Orienteering club had record entries for this annual series of 3 x 90 minute rogaines. Full results and a report are available on the NWOC website.

Equestrian Rogaine, Canterbury foothills, Sunday 14th June
         This was the 5th Equestrian rogaine to be organised by Carol Fowler and Stuart Payne. 88 horses in 27 teams enjoyed a fine, sunny day out in the hills on Brooksdale Station, a few km west of Springfield. See the results, pictures, and map of this unique Rogaine on Facebook.

Mid-Winter Night Rogaine Series, Christchurch Port Hills, 10th and 17th June
         Check out the results and pictures on the Wicked Rogaines website.

Wellington Afterwork Rogaine Series, Wellington, 18th June
            The monthly Welly afterwork in the Kaukau region on Thursday 18th June attracted 55 people who braved the strong northerly wind and heavy rain. For a full report and results see the Welly website.

Heights of Winter, North Canterbury, Saturday 27th June
         Over 400 people enjoyed a fine sunny day after an invigorating morning frost. See the results, pictures and planner's report on the NZRA website. Matt Bixley discusses the event on his blog.


Championship Events
New Zealand 24hr Championships, 31st October - 1st November, Marlborough
          This is the only annual 24hr Rogaine to be held in New Zealand. Chief organiser is NZRA President, Mondo Kopua. Even though classified as a "championship" the event is not just for the elite athlete but can be enjoyed by any team accepting their own level of challenge. Remember you don’t have to be out on course continuously for the full 24hrs. You can return to base for food (supplied) and rest at any time. There will be a shorter non-championship event as well. Keep your eye on the event website for more information as it becomes available.

3rd Australasian Championships, 10th – 11th October 2015
        Entries are open for the 3rd Australasian Championships to be held in the Capertee National Park about 3½ hrs west of Sydney on the weekend 10th – 11th October. This is a wonderful rogaining area with mainly rolling hills, river flats and open bushland providing easy travel. See the website for full information. Entries are open.

14th World Rogaining Championships (WRC2016), 23-24 July 2016
           The course for the 14th World Rogaining Championships in the MacDonnell Ranges east of Alice Springs, Australia is now set and awaits your participation. Take the opportunity to join in as it will be another 4 or 5 years before the World Champs are this close to home. Entries are limited so read the WRC2016 qualification tips if you are keen to be there. The event website has recently opened and registration begins 23rd October 2015.

That is it for now. Keep your eye on the event calendars for further rogaines.
