Metlink City Safari
===================
This coming weekend will see the running of the Metlink City Safari in Wellington. The
Safari is an urban rogaine where you have the option of using public transport (bus,
boat, train or cablecar) as well as your legs. You are challenged by having to mesh with
the standard transport timetables. As of a week ago there were 400 entrants and a few
late entries may be accepted. See the event website http://www.citysafari.org.nz .


T'Walk 2009
===========
T'Walk (24 hour walk) was held the weekend 2nd/3rd May in the Lake Heron area, based at
Castleridge Station. It was a magnificent location with a picturesque backdrop of
pristine snowy mountains. (Luckily there were no checkpoints above snow level). The night
temperature was crisp, the grass crunchy with frost and the streams were chilly. Being a
full week before the full moon the way was illuminated by starlight rather than
moonlight. 200 people took up the challenge and all came away smiling. Thanks to Rowan
and his team for a marvellous weekend. See results and comment on the T'Walk website,
http://www.cutc.org.nz/twalk/ .
