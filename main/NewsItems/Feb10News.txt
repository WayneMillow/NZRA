R&R Sport Taylors Mistake Series, 3 hrs, Christchurch - from Wednesday 3rd March
=================================================================================
    The popular R&R Sport Taylors Mistake Rogaine Series organised by Nora Audra and
Ian Edmond, and which runs for 5 successive Wednesdays, begins next Wednesday.  The
series will kick off at the Sign of the Bellbird, and then relocate to a different
spot around the Port Hills each Wednesday. So grab some friends and get a team
together for some super fun Wednesday evenings! A 1hr Family event will be provided.

Information and an entry form are available at http://www.madeventz.co.nz/tmrogaine
. Teams can enter on the night, but they must pre-enter to be guaranteed a map!

2010 New Zealand Championships, Wellington, 17-18 April 2010
=============================================================
     A reminder that entries are open for the NZ Rogaine Champs - Moonshine24. The
event will be in the Akatarawa Ranges between Upper Hutt and Kapiti on 17/18 April
2010. The start finish location is about an hour’s drive north of Wellington. This
event is your only opportunity to hone your skills on a 24 hour course in
preparation for the World Championships later in the year. The map will be purpose
built for the event.

Course planners are scouring the rugged bush as we speak, appearing briefly to
download GPS tracks and upload video onto the blog. The event, masterminded by Mike
Sheridan (Tararua24, RidgeRunners and other creations), will be Orienteering Hutt
Valley's 90-somethingth rogaine.

See the event website, http://www.moonshine24.co.nz/, for in-depth information,
pictures, blog, and the entry form. There will be 3hr, 6hr and 12hr non-championship
events in addition to the 24hr championship. Entries close 7th April.


TWALK 2010, Canterbury, 29-30 May
==================================
Dates for TWALK have been set! It will be held on 29-30 May, spread the word.

This will be the 44th running of T'Walk, organised by the University of Canterbury
Tramping Club, the pioneer of long distance foot navigation events in NZ. It differs
from a Rogaine in that teams follow a predetermined route. A "not to be missed"
event providing 24hrs of continuous fun. For details and entry information keep an
eye on the T'Walk website, http://www.cutc.org.nz/twalk/ .


2010 Australian Championships, Snowy Mountains, 27-28 March 2010
================================================================
       The 2010 Australian Rogaining Championships will be hosted by the ACT
Rogaining Association in the magnificent Snowy Mountains. The course will be set by
Australian rogaining champions, David Baldwin and Julie Quinn, and will provide
excellent navigational challenges in a beautiful environment. Terrain will vary from
alpine ash forests, through snow gum woodlands and granite boulder fields, to open
plains. Autumn in Kosciuszko typically provides superb clear days with cool
evenings. The Hash House will offer ideal camping and there is caravan park and
lodge accommodation within 5 minutes drive.

Numbers are limited to 550 people so get your entry in via the website,
http://www.act.rogaine.asn.au/arc2010. This is a great opportunity for people to
build their 24hr experience in preparation for the World Champs in New Zealand in
November.
