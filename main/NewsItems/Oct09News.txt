9th World Championships 2010 - Cheviot, 20-21 November 2010
===========================================================
     Online entries open in less than four weeks, on 1st November 2009. There is an upper
limit of 550 people so IT WILL BE IMPORTANT to get your application in as soon as
possible.

Entry criteria defined by the International Rogaining Federation (IRF) will guarantee
entry for some teams who have a history of good performance in 24hr Rogaines while for
others the order of application will be used in determining priority. Registrations will
be taken in three phases. Consult the website, http://wrc2010.org.nz/registration.htm for
explanation of the registration process.

The entry fee for the World Champs is $NZ180. NZRA is encouraging Juniors to enter by
offering a $NZ90 subsidy. (Juniors will need to pay the $180 up front and then claim the
subsidy from the NZRA Treasurer).


R&R Sport Taylors Mistake Series - Port Hills, ChCh
====================================================
     Nora Audra and Ian Edmond are organising a Spring version of this popular series
starting on October 14th and running for 3 successive Wednesdays. We will visit a
different part of the Port Hills ranging from the wilds of Kennedy's Bush through to the
fresh salt air of Sumner with a sweep through Rapaki along the way. The events will be
three hours in duration. For more information and an entry form visit
http://www.madeventz.co.nz/tmrogaine.


Mainly Tramping Wanaka Rogaine - Saturday, 14th November
========================================================
    There will be 6hr and 12hr options at the Snow Farm, Cardrona Valley. Entries close a
week before the event. See the event website http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/ for details.


Groundeffect CyclicSaga, Weekend 28th - 29th November, Canterbury
=================================================================
      The 2 day Cyclic Saga has been rescheduled to before Christmas. This popular MTB
Rogaine will have a shorter option, "The Son of Saga", each day. For complete information
see the Saga website, http://www.cyclicsaga.co.nz/.


2010 New Zealand Championships, Wellington, 17-18 April 2010
============================================================
         The 2010 New Zealand Championships are to be hosted by Orienteering Hutt Valley
in the Akatarawas, near Wellington. Chief organiser is Mike Sheridan so a challenging
event is assured. The event website is scheduled to open soon and will be accessible via
the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz.
