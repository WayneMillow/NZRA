New Zealand Rogaine Champs
======================
      The champs were run last weekend in the Wairamarama/Limestone Downs area about
50km south of Auckland. It was a brilliant area with a challenging course.
PROVISIONAL results and some impressions from Pete Squires are available on the
website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz . Final results should be available next week.
      Thanks are due to Mark Copland and his team for organising the first ever 24hr
rogaine to be held in the North Island. Well done, Mark.


Some future events
==================
  (1) Hawke's Bay "Nevis Ay Die" Rogaine – Saturday 17th April. There are 4, 8, &
12hr options. Entries close Tuesday 13th April. Download an entry form from
http://www.rogaine.org.nz .

  (2) Hopefull Rogaine (near Blenheim) – Saturday, 24th April, 3hr & 6hr events.
          This will be another Barry Hope special organised for the Marlborough
Orienteering Club. Barry will find yet another new piece of Marlborough countryside
to use. More information will be available at http://www.rogaine.org.nz as it becomes
available.

  (3) T'WALK - Canterbury, 8/9th May (24hrs). This will be the 36th running of this
event, organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club. Rogaining actually
evolved from a T'WALK (24hr Walk) run by Melbourne University students. T'WALK
differs from a Rogaine in that teams follow a fixed route. A "not to be missed"
event. Watch out for more information on the website.


     Other events to look out for are a 4hr Rogaine in Canterbury on Sunday 30th May,
the Lactic Turkey Multisport Bikes Piha Rogaine (Auckland) on Saturday 12th June, and
the 12hr Heights of Winter (Canterbury) on Saturday 26th June.
