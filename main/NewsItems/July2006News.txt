                         Rogaines in July
                       ====================                       

There will be two NZRA supported rogaines during the month of July. One is in Dunedin and the
other on Banks Peninsula in the Christchurch area.

(1) Hills and Valleys, Dunedin, 3 and 6hr options, Saturday 8th July, organised by Bob
Cunninghame. Entries close Monday 3rd July, i.e. in a couple of days. Go to the NZRA website,
http://www.rogaine.org.nz for details.


(2) Wairewa Rogaine, Little River, Banks Peninsula, Sunday 23rd July. This is a 6hr event
organised by Bruce Meder. Entries close Sunday 16th July. See the NZRA website,
http://www.rogaine.org.nz for further information.



The following rogaines will also be run in various parts of New Zealand during the month of
July.

(i) Lactic Turkey Huia, Auckland, 3 and 6hr options, Saturday 8th July. Entries close Monday
3rd July, i.e. very soon. Go to the Lactic Turkey website, http://www.lacticturkey.co.nz/ for
details.

(ii) OHV Birthday Rogaine, 3 and 6 hr, Upper Hutt, Sunday 9th July. This event is part of
Orienteering Hutt Valley's 30th birthday celebrations. For details see the NZOF rogaine
calendar at http://www.mapsport.co.nz/rog/rogaine.html

(iii) The Fox Hunt, Auckland, 3hr urban rogaine, Sunday 16th July. See the The Fox Hunt
website, http://www.thefoxhunt.co.nz/ for more information.



Heights of Winter
=================
    285 people enjoyed a perfect day and a magnificent course for the annual Heights of Winter
in Canterbury on June 10th (only 2 days before the big snow - Lady Luck shines on us
sometimes). Our thanks must go to the organising team for the sterling efforts they put in to
make our day so enjoyable. A report on the event by Grant Hunter is available on the Sportzhub
website, http://www.sportzhub.co.nz and full results are on the NZRA website.

   The results of other recent events are also available via the website
http://www.rogaine.org.nz


Primal Quest
============
      New Zealand Rogaining champions, Aaron Prince and Chris Forne, together with Sara Wallen
and Marcel Hagener are currently in 2nd place 5 days into this 6+ day adventure race in Utah.
Their team is called GoLite/Timberland. Have a look at the website
http://www.ecoprimalquest.com/2006/race/ . Awesome!!
