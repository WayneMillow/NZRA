Entries closing soon

Heights of Winter 2013, South Canterbury, Saturday 8th June
           Entries are flooding in for the Heights of Winter – don’t miss out; enter online now at the Peninsula and Plains Orienteers (PAPO) website http://www.papo.org.nz/. Entries close on 29th May - only 4 days away.

This year the Heights of Winter is based at Woodbury, near Geraldine. The options available are 10 hrs, 6 hrs and a short 2.5 hrs intended for, but not restricted to, parents and children from local schools. The terrain includes rolling farmland to more rugged hills with streams and a small river. The height variation over the course is approx 450m. Vegetation is variously open farmland, tussock country, and some wooded areas. See you there.


1st Australasian Championships, North Queensland, Sat-Sun 22-23 June 2013

Entries close in less than a week, on 31st May. Enter on-line at http://adventuresportnq.info/arc2013.

The event is being hosted by Rogaining North Queensland on the Atherton Tableland, a 40 minute drive from Cairns International airport.

It is unclear whether there will be restricted entry for South Dakota but if there is then this Australasian champs is the last for pre-qualification via the first 3 places in an age/gender category. Pre-qualification gives you assured entry into a World Champs. The full entry criteria are available. (Warning: not easy reading).

Navquest Rogaines, various North Island locations, 9th to 30th June
          The NavQuest is a set of four 3hr rogaines, each in a different location in the North Island, aimed at secondary school students, their families and teachers. Members of the public are more than welcome to join in by entering in the open category. Go to http://www.opc.org.nz/navquest/ for more information and to register a team online. The NavQuest is organised as a partnership between the Sir Edmund Hillary Outdoor Pursuits Centre of New Zealand (OPC) and orienteering clubs in Rotorua, Auckland, and Wellington. Coordinating organiser is Darren Ashmore.

Events just past

Wanaka Rogaine, Lindis Valley, Saturday 11th May
            A total of 304 people enjoyed the challenging vistas of the Timburn and Long Acre stations in the Central Otago rogaine organised in glorious weather by Highland Events on 11th May. There were 80 in the 12 hr, 140 in the 6hr, and the balance was made up in the 3hr family event.

Organiser Ed Stevens comments: "The highlight of the day for me was seeing 80 odd kids tearing off in various directions with their parents trying desperately to keep them restrained. This sport is very much alive in Central Otago".

The 12hr event went to the team of Nathan Faavae and Dougal Allan with a score of 3800, 2nd was Jim Cotter and Milan Brodina on 3450, closely followed by Matt Bixley and Nick Williamson with 3430 points for 3rd. Listen to Dougal Allan reflecting on his experience with Nathan at
http://www.maxyourmedia.com/radiosport/Radiosport140513_Allan.mp3.

Full results can be found on the Highland Events website, http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/rogaine.


T'Walk (24hr Walk), Canterbury, Sat/Sun 18th - 19th May
          The Canterbury University Tramping Club students did it yet again – they provided a great T’Walk  for us all to enjoy. The weather may have been rather moist, and the night dark, but that did not deter 288 people from straggling around the countryside for 24 hours in Lees Valley, North Canterbury.

There was the usual number of quirky checkpoints with some being a bit too quirky, and clues too cryptic, for many of us. Frustrating? Yes, but T’Walk does have a character of its own. Good food provided by cheerful cooks kept us going.

To the landowners – thanks for allowing us to range unrestrained across your properties. And to the bus drivers - the return bus ride was a highlight in itself. Thanks for managing to negotiate the tight bends in the Ashley Gorge without mishap.

See the T'Walk website, http://www.cutc.org.nz/twalk/, for results and maps.
