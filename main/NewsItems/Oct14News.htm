Australasian Championships
Victoria, weekend 11th – 12th October
    
There are only nine Kiwi entrants so far. We need more to give the Ozzies a run for their money so get your entry in for the “Fossicker’s Dream” 2014 Australasian Championships. Note that entries are being accepted up to the day of the event. 

These Championships will be in the Castlemaine region about 1½ hrs drive north-west of Melbourne. A complex mixture of spur/gully terrain in an old goldfields area will make this a challenging event so it will be wise to take advantage of the trial course the previous day. There will be a non-championship 6hr event as well as the championship 24hr Rogaine. For full information and entry see the champs website.


Other Rogaines in October & November

Welly Afterwork Rogaine Series, next event 14th October
          The next 3hr afterwork Rogaine, organised by Ann Kendon and Jo McKenzie, is in Wellington West (Karori) in largely rural terrain. See the Welly Rogaine website for details. If any non-Wellingtonians happen to be in Wellington on the dates of these events they are sure to be most welcome to join in the fun.
 
Waikato Rogaine Series
           A series of four 2hr Sunday Rogaines with the first on 12th October offering a 2hr foot rogaine preceded by a 1hr MTB rogaine if you wish – do one or both. Further events will be on 2nd Nov, 16th Nov, and 30th Nov. For more information see the Orienteering Waikato website.

EPIC Returns, near Arrowtown, Saturday 18th October, 12, 6 & 3hr options
          After a 4 year break Bruce McLeod and Phil Wood of EPIC (Enigmatic Pseudo Insane Challenge) Navigation fame are organising a rogaine somewhere near Arrowtown. There will be 3hr, 6hr, and 12hr options with the latter being a T'Walk style multi-loop event. Turn up and relive the fond memories of EPIC's last event - the 2010 "Ophir Awesome EPIC". For detailed information see the EPIC website.

Vegas Vindaloo MTBO Weekend, Rotorua, 18th - 19th October   
           The Vegas Vindaloo weekend includes a 2hr MTB rogaine in an area of the Whaka Forest that is not so well used. For more information check out the OrienteeringNZ and Orienteering Bay of Plenty websites.

Ahuriri Equestrian Rogaine, Tai Tapu, Canterbury, Sunday 19th October 
          Another 3hr horse rogaine organised by Stuart Payne and Carol Fowler for teams of 2 to 4 riders and horses in support of the GRACI Foundation (Gynaecology Cancer Research Trust). If your knees are failing you try a horse. See the event Facebook page.

Wanaka 3hr Rogaine, Saturday 1st November
         This 3hr family friendly Rogaine will be based at the picturesque Glendhu Station, Wanaka. The course, set by Ed Stevens (Highland Events) for Holy Family School, will take full advantage of the hilly, but benign, farmland terrain. Sign up on the online registration form now available.

Mt Evans, Banks Peninsula, Sunday 9th November, 3 & 6hr options
          Based at not-often-visited Camp Bay around the harbour from Purau Bay, the terrain is hilly farmland with a mix of open grassland, scrub, native forest, and some plantations. People will be rewarded with great views out across the harbour and beyond. See the Canterbury Rogaine website for details. Entries are open. Chief organiser is Alastair McDowell, rogaine.series@gmail.com or phone 021 1174 199.

Big Trig 2014, Akatarawas, Wellington, Saturday 29th November, 3, 6 or 9hr options
          Organised by O Hutt Valley in the Akatarawas. It will be an event worth going to, with rugged native bush, fantastic views over Cook Strait, and some easy areas for family groups. Gordon Balfour is the chief planner. For further information see the event website. Download a flyer.


Details for each of the above Rogaines are available on the individual event websites as above. For more Rogaines in the months ahead refer to either

New Zealand Championships 2014
Nevis Valley, Otago, 6th-7th December

The 2014 NZ Championships will be run on the Ben Nevis and Craig-Roy Stations in the Nevis Valley, south-west of Cromwell. This interesting valley is quite remote but is readily accessible in summer – Google “Nevis Valley” for a glimpse of what the area offers. Chief organisers are the experienced Highland Events team of Ed Stevens and Terry Davis who are well known for their twice yearly Wanaka Rogaines. Pete Squires is the NZRA representative. There will be a 12hr, 6hr, and 3hr Family events in addition to the Championship.

For more information, and to sign up, see the Highland Events website.


 
Recent Results

12th World Rogaining Championship, South Dakota, 16th-17th August 2014
               Now that the official results are out we can report on the New Zealand performances. The 34 Kiwi Rogainers acquitted themselves well, and with some distinction, at the World Championships run in the Black Hills of South Dakota. Take a look at the New Zealand results, articles, and pictures and an account of their journey by the winning team from Estonia, who won despite the handicap of losing their luggage.

Richard English has calculated the country honours list assigning gold: 3pts, silver: 2pts, and bronze: 1pt. The results are: Australia 15.5, USA 14, Estonia 11, NZ 10.5, Russia 8, Spain 3, Ukraine 3, Latvia 3, Finland 2, Canada 2. (The 0.5 arises from teams split between two countries).


Wily Weka Challenge, Nelson, Saturday 6th September
            There was a great turnout of 270 participants for the 3hr and 6hr Wily Weka Challenge run in the Nelson urban area and parks including the Grampians, Botanics and City. See the website for the results.

Highland Events Spring Rogaine, Central Otago, Saturday 13th September
          The Highland Events Spring Rogaine, enjoyed by over 250 participants on Cairnmuir Station, started out damp for the first couple of hours but then it cleared up for a sunny afternoon. Reports are that the terrain was steep with many ups and downs and not much flat land. See the Highland Events website for results of the 3hr family, 6hr and 12hr events.

Welly Afterwork Series, 16th September
          On the evening of Tuesday 16th September the Eastbourne bush across the harbour from Wellington city was alive with 74 participants enjoying a Welly Afterwork Rogaine. See the Welly Series website for the results and a report by planners Graeme Silcock and Sue Eastwood. For the dates of future rogaines in the Welly series see the OrienteeringNZ calendar.
