New Zealand Rogaining Association (Inc.)
1st June 2011
 

 Dear Rogainer,

Heights of Winter, Canterbury, Saturday 18th June, 12hrs and 6hrs

      Entries close next Monday, 6th June. Get your entry in now.

      Peninsula and Plains Orienteers are the organisers this year with Stuart Payne as chief assisted by Ian Edmond and Nora Audra. The terrain is attractive rolling hill country west of Culverden. There is a choice of 6-hour or 12-hour rogaines with food provided to refresh you at the end. See the PAPO website, http://www.papo.org.nz/, for information and online or paper entry.
 

Other events coming up include:
  • Nelson Afterwork Winter Series: A series of five afterwork rogaines on successive Wednesdays beginning 1st June in the Nelson region and organised by Nelson Orienteering. For more information consult the club website http://www.noc.org.nz/
  • Hutt Valley Afterwork Series - Wellington region: Keep your eye on the maptalk calendar, http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine, for the day and place of these events. They are usually of 3hr duration and start at 6pm following map handout at 5.30pm.
  •  NavQuest Secondary School Rogaines - upper North Island: There will be four 3hr rogaines in Auckland (2), Taupo and Rotorua organised by Darren Ashmore of the Edmund Hillary Outdoor Pursuits Center and assisted by Counties Manukau Orienteering. The events are aimed at secondary students but there is an open category as well. The first rogaine is on the 12th June so don't delay in getting an entry in. See the information on the OPC website, http://www.opc.org.nz/newevent.php
  • Annavale Attack (4hr) and Amble (3hr), Canterbury, Sunday 14th August: A navigational adventure in the Springfield region of the Canterbury foothills in support of Darfield HS Rural College. Mark it in your calendar. The 4-hour rogaine (Attack) is for the competitive and the 3-hour rogaine (Amble) is intended to be more recreational. See the information on the Darfield High School website.
  •  25th Winter Classic, Wellington, Sunday 21st August: The Winter Classic is an endurance, long distance orienteering event that is both physically and mentally demanding. There is a 3hr Challenge Rogaine run in conjunction with the event. See the Classic website for details. Entries close 14th August
  • Office Hours, Canterbury, Saturday 3rd December: Join NZRA for a pre-Christmas blowout. This mid-duration event will suit newcomers and trampers as well as regular rogainers. But, we’ll need help. If you’ve done a few events and are ready to contribute to planning and organising, here's an opportunity. Contact Grant Hunter at 351-6369 or g.hunter@clear.net.nz with your offers of assistance.
