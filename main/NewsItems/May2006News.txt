                  World Rogaining Championships, October - limited entry
                 ========================================================
   If you are planning to take advantage of the World Rogaining Champs being on our doorstep in
Australia then note that the event is limited to 500 people and filling fast. As of May 1st there
were 269 places taken. You need to get your entry in pronto.



                  Feast of Rogaines
                 ===================

  There is a rich feast of rogaines throughout NZ in the next 4 weeks:

1. Taylors Mistake Series final event (3hr), Christchurch, Thursday 11th May.

2. T'Walk 2006 (24hr), Canterbury, 20th and 21st May, entries close 17th May.

3. Wellington City Safari (6/3hr), Wellington, Saturday 20th May, entries close 10th May.

4. Wellington Afterwork series begins 24th May.

5. Marlborough MOC-Wards Rogaine (6/3hr), Marlborough, Saturday 27th May, entries close 23rd May.

6. Heights of Winter (12/6hr), Canterbury, Saturday 10th June, entries close 29th May.

7. Lactic Turkey Piha Rogaine (6/3hr), Auckland, Saturday 10th June, entries close 5th June.


Further details on these events may be accessed via the NZRA website, www.rogaine.org.nz .
