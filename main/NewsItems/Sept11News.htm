New Zealand Rogaining Association (Inc.)
8th September 2011
 

Dear Rogainer,

There are three rogaines, in Canterbury, Central Otago, and Auckland, scheduled for this month.

Canterbury Series, Lake Lyndon, Saturday 17th September
The second event in the Canterbury Series is in the Lake Lyndon district on Saturday17th September. As for the first event there will be a 3hr schools competition, a 3hr social grade, and a 4.5hr all-comers challenge.

The events are suitable for participants of all ages and abilities.  The school competition is a special feature of the series which seeks to promote rogaining and to further junior development through free entry to all high school students.

For more information about the series, and to enter online, see the website http://www.canterburyrogaine.com/ . Entries close Thursday 15th September.

Mt. Outdoors Rogaine, Earnscleugh Station, Saturday 17th September, 12 & 6 hrs
Organised by Ed Stevens and Terry Davis, and sponsored by Mt. Outdoors adventure store, this event is located in “awesome rogaining country” at the bottom end of Lake Dunstan on the other side of the Clutha River from Alexandra in Central Otago. See the website http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/rogaine for more information and to enter. Entries after Monday 12th September incur a late fee.

Lactic Turkey Peak Road Rogaine, Auckland, Sunday 25th September
This is a 3hr event on rolling farm land punctuated by magnificent Kauri trees and native bush near the north end of Riverhead Forest. Entries close 21st September.  Information and entry forms are available on the Lactic Turkey Events website, http://www.lacticturkey.co.nz/RogaineInfo.htm.

New Zealand Champs 2012 - notification
In a break from tradition we expect to move the 2012 New Zealand Champs from the usual autumn date into late spring with a tentative date of late November 2012. We will confirm the date later this year.
