The Ophir Awesome E.P.I.C., Central Otago, Saturday 11th September, 12 & 6hrs
=============================================================================
      The E.P.I.C. is back with 12 and 6hr Rogaines in the historic Ophir area, 
Central Otago. Organisers are Bruce McLeod and Phil Wood. For an entry form and 
information see the E.P.I.C. Navigation website 
http://www.epicnavigation.co.nz/. Entries close 7th September.


"Springtime in the Park", Marlborough, Saturday 11th September, 3hrs
====================================================================
      This is the 8th Marlborough Rogaine organised by the Marlborough 
Orienteering Club. It will be based in the Wither Hills Farm Park. Get details 
and an entry form from http://www.rogaine.org.nz/. Contact is 
holdaway@xtra.co.nz.  Entries close Wednesday 8th September.


Auckland Rogaine, Woodhill Forest, 12th September, 3hrs
=======================================================
The event is being organised and run by the Auckland Orienteering Club. Details 
and online entry at http://www.navevent.co.nz/AKRogaine2010/. Entries close 
Thursday, 9th September (enter on the day for a fee).
 

Wellington Afterwork Series, Wellington, next event 15th September
==================================================================
      The next event in this series of 3hr afterwork events in the Wellington 
area is on 15th September. There are further rogaines on 7th October and 2nd 
November. See the Maptalk calendar, 
http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine, for details. 


Katharine Eustace Fundraising Rogaine, Wanaka, Saturday 25th September
=======================================================================
      Fun family 3hr and 6 hr rogaines plus grab-a-map for those who just want 
to go out and find out how Rogaining works. HQ at the Albert Town Tavern. This 
will be great practice for the real deal on 23rd October.

This is a fundraising event for Katharine Eustace who is a keen Wanaka Rogainer 
with many other athletic talents. Katharine will be representing NZ at the 
World Champs, and competing on the World Circuit, in Skeleton Racing. So come 
along and help Katharine reach her goals. There will be a fundraising auction 
after the event plus other fun stuff. Highland Events organised a similar event 
last year and it was alot of fun. For further details see 
http://www.highlandevents.co.nz


Taylors Mistake Spring Series, Port Hills, Christchurch, begins 20th October
============================================================================
     This series of 3 x 3hr afterwork Rogaines on successive Wednesdays is 
being organised once again by experienced adventure racers Nora Audra and Ian 
Edmond. There will be a 1hr Family event as well. Don’t miss the opportunity to 
be out there experiencing the highlights of the Port Hills. An entry form is 
available at http://www.madeventz.co.nz/tmrogaine


Highland Events Rogaine Heaven, Wanaka, Sat 23rd Oct (Labour Weekend), 12 & 6hr
================================================================================
      Organised by Ed Stevens and based at the Albert Town Tavern, the course 
will be quite fast and also ideal for the beginner rogainer. The course 
features large mainly flat areas with low open Kanuka scrub and there are some 
slightly hilly parts going from 275m to 570m. See 
http://www.highlandevents.co.nz for further details.


The Great Forest Rogaine, Rotorua, Saturday 6th November, 8hr
=============================================================
      An 8hr foot event and a 6hr MTB event with the same controls for both. 
Utilising Rotorua's wonderful and right up-to-date Whaka Forest Map, this event 
is perfectly timed for a pre-World Rogaine Champs blowout, and will also 
feature as a part of the World Champion Team Orion's AR Training Weekend being 
held in and around Rotorua. The event website is now up and running and 
provides online entry at http://www.madeventz.co.nz/orionadventure. Contact is 
Mark Copeland, copeland@copelandlaw.co.nz.


World Championships, Cheviot, weekend 20th-21st November, 24hr
==============================================================
     The organisation of the World Championships by a small core team of people 
is on target and proceeding steadily, http://wrc2010.org.nz. Entries are closed 
but we are looking for volunteers to assist us during the event. If you can 
help please email Phil Bones at admin@wrc2010.org.nz to offer your services.


2011 Australian Championships, Tasmania, weekend 22nd-23rd January, 24hr
========================================================================
     To be hosted by Rogaining Tasmania in the northern midlands of Tasmania. 
The course will be set by experienced rogainers John Brock, Darryl Smith, Karen 
Pedley and Nigel Davies. The terrain will be a mixture of open plains 
(farmland) and steeper lightly wooded hills. See the event website, 
http://www.arc2011.rt.asn.au/. This is a qualifying event for the 10th World 
Championships, Czech Republic, 31st August 2012.
