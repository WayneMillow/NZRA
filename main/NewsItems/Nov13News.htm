Events coming up
Two significant longish Rogaines are on offer in November, one in each Island - the Golden Road organised by Bob Cunninghame and team in Otago on 23rd November, and the Belmont “Big Trig” organised by Michael Wood in Wellington the following weekend.

Prior to these two events there is the Lactic Turkey Karekare Gladefest in Auckland; and the afterwork series in Wellington and Palmerston North continue.


Karekare Gladefest
Karekare, West Auckland, Saturday 16th November
          Organised by Shaun Collins in support of the Lone Kauri Community School and based at the Pohutukawa Glade the course takes advantage of beach and bush with event durations of 90 minutes or 3hrs. Full details and entry are available from the Lactic Turkey website. Post-rogaine activity includes live music from local bands, face painting and lolly scrambles for children, and hot food and coffee for all.

The Golden Road Rogaine
Otago, Saturday 23rd November
           The Golden Road Rogaine is based at the El Dorado Woolshed on El Dorado Road. Organised by Bob Cunninghame and team, there will be a 13-hour “Classic”, a 6-hour “Introduction” and a 3-hour “Family Special”. The terrain, north-west of Waikouaiti and about an hour’s drive from Dunedin, is a mix of relatively open farmland, tussock country with rocks and scrub in places, plus some forest blocks. There are even several distinct volcanic cones. Download the event flyer, see the detailed information, and enter online now. Don’t delay, entries close in two weeks on Sunday 17th November.

Belmont “Big Trig” Rogaine
Belmont Regional Park, Wellington, Saturday 30th November
         Belmont Regional Park is a 3500ha triangle from the top to the bottom of Lower Hutt, and across to Cannons Creek, Porirua. It features farmland up to 456m with views to the South Island, 60 unusual WW2 ammunition bunkers, extensive areas of native bush and more. There will be 10hr and 6hr options. The event is being organised by Michael Wood under the Orienteering Hutt Valley banner. Download a flyer and check the event website for all the detail. Entries after Wednesday 20th November will incur a late fee.

Welly Afterwork Series, April to November
    The final event in this series of 3hr afterwork Rogaines at monthly intervals in the Wellington region is on Tuesday 19th November. If any non-Wellingtonian happens to be in Wellington they are sure to be most welcome to join in the fun. See the Maptalk calendar, http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine, for details of the Welly Series.

New Plymouth Urban Afterwork Series, 30th October to 4th December
        This is a series of six 90min or 3hr Urban rogaines at weekly intervals. You can walk, run or cycle. See the Maptalk calendar, http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine, for details.

12th World Rogaining Championships – registration is now open
Black Hills, South Dakota, 16th & 17th August, 2014
           As previously notified, if you are keen to participate in these championships you should register before 14th November. There are a total of 1000 places available. Some of these places are reserved for people who pre-qualify by reason of top performances in previous WRCs, regional championships and national championships. If you don’t know whether you pre-qualify consult the list of pre-qualifiers. (Note: be sure to scroll down the list which is split into two categories - automatic pre-qualifying entrants and preferred status pre-qualifying entrants).

Even if you don’t pre-qualify there is still a good chance that you will get in provided you register by the 14th November. See the event website for all the details and to register. After day one of registration there are already 15 Kiwis with various status in the list of entrants.
 

Recent Event Results

Canterbury Series "Hogs Back" Rogaine, Castle Hill Village, 19th October
          In contrast to the wind and snow of the previous weekend the Canterbury High Country turned on a stunning mid-October day for the return of the Canterbury Rogaine Series. The Hogs Back Rogaine was well supported with more than one hundred and sixty people taking part in the three and six hour courses on offer.

Top performers on the day were Matt Scott and Lara Prince who collected a score of 2380 to place first overall in the six hour event. Alistair Cory-Wright made a commendable effort scoring 1280 points in the three hour event. Full results are on the event website.

The Rogaine was a fundraiser for a new and re-imagined youth adventure racing team, led by local rogainer Tim Farrant.  The team are training hard and hoping that their navigation skills will keep them in good stead for the upcoming GODZone Adventure Race to be held near Kaikoura in March. 

And a loud shout out from Tim:
 “A big thank-you to all the competitors for taking part and to Bivouac Outdoor for their fantastic support for this event. Stay tuned for updates as we are looking to run another event this summer”.

Welly Afterwork, Tuesday 15th October
          The penultimate event in the 2013 Welly Afterwork Series was run on Tuesday 15th October. There was a satisfying turnout of 108 runners and walkers despite the poor weather earlier in the day and the cool temperatures on the night. A report by organiser Jo McKenzie and the results may be found at http://www.mapsport.co.nz/wellyrog/awrog13res.html. The final event in this year’s series is on Tuesday, 19th November.


14th World Rogaining Championships – tips for gaining entry
Alice Springs, Central Australia, 23  & 24 July, 2016
         The year 2016 is a long way off, but since the 14th World Champs is relatively close to home there should be keen interest from Kiwis. You might imagine that wandering in a “desert” for 24hrs is not your thing. However, be assured that this area is far from being a desert and is spectacular rogaining country. The Aussie organisers have published a few tips for Australian and New Zealand rogainers wanting to maximise their opportunity for gaining entry. Take a look at the tips and note that the next Australasian Champs is in the old goldfields area of Castlemaine in Victoria, weekend 11/12 October, 2014.
