Events in March and April

Two Afterwork Series
      The Wednesday evening afterwork New Plymouth Urban Series and Summer Nav Series continue in Taranaki and Bay of Plenty respectively. The New Plymouth series organised by O Taranaki continues to 12th March while the 60min Summer Nav events organised by Orienteering BOP finish 26th March. See the maptalk calendar for complete information on each event.


Suburbs to Surf, Dunedin, Sunday 2nd March
            The Suburbs to Surf organised by Tim Jowett in Dunedin takes place this coming Sunday.  There will be 6hr & 3hr foot and 3hr MTB options on the rural-urban fringe of the Otago Peninsula with about a third of the map in a suburban area. This is a "shoe-string" rogaine so for controls competitors will write down answers related to features at each control site visited. The event base is the Guthrie Pavilion, Bayfield High School, 2 Shore Street, Andersons Bay. For more information see the information sheet on the NZRA website. Contact is Tim Jowett, Jowti231@gmail.com, ph. 027 656 7251. Enter and pay on the day.


Equestrian and mini-Foot Rogaines, Whitecliffs, Canterbury, Sunday 9th March
         The Equestrian event for teams of 2 to 4 on horseback will be 2.5 hours with registration from noon. Pre-entry is necessary for the equestrian rogaine, and further information and an entry form are available at http://www.ecess.org/fundraiser.html. Also check out the flyer.
         The mini-Foot rogaine for teams or individuals will be 90 minutes in duration and registration will be open from 9am for a 10am start. Enter on the day, but an email of number entering to Stuart Payne at oldhat@actrix.co.nz will guarantee map(s). Organisers: Stuart Payne & Carol Fowler for Eyre Community Environmental Safety Society.


Bouldergaine, Castle Hill, Saturday 15th March - a world-first concept
       Run by the New Zealand Alpine Club in association with PAPO the Bouldergaine is a hybrid of Rogaining and Bouldering. It combines Rogaining with rock climbing on boulders to a safe height without the need for equipment. Read the Alpine Club press release. Full details of the 3hr event can be found at http://www.nbs.org.nz.

 
Urban Fundraiser Rogaine, Massey Campus, Saturday 22nd March
           This 90min Urban rogaine is a fundraiser for the Massey Child Care Centre and is based at the Massey campus. It is child/buggy friendly but also caters for those who wish for a fast blowout. The event will be followed by a sausage sizzle and spot prizes. More detail will be in the NZRA calendar soon.


R&R Sport Wicked Autumn Series, Christchurch, Wednesday 26th March & 9th April
           This year there will be two afterwork 2hr Rogaines in the Port Hills on Wednesday evenings. The first event is on 26th March, and the second on 9th April, and both will include a 1hr Family event. Keep your eye on the Wicked Facebook page for further information.


Geraldine Rogaine Autumn Muster (GRAM), Geraldine region, Sunday 30th March
        Organised by the Geraldine High School PTA in the South Canterbury foothills at or near the location of the 2013 Heights of Winter event. There will be two options, 3 and 4½ hours, with categories for school children and adults. Planner is Mike Harding & controller is Stuart Payne. For more information and entry see the Geraldine High School website. Contact is mikeharding@ihug.co.nz.


Mt Outdoors Lindis Rogaine, Lindis Valley, Saturday 26th April
          The Highland Events (Ed Stevens and Terry Davis) autumn Rogaine will be based at the Shirlmar Station woolshed, Goodger Road in the Lindis Valley. The course will also take in the Morven Hills station and possibly Geordie Hills.  The terrain on these fine wool sheep stations is similar to last autumn’s Timburn Rogaine and will be fantastic for beginners and elites alike. There is a very good track system through the properties for the Mountain Bike event too. Entries for the 12hr, 6hr and family 3hr events, and a 6hr MTB option are open on the Highland Events website.

Waitangi Day Rogaine, Christchurch Port Hills
         There was an impressive turnout of Canterbury Rogainers at Taylor's Mistake on Waitangi Day for a second instalment of the current Canterbury Rogaine Series, organised by local adventure racing team Next Generation.  The more than 200 participants taking part in the 3-hr and 90-min events was well over four times the number of online entries causing slight havoc with map and clue-sheet supplies. In superb weather teams enjoyed great conditions for navigating the challenging open country with intricate rocky outcrops and a complex trail network. The exposed coastal terrain was put to use with some control points located on the rocky shoreline. Godley Head’s historic military area concealed one clue in an underground bunker.

The atmosphere of the late-afternoon finish was fantastic with the BBQ on full blast feeding the crowds of happy rogainers, as well as other Cantabrians enjoying New Zealand’s national day at the beach.  With plenty of checkpoints on offer, no team managed to clear the course but local rogainer Robert Jarvis came close with the top overall score of 1790 in Open Men, closely followed by Tim & Sarah Wright in the Open Mixed with a score of 1740 points.  Anna Lindgren & Sylvia Maclaren took the Open Women’s title, while Dave Buter and Levi Herbet were top in the Junior division, and Martin & Rupert Shepherd claimed the Family title. For full results see the Canterbury Series website http://canterburyrogaine.com/.

Thank you to all who took part including the many first-time family groups in the 3hr event.  Thank you also to webmaster extraordinaire Katie Bolt, and Bivouac Outdoor for their fantastic support.  See you all next time!

Championships coming up

12th World Rogaining Championships
Black Hills, South Dakota, 16th - 17th August 2014
           There are many places still available. The current total of 409 entrants is well short of the 1000 limit. To date 31 Kiwis have registered their interest but we expect a few more once they get themselves sorted. The event website and blog is at http://peakassurance.net/. See the list of entrants.


2nd Australasian Championships (Goldfields Bonanza)
Victoria, Sat-Sun 11th - 12th October 2014
           Set in an old goldfields area in the Castlemaine region, the terrain is a complex and challenging mixture of spurs and gullies only 1.5hrs drive from Melbourne. There will be a 6hr event as well as the championship 24hr Rogaine. The website is at http://vra.rogaine.asn.au/arc14/.


New Zealand Championships – new date and location
Nevis Valley, Otago, Sat-Sun 6th - 7th December 2014
          As noted in the last newsletter we were looking forward to returning to the Pisa Range for the New Zealand Champs this year. However, circumstances beyond our control have led to a date and location change with the event now based in the lower Nevis Valley with its unique landscapes and historic remnants of the gold mining era. This interesting valley is quite remote but is readily accessible in summer; Google “Nevis Valley” for a peek at what the area offers. The event is scheduled for the weekend of 6th & 7th December. Chief organisers are the experienced Highland Events team of Ed Stevens and Terry Davis who are well known for their twice yearly Wanaka Rogaines. Pencil this new date in your calendars and look for more information on the NZRA website further down the track.
