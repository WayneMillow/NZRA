Moonlight on Mahinerangi 24hr & 7hr Rogaines
Otago, 24th & 25th November

Entries are OPEN – get your entry in now

The Moonlight on Mahinerangi classic 24hr and 7hr rogaines will be run in Otago within a 35min drive of Dunedin airport! See the event website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz/nzchamps2012/, for details and online entry. Transport is available from Dunedin city and Dunedin airport.

Even though the 24hr Moonlight on Mahinerangi is officially the 2012 New Zealand Championship remember it caters for all tastes from top-class super-fit competitors to the strictly social rogainers who will return to the hash house for food (supplied), socialising, rest, and sleep before heading out again. An advertising poster is available (http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/Events/NZRA_Champs_Mahinerangi.pdf) – please download and distribute this poster widely to promote the event.


Other events coming up

Catchpool Rogaine, Wellington, Sunday 28th October
           A 6hr event in the Rimutaka Forest Park starting from the car park at the bottom of the Orongorongo and Five Mile Tracks. See the Maptalk website, http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine, for more details. Entries close 25th October.

Great Green Meadowbank Challenge, East Auckland, Saturday 10th November
           The Great Green Meadowbank 2hr Rogaine is being organised by the Year 5 class at Meadowbank School as part of their curriculum, with guidance from Shaun Collins of Lactic Turkey Events. In a variation from the standard question and answer sheets used to verify checkpoint visits, each site is tagged with a QR code so that clues may be downloaded by smartphone. In addition, bonus points can be earned from a variety of environmental challenges scattered around the course. The children invite anybody and everybody to join in the fun on Saturday 10th November. There is more information on the event website. Entries close 6th November.

R&R Sport Wicked Rogaine Spring Series, Port Hills, Christchurch
           This Spring series will be run over the last three weeks of November starting on Wednesday the 14th November at 6pm. Due to the earthquakes large expanses of the Port Hills are not available to operate a 3hr event so this year there will be a series of three 2hr events. Registration and maps will be available from 5pm. Consult the Wicked Facebook page for further information including the location of each event.

10th World Rogaining Championships, Czech Republic

697 participants in 333 teams from 28 countries took part in the 24hr World Championships at the end of August in the Ore Mountains, north-west Czech Republic. An unusual feature of the event was that part of the course ventured into Germany so that passports had to be carried.

The course was forest-based with little open country. Conditions in large tracts of the forest were unpredictable with messy undergrowth, fallen and felled logs and some significant bogs. Other forest was open but the unpredictability encouraged most people to follow the many accurately marked roads and tracks.

The top placings were predominately recorded by European competitors. NZ ranked 7th (Australia 4th) on the medal table with silver medals to Matt Bixley/Robert Jarvis (2nd MV & 8th overall) and Kathrin Mueller/Val Rogers (2nd WSV). Matt and Robert were the second non-European team to finish being three places behind a South African team. They were beaten into 2nd place in MV by only 40 points (in 3200).

Other notable NZ performances were:
  • Jana Nyklova – 18th overall and 4th in XO. Jana has been resident in Christchurch for a number of years but competed for her home country, Czech Republic, in WRC.
  • Tim Farrant and Jeremy Wilson – 19th overall
  • Tim Sikma – 20th overall. Tim is a Kiwi currently resident in Western Australia. He was partnered with Mark Imbert of Australia.
The next Kiwi was Vivienne Prince filling 60th place (7th XV) with Richard Robinson (Australia). For full results see the website http://www.rogaining.cz/wrc2012/en/ . The next World Champs is in Russia 27th - 28th July, 2013.
