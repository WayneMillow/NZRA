Mt Outdoors Wanaka Rogaine

See the Highland Events website, http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/rogaine, for detailed information and online entry. Recent Event Results

11th World Rogaining Championships, Russia, 26th - 27th July
            Vivienne Prince, partnered with Richard Robinson (Australia), was the sole New Zealand representative at the World Champs in Russia. With 2090pts they finished 4th XSV and 102nd overall, 140pts behind the winning XSV team.  Latvian teams filled the top three XSV places.

Vivienne relates her experiences in a report which will be on the NZRA website soon. Viv and Richard may be disappointed with their “24 hours of aimless wanderings in the swamp” but they are credited with being 16th (amongst 314 teams) in the most efficient planners stakes.

For full results see the event website at http://wrc2013.com/ and take a look at the map (4.8MB), notable for vast expanses of blue – no chance of keeping dry feet there.

The winning Eensaar brothers from Estonia reflect on their performance in their blog at http://tpmv.blogspot.com.au/2013/08/rogaining-world-championships-2013-alol.html.


Hamels Haunts III, Dunedin, Sunday July 28th
          Matt Bixley’s minimalist training rogaine based at the Bullring was somewhat swamped with twice as many as expected turning up. Matt coped; although the soup was a trifle watery. So popular was the event that, as noted in future events above, a second training run is scheduled for 8th September. See http://runlongergeek.wordpress.com/2013/08/02/hamels-haunts-iii-results/ for results and report.

Equestrian Rogaine 2013, Sunday 4th August
           The second equestrian rogaine, organised by Stuart Payne and Carol Fowler in support of Darfield High School’s rural college, attracted 88 riders and their steeds. This year the event shared the Burwood Forest with runners, walkers, and mountain bikers and the horses were therefore restricted to dedicated horse trails. To make the most of the course a 2hr foot rogaine was held as well. See http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/Results/BottleLakeEquestrianResults.pdf for the organisers’ comments and results.

Welly Afterwork, Thursday 15th August
          The 5th event in the 2013 Welly Afterwork Series was run on Thursday 15th August. 60 runners and walkers were attracted to this event in some typically tough Wellington terrain. See the report by organiser Kelvin Thiele and the results at http://www.mapsport.co.nz/wellyrog/awrog13res.html.



Next WRC – registration opens in October
            The 12th World Rogaining Championships will be in the Black Hills of South Dakota, 16th & 17th August, 2014. The event centre is 50 minutes west of Rapid City. As usual there will be places reserved for top performers in previous WRC, regional championships and national championships. However, there are a total of 1000 places available so many non-prequalifying people should get in. Registration is scheduled to open in October. We have no way of knowing whether the number of places will be exceeded but those registering in the first 14 days have an improved chance of being accepted. So, if you wish to compete in the Black Hills, get sorted and ready to register. See the note on registration on the NZRA website. Also keep up to date via the event website, http://peakassurance.net/, and the event blog, http://peakassurance.net/Blog/Blog.html.

           Looking a long way ahead, but close to home, the 2016 World Championships will be near Alice Springs in Central Australia. The Australian championships were run there in 2007. It is a fascinating place to visit and great rogaining terrain. (The intervening 2015 WRC is in Northern Finland, above the Arctic Circle).


No NZ Championships this year
           Just repeating in case you missed it - there will be no NZ Champs this year. Having investigated all options we are unable to offer a viable event in 2013. However we are working towards a New Zealand Championship event for 2014 and beyond. Stay tuned for future announcements.
