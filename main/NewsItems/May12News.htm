Events coming up

Hamel’s Haunts, Dunedin, Sunday 17th June, 3hr and 6hr options
            Matthew Bixley follows his 2011 Hamel’s Haunts with another this year. It is two weeks prior to Heights of Winter so you can brush up on navigation for that event. There will be a mixture of easy controls on marked and unmarked tracks with additional more difficult controls potentially requiring scrub and bush bashing. Expect bush, tussocks, pine forest, some gorse, gravel, mud, and swamp. Most of the navigation is pitched at entry level with a couple of sections requiring more care. Should fog or mist roll in some of the navigation will be at the more experienced end of the scale. For details see http://runlongergeek.wordpress.com/rogaine/, email matt.bixley@agresearch.co.nz, or phone Matt on 021 046 9255.

Heights of Winter, North Canterbury, Saturday 30th June, 6hr and 12hr options
               The date of the annual Heights of Winter coincides with a full moon so prepare yourselves for some mid-winter fun. For event information and on-line entries see the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz. Planning is well under way. The terrain is varied ranging from gentle hills to steep and rugged hill-country with an elevation range of 500m. Vegetation is a mix of clean open grassland, scattered scrub, open native forest, and a few plantations. Enter now.

Auckland Bivouac Rogaine Series, each Sunday in May
             Organised by North West Orienteering, Auckland Orienteering, and Counties-Manukau Orienteering Clubs, this series of Rogaines of 90 minute and 3 hour durations are at a different location each Sunday in May. The last event in the series is in the Waiuku Forest next Sunday, 27th May. Consult the website, http://www.rogaineseries.co.nz/ for complete information and entry.

Wellington Afterwork Series, April to November
              This is a series of 3hr afterwork Rogaines at monthly intervals from April to November in the Wellington area and under the auspices of O Hutt Valley. Planners for the next event on 6th June are Debbie Mansfield and Julie Gordon. See the Maptalk calendar, http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine, for details.

Navquest Series, Sunday 6th May through Sunday 24th June
              The NavQuest is a series of four 3hr rogaines aimed at secondary school students, their families and teachers. The first event was run on the 6th May and the second is scheduled for next Sunday 27th May.

Members of the public are more than welcome to join in by entering in the open category. Go to http://www.opc.org.nz for more information and to register a team online. The NavQuest is organised as a partnership between The Sir Edmund Hillary Outdoor Pursuits Centre of New Zealand (OPC) and North Island orienteering clubs in Rotorua, Auckland, Wellington and Taupo. Coordinating organiser is Darren Ashmore.

Recent Events

T’Walk (24hr walk), Canterbury foothills, Sat/Sun 5/6 May
              The 2012 edition of this iconic event was based at Glenthorne Station on the shores of Lake Coleridge in the Canterbury foothills. Over 200 people enjoyed a fine, mostly moonlit night except for a little fog at some of the higher locations.

The huge winning team comprised Chris Forne, Emily Wall, Tim Farrant, Michael Smithson, Katie Bolt, Robert Monro, Jana Nyklova, JJ Wilson, Emily Wilson, and Rhys John. Perhaps you will recognise most of those names. With so many experts in the team it is a wonder that they agreed sufficiently to even get under way. The winning student team of Edward Radcliffe, Hamish Laing, Owain John, and Alexander Telfar showed good form in filling second place – well done. A strong team from Wellington finished in 4th place.

For the results, maps and organiser’s thoughts see http://www.cutc.org.nz/twalk/


Highland Events Autumn Rogaine, Lowburn Hall, Cromwell, Saturday 12th May
            The Highland Events 12hr and 6hr rogaines saw 45 and 76 people, respectively, running around in the Lowburn area of historic central Otago. In Matt Bixley’s words “Lots of cool historical places from old gold mining days, beautiful gorges, torrs, historic and active water-races, stunning views, interesting variations of sidling and climbs with both marked and unmarked tracks”.
 
First place in the 12hr event went to Greig Hamilton and Tim Farrant while the 6hr winners were Charles and Budd Law. See the website, http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/rogaine, for full results and the map. For the winning routes of both the 12hr and the 6hr events follow the “Rogaine Blog” link on the website.


2012 Australian Rogaine Championships, Flinders Ranges, Sat/Sun 5/6 May
               The Australian champs in the Flinders Ranges of South Australia lived up to expectations with a challenging course through gorges, sheer drop offs, stands of native pine, open mallee scrub, the odd gum tree and ever-present, mainly red, rocks. It was a splendid physical and navigation challenge made more so by 12hrs of darkness. There were many bruised feet and very tired people at the finish.

Six NZ teams, with the following placings, were among the 111 teams that completed the course:
    1. Pete Squires, Anne Kennedy, Bill Kennedy – 1800 pts, 1st XSV, 2nd XV, 21st overall
    2. Julie Gordon, Debbie Mansfield – 3rd WV, 1540pts, 35th overall
    3. Nick Collins, Annie Sanderson – 1420pts, 3rd XSV and 40th overall
    4. Mark Struthers, Annette Windross, Dennis Litt, Henry Beex – 1400pts, 41st overall
    5. Andrew McGowan, Martin Etherington – 1260pts, 49th overall
    6. Simon Rea, Jeff Williams – 1040pts, 61st overall
See the website: http://rogaine.asn.au/arc2012 for full results including individual courses plotted on Google Earth.

The event was superbly organised by the South Australian Rogaining Association. The next Australian Championships is a 40 minute drive from Cairns in North Queensland on 22nd - 23rd June 2013.


Bits and Pieces
  1. For those going to the WRC, Bulletin #2 has been published on the WRC website, http://www.rogaining.cz/wrc2012/en/.
  2. 2012 New Zealand Championships, Otago, weekend 24th - 25th November
               Remember that, in a break from tradition, the 2012 New Zealand Championships has been moved from Autumn to late Spring. It will be held in Otago within 100km of Dunedin on the weekend of 24th - 25th November. Organiser-in-chief is Bob Cunninghame. More detail will be available on the NZRA website soon.

Besides the 24hr championship event there will be a shorter option, duration yet to be decided, for those who find 24hrs on the hoof rather daunting. But as President Pete Squires said when speaking about the 24hr event in his last annual report:
“I would encourage teams to consider splitting their time in two, and having a few hours of rest at night. This is widely done in overseas rogaines and brings a sense of camaraderie to the hash-house in the evening”.
Rogaining is not ALL about competition. There is a social aspect enhanced by mutually experiencing the outdoor challenge.

We hope to see you all at a Rogaine soon.
