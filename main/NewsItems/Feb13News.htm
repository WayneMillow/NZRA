From our Secretary - The Association Committee on the spot.
              Last November we held our annual national meeting, with two representatives from the NZ Orienteering Federation. Central on the agenda of this meeting, and our regular meeting on 4th February, was how to deliver the annual 24 hour NZ Championship event, which we try to alternate between the North and South Islands. Until recently we’ve shared the load, alternating with orienteering interests. 24 hour events are demanding to stage and the orienteering groups have understandingly had to pull the plug, though some may still be able to help on a partnership basis. Our Association is likely to struggle as well, particularly to stage events in the North Island. In fact we are still looking for a North Island venue and organisers for THIS YEAR. Can YOU help?

Other big one for us is building the people organisational resource base, both locally, regionally and nationally, and particularly at the younger end of our demographic. The willingness and creativity of independent individuals and small teams to organise events in many localities is a great strength and diagnostic feature of rogaining in New Zealand, but it can make it really hard when seeking a national perspective, such as when designing championship events.  More confident use of social media should be part of our mix
 
Read more in the minutes from the November and February meetings.  We welcome your innovative and constructive views on how we might do better in these areas. How can you help? 
 
Grant Hunter, Secretary NZRA


Annual General Meeting, 25th February, Christchurch

The NZRA AGM will be held Monday 25th February, 7.30 pm at 6 Harrods Court, Christchurch. The Agenda will include:
  1. Minutes of the 2012 AGM and matters arising
  2. President’s Annual Report
  3. Financial report
  4. Election of officers
  5. Other business
Additional agenda items and nominations for the positions of President, Treasurer, Secretary, and committee members should be lodged with the Secretary (g.hunter@clear.net.nz) at least two days before the meeting.

For those living outside of Christchurch and having a compelling interest in attending this meeting, NZRA will consider helping with travel costs. Alternatively we may be able to arrange an electronic link. Please raise this with the Secretary, g.hunter@clear.net.nz.

The minutes of the 2012 AGM are available at http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/NewsItems/AGM2012_minutes.pdf


Events coming up

Kings Heist Rogaine, Marlborough, Saturday 23rd February
            The Marlborough Orienteering Club invites you to enter the Kings Heist Rogaine and strategise your way around some iconic rolling Marlborough farmland. See the flyer at http://rogaine.org.nz/main/Events/KingsHeist.pdf for more information. Send an email to maps@moc.kiwi.nz for an entry form, or phone Peter Hamill on 021 383 085 (Entries close 8:00am Thursday 21 Feb 2013).

Taranaki Secondary Schools Rogaine, Wednesday 6th March
            Whilst open only to Taranaki students it is worth noting the success of this event and we look forward to it continuing to attract strong competition. May it inspire other regions to consider such a fixture. See the report by Nick Collins on last year’s 8th Taranaki Secondary School Championships event supported by NZRA, and for which 571 students turned out in a “deluge of biblical proportions”.

R&R Sport Wicked Rogaine Autumn Series, Port Hills, Christchurch
           The mid-week Autumn series of three events will be run on successive Wednesdays beginning 3rd April. Critical localised areas of the Port Hills are still not available due to the earthquakes so the rogaines will be limited to 2hr events again. Registration and maps are expected to be available from 5pm with a mass start at 6pm. Consult the Wicked Facebook page for further information including the location of each event.

Championship News

Australasian Championships, North Queensland, Sat-Sun 22-23 June 2013

As foreshadowed in the last NZRA newsletter, Rogaining North Queensland will host the 1st Australasian Rogaine Championships, Sat-Sun 22 & 23 June 2013, on the Atherton Tableland, a 40 minute drive from Cairns International airport. On-line registration is now open on the event website http://adventuresportnq.info/arc2013 where further information is available.


11th World Rogaining Championships, Russia, 26th - 27th July 2013
                 The 11th WRC will be based in Alol village in the Pustoshka district of the Pskov region, 500km west of Moscow and 400km south of St. Petersburg. The event website opened on 5th December and 510 people have registered to date. The ceiling on numbers is 1200 people so there are many places still available. If you need an excuse to visit Russia then this WRC could be it.

Vivienne Prince, in a team with Richard Robinson (Australia), is the only New Zealand rogainer registered so far. Where is everybody? Note that a new UltraVet age group (65 and over) has been introduced.
