R&R Sport Taylors Mistake Rogaine Series, Christchurch, weekly from 1st April
=============================================================================
      The R & R Sport Taylors Mistake Series of 3hr afterwork rogaines initiated by
Jamie Stewart and Chris Forne starts in two weeks time. This year Nora Audra and Ian
Edmond are the organisers. There will be three events based at Sign of the Bellbird, Mt
Pleasant, and Taylors Mistake in successive weeks. For more information visit:
http://www.madeventz.co.nz/tmrogaine. Pre-enter to be guaranteed a map.


Other upcoming events
=====================

(1)   This week the Taranaki Secondary School Rogaine Championships will be held in New
Plymouth. This is a 3hr Rogaine for Secondary Schools only. Start is at 6pm on
Wednesday. This is an event unique to Taranaki. Perhaps other regions should be
encouraged to run a similar Rogaine. See the O Taranaki website,
http://www.orienteeringtaranaki.org.nz/ , for more information.


(2) Navigation Training Course, Wellington, from 19th March.
      Two 2hr evening sessions (19th and 26th March) and a 6hr practical session (4th
April) organised by Mike Sheridan. See the maptalk calendar,
http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine , for details.


(3) Afterwork Series, Wellington, beginning 7th April
      A series of afterwork 3hr events at intervals of 3 or 4 weeks in the Wellington
area beginning 7th April and running through to 16th July. See the maptalk calendar,
http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine , for details.


(4) T'Walk 2009, Canterbury, weekend 2nd/3rd May
      This will be the 43rd running of this unique 24 hour event, organised by the
University of Canterbury Tramping Club, the pioneer of long distance foot navigation
events in NZ. Visit the T'Walk website, http://www.cutc.org.nz/twalk/ , sometime late
March for detailed information.


(5) Wellington Metlink City Safari, Sunday 17th May
      In the City Safari you can use public transport (bus, train, cablecar, ferry) in
addition to your legs. For more information see the event website
http://www.citysafari.org.nz .




New Zealand Rogaine Championships
=================================
       The 24 hour NZ Champs were held last weekend on a course in East Otago. There was
also an 8hr non-championship rogaine for those who did not wish to do the full event.

The competiton was close with the team of Phil Wood and Ross Rotherham coming in first
by the narrowest of margins (10 points) from the de Monchy brothers, Pim and Dennis.
Third was Greig Hamilton and Tim Sikma only 40 points further back. For full results
including map, winners' route, pictures, and organisers' reflections see the NZRA
website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz . The inaugural University Challenge was won by
Otago.
