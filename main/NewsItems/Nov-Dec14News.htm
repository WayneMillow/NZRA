The Big Trig 2014: Wedgery Rogaine
Wellington, Saturday 29th November, 3, 6 or 9hr options

Normal entries close 19th November (tomorrow)
Late entries to event day at organiser’s discretion
Based at Whareroa Farm, just north of Paekakariki on SH1 on the fringe of the Akatarawas there will be some easy areas in Queen Elizabeth Park ideal for family groups, but the truly rugged Rogainers will be able to sample the native bush of the Akatarawas. Those willing to climb a few hills will be rewarded with fantastic views over Cook Strait. The event is organised by O Hutt Valley with Gordon Balfour as the chief planner. For further information see the event website.



14th New Zealand Championships
Nevis Valley, Otago, 6th-7th December

Regular entries close next Monday 24th November
Final cut-off with late fee 10pm Monday 1st December
To be run on the Ben Nevis and Craig-Roy Stations in the remote Nevis Valley, south-west of Cromwell. For event details, course notes, pictures, and online entry see the Highland Events website.
This is the BIG one of the year – 24hrs of fun – don’t miss it
Remember you don’t have to be highly competitive and on the move for the whole 24hrs. Teams can challenge themselves as much or as little as they are comfortable with - you can return to base at any time for rest, food, and maybe sleep. There will be continuous food provided from 8pm to 8am.

To meet all expectations and abilities there will be a 12hr, 6hr, and 3hr Family events in addition to the Championship.

Recent Result

Mt Evans Rogaine, Banks Peninsula, Sunday 9th November
                A warm Canterbury day saw 96 people in the 6hr event and 88 people in the 3hr event at the Mt Evans rogaine organised by Alastair McDowell to raise funds for the Apollo’s Arrows adventure racing team’s entry into the 2015 Godzone Adventure race. This youthful team currently consists of Alastair McDowell, Isla Smith, Jordan Pearson, and Ben Gibson.

After running around the steepish slopes of Mt Evans the dehydrated and tired participants were glad to indulge in the cold drinks at the finish, and to sink their teeth into what the BBQ provided. For results and a full report see the Canterbury Series website. Look out for a follow-up event being planned by Apollo’s Arrows for later in the summer.





13th World Rogaining Championships, 22nd/23rd August, 2015
This will be the first WRC to be run in the Arctic; in the Saariselkä region of Lapland, about 200km north of the polar circle. While we associate perpetual daylight with that region continuous daylight ceases at the WRC2015 location about 21st July. By 22nd August the sun sets at 9.30pm and rises about 5am so, on that basis, there will be roughly 7½ hrs of darkness. However, twilight at those latitudes lingers on for about 1½ hrs so there will be only 4½ hrs of real dark. The sun will not rise far above the horizon even at midday.
The exact details of the registration process have yet to be published but registration begins 1st December. Entries are limited to 1000 people. Whether this limit will need to be invoked is a matter for conjecture but is likely given the event is in Europe. See the registration advice on the NZRA website.
Bulletin 1 and course setter notes, including advice on weather, terrain, and map, are available on the WRC2015 website. Keep up to date via the event Facebook and Twitter.
