Events coming up

Ayrdale Attack and Amble, Springfield, Canterbury, Sunday 19th August
           Organisers Carol Fowler & Stuart Payne promise an adventure to mark the imminent arrival of Spring. The event is afflicted with an identity crisis this year but it seems the Annavale Attack (4hr) & Amble (3hr) has morphed into the Ayrdale A & A even though administration is at the Annavale woolshed. Confused? For the real oil, information, and an entry form see the Darfield High School website, http://www.darfield.school.nz/rural. Get your entry in pronto – closing date is Wednesday 15th August.


Wandering Kiwi Rogaine, Ohope, Saturday 8th September, 6hr & 3hr
      This bush reserve, beach and urban based rogaine, is a fundraiser for the Whakatane Kiwi Trust, (http://www.whakatanekiwi.org.nz). 11am and 2pm starts, $5 students, $10 for adults, pre-entry necessary. The event centre is at Mahy Reserve, Ohope, just over the hill from Whakatane. All ages welcome. Contact is Alister Coulter, alister64@hotmail.co.nz


Mt Outdoors Wanaka Rogaine, Central Otago, Saturday 15th September
            Ed Stevens reports “We can now confirm that the Highland Events rogaine set down for 15 September is all go. HQ is going to be the Tarras Hall. The course will be ideal for both beginners and experienced alike, ranging from easy flat to some nice manageable hill country on four iconic Central Otago fine wool properties. This will be Rogaine heaven; every time I drive through the Lindis Valley I dream of holding a Rogaine over this land, so here we go folks, this will be amazing”. 

There will be 6hr and 12hr events.  See the website http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/rogaine for more information, online entry and eventually some pictures. Entries finally close Thursday 13th September but a late fee kicks in Monday 10th September. Any queries call Ed 027 4451091 or Terry Davis 021 12846844


Lactic Turkey Riverhead Rogaine, Northwest Auckland, Saturday 22nd September
            Shaun Collins and team are organising the Riverhead Forest rogaine again this year with 1½hr & 4hr options. See the Lactic Turkey Events website, http://www.lacticturkey.co.nz/Rogaines.htm  for information and to enter online.


Victorian Championships weekend 22nd - 23rd September
           The Victorian Rogaining Association welcomes any Kiwis who would like to join in their state championships on the Strathbogie granite plateau. The plateau was the birthplace of Rogaining in 1976 and is an easy 2 hour drive from Melbourne. For information see the Victoria website, http://vra.rogaine.asn.au/


Moonlight on Mahinerangi, 24hr and 7hr, Otago, weekend 24th - 25th November
         Prepare yourselves for this classic event in Otago within a 35min drive of Dunedin airport! Planning for the ONLY annual 24hr rogaine in New Zealand is well under way with Bob Cunninghame as organiser-in-chief. There is a 7hr option as well. See the event website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz/nzchamps2012/, for details.

Even though the Rogaine is billed as a “championship” remember it caters for all tastes from top-class super-fit competitors to the strictly social rogainers who will return to the hash house for food (supplied), socialising, rest, and sleep before heading out again. An advertising poster is available (http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/Events/NZRA_Champs_Mahinerangi.pdf) – please download and distribute widely to promote the event.

Recent Events

Karekare 12 Hour Rogaine, Waitakere Ranges, Auckland
         The upside-down Karekare Rogaine (midnight Friday to noon Saturday) attracted 41 enthusiastic entrants in 15 teams. Leighton Watson set an incredible course incorporating the best of the Southern Waitakere Ranges – Piha’s roaring surf, Lion Rock, Kitekite falls; coastal tunnels, dunes, and high cliff-top tracks; caves, trig stations, and swamps; and even the highest point in the ranges – Te Toiokawharu. And the state of the tide introduced an additional variable.

Alastair McDowell reports “The event was a great success again - it was awesome to see so many shattered teams walk in after a night's adventure!” See Alastair’s full account with photos and map at http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/Results/Karekare%2012%20Hour%20Rogaine%20REPORT.pdf
