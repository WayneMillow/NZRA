Christmas Rogaine, Port Hills, Christchurch - Wednesday, 16th December
======================================================================

    Whoopeee! Come frolic on The Port Hills for an evening! HOHOHO!!! 

The annual 3hr afterwork Christmas Rogaine on the Port Hills will be run on Wednesday 16th 
December. This fun event will once again be organised by Tim Farrant and friends. Registration and 
maps will be available from 5:30pm with a 6:30pm start from Victoria Park car park. For an entry 
form and further information see the event website http://www.cutc.org.nz/twilight_rogaine/. 
Pre-entry by 14th December earns you a $5 discount.


2010 Australian Championships, Snowy Mountains, 27-28 March 2010
================================================================
       The 2010 Australian Rogaining Championships will be hosted by the ACT Rogaining Association 
in the magnificent Snowy Mountains. The course will be set by Australian rogaining champions, David 
Baldwin and Julie Quinn, and will provide excellent navigational challenges in a beautiful 
environment. Terrain will vary from alpine ash forests, through snow gum woodlands and granite 
boulder fields, to open plains. Autumn in Kosciuszko typically provides superb clear days with cool 
evenings. The Hash House will offer ideal camping and there is caravan park and lodge accommodation 
within 5 minutes drive.

Numbers are limited to 550 people. There are already 117 entries and 6 teams of Kiwis. This is 
another great opportunity for people to build their 24hr experience in preparation for the World 
Champs in New Zealand in November.


2010 New Zealand Championships, Wellington, 17-18 April 2010
=============================================================
       This is yet another opportunity to enhance your 24hr Rogaine experience in preparation for 
the World Champs next November - a great chance to optimise your effort over 24hrs, test your 
concentration, and learn the idiosyncrasies of your team mates under the stress of a 24hr Rogaine.

The "Moonshine24" New Zealand Championships, will be hosted by Orienteering Hutt Valley in the 
Akatarawa Ranges, near Wellington. Chief organiser is Mike Sheridan so a challenging event is 
assured. 

Entry forms are now available. See the event website, http://www.moonshine24.co.nz/ , for in-depth 
information, pictures, blog, and entry form. There will be 3hr, 6hr and 12hr non-championship 
events in addition to the 24hr championship.


2010 Icebug24, Sweden, 29th - 31st July
========================================
      We have heard from Aaron Prince that the annual Icebug24, 
http://www.icebug24.com/index.php?language=english , is based in the village of Hemavan in the 
north of Sweden. If anybody happens to be in the Europe in late July this is a great event to try. 
A novelty is that it is so far north that there is little or no darkness, and what there is will be 
more twilight than dark.

It is possible to fly to the village from Stockholm and once there everything is within walking 
distance, therefore no need for rental cars. As Hemavan is a ski village and this is the off season 
accommodation is relatively cheap. There is even prize money, 24000 SEK. It is a really scenic area 
in the mountains and you are very likely to meet some reindeer out on the course. The dates tie in 
well with the world MTBO champs in Portugal.


9th World Championships 2010, Cheviot, New Zealand, November
============================================================
     Phase 1 of the entry process, which gives priority to teams that have qualified via their 
performance in prior championship rogaines, finishes in 10 days. Teams from under-represented 
countries, and in under-represented age/gender categories, are invited to confirm their entry in 
phase 2 which completes at the end of January. Invitations will then be sent to other people who 
have registered up to a cap of 550.

As of today 623 people have registered, 190 of those have prequalified, and there are 65 candidates 
for entry via the under-representation criteria. There is a good selection of people from outside 
Australasia. See the List of Entrants on the website, http://wrc2010.org.nz/

Registrations are still being taken. There are bound to be withdrawals (but we have no idea how 
many) so don't be put off by the entry cap.
