Kiwis win Australasian Championships

Congratulations to Phil Wood and Robert Jarvis who recorded placings of 1st overall, 1st MO, and 1st MV with 3140pts in the Australasian Championships run near Cairns in Far North Queensland weekend of 22nd/23rd June. Kiwis have competed with some distinction in previous Australian Championships but this was the first official Australasian event for Australia and New Zealand combined. Have a read of Phil’s account of their experiences.

The organising team led by Mike Coleman put on a fantastic event with great terrain, an excellent and challenging map, and plenty of nutritious food both at the Hash House and at the two very welcome All Night Cafes out in the field. Take a look at the results, map, and pictures.


Additional Kiwi podium placings were:
  • Viv Prince with Richard Robinson (Australia), 2720pts, 6th overall, 1st XSV, 2nd XV, 3rd XO
  • Emma and Guy de Lacey, 2250pts, 19th overall, 2nd XSV, 3rd XV
A total of 16 NZ Rogainers competed and the remaining NZ placings were:
  • Mark Struthers, Annette Windross, Dennis Litt, Henry Beex, 1880pts, 27th overall
  • Trevor Merrifield, Andrew McGowan, 1710pts, 34th overall
  • Pete Squires, Anne & Bill Kennedy, 1590pts, 37th overall. First, by default, in the XUV (65+) category introduced this year. No other UV teams fronted.
  • Annie Sanderson, Nick Collins, 1320pts, 45th overall

No NZ Championships this year
             Regretfully, there will be no NZ Champs this year. Having investigated all options we are unable to offer a viable event in 2013. However we are working towards a New Zealand Championship event for 2014 and beyond. Stay tuned for future announcements.

 
Events coming up

Hamels Haunts III
Dunedin, Sunday July 28th
          Organised by Matt Bixley again, this year’s Hamels Haunts is a TRAINING event and MTB friendly. It is to give people a chance to practice navigating, route finding, riding, transitions, team-work etc. in a non-competitive environment.
           You choose your start time (between 1pm and 3pm), you’ll be handed your map and go from there. Map reading, navigation, trekking and riding will be at levels expected for the Dunedin Mazda Adventure Race and for the Spring Challenge. Checkpoints will vary from very easy to some more difficult ones for those that want more of a challenge. Coaching/Advice will be given to groups that would like it before they head out.
            Cost $10 (under 15 free), there will be soup/bread at the finish, no prizes so it’s only bragging rights, and I’m not even sure if there will be results, but there probably will be. See http://runlongergeek.wordpress.com/rogaine/ and https://www.facebook.com/events/269102113232051/ for up-to-date information. Matt's contact is matt.bixley@agresearch.co.nz or phone 021 046 9255.

Equestrian Rogaine 2013
Bottle Lake Forest (Burwood, Christchurch), Sunday 4th August
           It is on again for a second year - an equestrian rogaine in support of Darfield High School’s rural college. Do you own a horse? Do you know someone who owns a horse? If so, then this 3hr navigational adventure on horseback, organised by Stu Payne and Carol Fowler, is for you. See http://www.darfield.school.nz/curriculum/special-programmes/rural-college/ for complete information and an entry form. Entries close Wednesday 31st July.
