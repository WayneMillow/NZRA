Heights of Winter and the North Canterbury drought update
 As we advised participants on the day, the Association has made a substantial donation to the North Canterbury Rural Support Trust, acknowledging the support farmers have given to rogaining since the first Heights of Winter in 1998, and that the current drought crisis they face is pay-back time. The Chair of the Trust has responded, “Can I just say how overwhelmed I am at the generous donation from your group. I can assure you it will be used in the wider context of helping the district farmers and their families. …the kindness of yourselves and others, in making an unsolicited gift, that means such a lot to people facing drought conditions on a daily basis”.

Events in the next couple of months

            Orienteering Hutt Valley (OHV) will be hosting the 2015 NZ Secondary School Rogaine Champs in the Waiterere forest next Saturday 8th August. There are 3hr Junior and 4hr Senior events. NZRA wishes all participants good running and clean navigation. Information may be found at http://nzssrogaine.org.nz/. Hamel's Haunt VI, Dunedin, Sunday 9th August, 3hrs
          The 6th event in a series organised by Matt Bixley is based at Laing's Road, Dunedin. See the details on Matt's blog. This will be a fun event and not to be missed even if the weather forecast is not looking all that desirable.

Lowburn 3hr Rogaine, Lake Dunstan, Sunday 16th August
           The Lowburn Rogaine is a Highland Events/Epic Navigation joint venture. Phil Wood, Australasian Champ, will be taking a navigation training session pre-event. A great fun family day out and an ideal introduction to Rogaining is ensured. See the website for details and entry.

Wellington Afterwork Rogaine Series, Wellington, next event 27th August

Wicked Rogaine, Wanaka, Sunday 30th August, 1 & 2 hrs
           The Wicked team of Nora Audra and Ian Edmond is organising a 2hr event, suitable for both gurus and newbies, based at Eely Point Recreational Reserve. There is also a 1hr option that would be attractive for families and kids. See the Wicked website for details.

Port Hills Rogaine, Saturday 5th September, 3 & 6hrs

Vegas Vindaloo MTB Rogaine, Rotorua, Sunday 6th September, 2hrs
           This annual 2 hour MTB rogaine will be run in a relatively unused area in the Whakarewarewa Forest on Sunday morning, 6th September. See http://www.obop.org.nz/ for further details.

Mt Outdoors Spring Rogaine, Saturday 12th September, 3, 6 & 12hrs

Recent Events
Kaiwara Rogaine, North Canterbury, Saturday 25th July, 6 + 3hr
        The controls are in, the results have been tallied and the dust has settled on the Kaiwara Hills after the latest instalment of the Canterbury Rogaine Series.

Organisers Tim Farrant and Jordan Pearson report:
       “Saturday dawned with weather reminiscent of summer, and an impressive field of almost 300 keen rogainers flocked to the Kaiwara Woolshed to get ready for a day in the hills.  By all accounts the rolling hill terrain with a mix of open country, pine plantations and pockets of native forest proved a good challenge for rogainers of all abilities and was enjoyed by all. The three hour event also doubled as New Zealand’s first rogaine-themed hen’s party for world champion adventure racer Sophie Hart, who is soon to marry fellow rogainer Nick Ross.
        In the six hour event, the Flying Falcons (Chris Forne and Tane Cambridge) made an ambitious plan to collect all the controls.....”
Take a look at the full report and the results on the Canterbury Series website, and the winners’ route on Facebook.


Great Forest Rogaine, Rotorua, Saturday 25th July
           About 140 people turned out for the Great Forest 3hr, 6hr, & 6hr MTB Rogaines in the Whakarewarewa Forest area. This popular annual event, suitable for all ages and abilities, is organised by Orienteering Bay of Plenty. See the OBOP website for full results.


Wellington Afterwork Rogaine Series, Wellington, 30th July
            Fifty five people enjoyed the 3hr challenge of a Bush Rogaine in the dark in the Belmont Regional Park on Thursday 30th July. The organisers report that the evening was cold but dry with only a light breeze. For a full report and results see the Welly website.

13th World Rogaining Championships (WRC2015)
          In just over 2 weeks, 22nd - 23rd August, 848 Rogainers from 26 countries will participate in the 13th World Championships in Northern Finland. In contrast to the 2014 championships in South Dakota in which 34 Kiwis competed with some distinction there will be only 6 NZers at this championship. We wish Viv Prince, Dave Armstrong, Andrew McGowan, and the Oldies team of Pete Squires, Anne & Bill Kennedy all the best in their endeavours.

Other Championship Events
New Zealand 24hr Championships, 31st October - 1st November, Marlborough
          This is the only annual 24hr Rogaine to be held in New Zealand. Chief organiser is NZRA President, Mondo Kopua. Even though classified as a "championship" the event is not just for the elite athlete but can be enjoyed by any team accepting their own level of challenge. Remember you don’t have to be out on course continuously for the full 24hrs. You can return to base for food (supplied) and rest at any time. There will be a shorter non-championship event as well. Keep your eye on the event website for more information as it becomes available.

3rd Australasian Championships, 10th – 11th October 2015
        Entries are open for the 3rd Australasian Championships to be held in the Capertee National Park about 3½ hrs west of Sydney on the weekend 10th – 11th October. This is a wonderful rogaining area with mainly rolling hills, river flats and open bushland providing easy travel. See the website for full information.

14th World Rogaining Championships (WRC2016), 23-24 July 2016
           The course for the 14th World Rogaining Championships in the MacDonnell Ranges east of Alice Springs, Australia is now set and awaits your participation. Take the opportunity to join in as it will be another 4 or 5 years before the World Champs are this close to home. Entries are limited so read the WRC2016 qualification tips if you are keen to be there. The event website has recently opened and registration begins 23rd October 2015.
