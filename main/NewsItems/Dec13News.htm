Events coming up

Christmas Twilight
Otahuna Bush, Christchurch, Thursday 19th December, 3hrs
          Ho Ho Ho! It's time to get ready for the annual 'Christmas Twilight Rogaine' organised by Emily Wall and Chris Forne. Once again the start and finish area will be at the base of the Otahuna Valley. Gibraltar Rock is open this year so there will be some new areas to explore. The terrain includes lovely native bush, which you may have to do a little bush bashing through, as well as open tussock lands, farm paddocks, gorse bushes, scenic views etc. You will love it! Further info will be posted on the Christmas-rogaine website. (We hope that Chris recovers from his current exertions in the Seagate team at the Adventure Racing World Champs in Costa Rica to assist Emily with the organisation of the Christmas Twilight).


Whangamata XMAS Rogaine
Whangamata, Saturday 21st December, 3hrs
          Organised by Ryan Thompson (also currently at the Adventure Racing World Champs in the Bivouac Colts team) and friends this is a 3hr Rogaine with a XMAS twist. The course will cover the beach, parks, coastal tracks and urban pathways of Whangamata. For further information see the event website at www.whangamataevents.co.nz/xmas-rogaine.


Akatarawa Attack MTB Rogaine
Akatarawas, Wellington, Sunday 26th January, 4hr & 8hr options
      A MTB rogaine organised by the Hutt Valley Mountain-bike Club in the Akatarawa ranges that lie between Upper Hutt and Kapiti. Teams of 2 - 5 people. For complete information see the event website http://www.akattack.org.nz/. Entries due by 16th January.


Canterbury Series Waitangi Day Rogaine
Port Hills, Christchurch, Thursday 6th February, 3hr & 90min options
      Tim Farrant and team are putting on a Waitangi-themed rogaine event on Thursday 6 February 2014 based in the Christchurch Port Hills. 3 hr and 90 minute options. More information is available on the event website http://canterburyrogaine.com/.

Recent Results

Karekare Gladefest, West Auckland, Saturday 16th November
          The Gladefest was organised by Shaun Collins in support of the Lone Kauri Community School. Based at the Pohutukawa Glade the course took in beach and bush with event durations of 90 minutes or 3hrs. For results see the Lactic Turkey website.


Belmont “Big Trig” Rogaine, Wellington, Saturday 30th November
       About 95 people turned out for the “Big Trig” organised by Michael Wood and team. Michael reports:
“A fairly rugged day weatherwise: very high winds, rain and mist on the tops. But with the expectation of improvement by mid afternoon (and hope of sensible route choice by teams) we were under way. We were rewarded by very pleasant conditions for the food, and everyone back within about 10min of schedule.”
For results and full comment see the event website.


Welly Afterwork, Tuesday 19th November
          The final event in the 2013 Welly Afterwork Series was run on Tuesday 19th November. About 130 runners, walkers and school children turned out on a pleasant Wellington evening. Take a look at the results at http://www.mapsport.co.nz/wellyrog/awrog13res.html.


Overseas Championships

12th World Rogaining Championships
Black Hills, South Dakota, 16th & 17th August, 2014
          It has transpired that the recommendation to register promptly was unnecessary. As of Tuesday 3rd December the total of 328 entrants is well short of the 1000 limit. 132 of those are from the USA. 31 Kiwis have registered their interest.  Other countries with significant numbers are Russia 48, Australia 33, Canada 26, Latvia 14, Estonia 12 and Japan 9. The list of entrants is available at http://peakassurance.net/Registration_files/Wait%20List.pdf. The event website is at http://peakassurance.net/.

We can but hope that there are many more top Rogainers contemplating entry to ensure the competition is truly a World Championship. A number of people have argued that annual world championships will dilute the competition and that the original biennial championship is more appropriate for Rogaining. The evidence so far would support this contention.


European Championships
Estonia, 7th - 8th June, 2014
          If you happen to be in Europe at the beginning of June a visit to Estonia for the European Championships would be a worthwhile addition to your itinerary. The Champs are based in the village of Orava in south-east Estonia, about 15km from the border with Russia. Terrain varies from pine forest mostly providing very good visibility, to swamps, bogs and marshes and picturesque lakes and rivers. Entries are open on the event website.
