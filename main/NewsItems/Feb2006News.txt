Tararua24 - results
===================

The New Zealand Championship Tararua24 Rogaine was run last weekend in the Eastern Tararuas in ideal
weather. Chris Forne and Aaron Prince retained their title with a brilliant display of sustained
rogaining and 3970 points. Only Hamish Goodwin and George Christison were close on 3730 points. Third
place was a distant 800 points behind the winners. For full results see the event website at
http://www.tararua24.co.nz/ .
