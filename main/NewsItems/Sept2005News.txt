BIVOUAC OUTDOOR NIGHT NAVIGATION SERIES
7th, 14th, 21st Sept. Christchurch
==========================================================================
Tune up your night navigation with this 3 race series.
1 hour events on Foot or Mountain Bike. New venue this year.

  LOCATION: Spencerville Park 25 mins N of ChCh - Pine Forested Sand dunes.
            (off Lower Styx Road – follow signs)
  REGISTRATION: From 6.00pm  (enter on the day)
  BRIEFING AND MAPS: At 6.45pm
  START: 7.00pm
  GEAR: Torch or headlamp, compass
  COST: $10 per race, $25 for all three races paid on the first night
