GRAM
Geraldine Rogaine Autumn Muster
3hr and 6hr Rogaines
Geraldine, Sunday 10th April

The annual GRAM is returning to Waihi Station (South Canterbury), with the three-hour event on the scenic hills and gullies of the farm, and the six-hour event extending onto some brand-new country with runnable Douglas fir forest. The event centre (Woodbury Hall), food (GHS PTA) and planning (Mike Harding/Stuart Payne) will be the same as in previous years. See the full information and enter now. Entries close on Monday.


Other events coming up – there are lots
Wellington Afterwork Rogaine Series, Wellington, monthly from 14th April
          The Welly afterwork series of 3hr events is on again this year at roughly monthly intervals until November with the first event on Thursday 14th April. See the Welly website for further information.


Whangamata Adventure Rogaine, Whangamata, Saturday 23rd April, 3hr foot + 3hr MTB
             Rogaining with a twist. MTB in the morning, on foot in the afternoon with a lunch break between. You can enter one event or enter both and compete for the overall title. 1:25000 map with 20m contours. See the Whangamata Events website for more information.


Equestrian Rogaine, Puaha Valley, Little River, Sunday 1st May, 3 hrs
            Carol Fowler and Stuart Payne are organising another farmland equestrian rogaine this time on Banks Peninsula in Puaha Valley, up the road from Little River. See the Horse Rogaine blog for information and entry and join the discussion on Facebook.


Auckland Rogaine Series, begins 1st May
          Three short 90 minute Sunday events 1st, 15th and 29th May in the Auckland region. For more information see the event website.


Highland Events Mitre 10 Earnscleugh Rogaine, Alexandra, Saturday 7th May; 12, 6 and 3hr
           Highland Events are organising their Autumn Rogaine on Earnscleugh Station near Alexandra. This is an iconic high country station in Central Otago with a range of terrain from flat to rolling and mountain country with mostly open tussock but also some Kanuka forest and some steep gullies. The 3hr event is intended for Family teams and Beginners. There is a 6hr MTB event too. Entries are open via the Highland Events website.


Metlink City Safari, from Newtown Park Wellington, Sunday 15th May, 3 or 6 hour
         The Safari is on again. This is a unique urban Rogaine in which you use public transport (bus, train, cable-car, ferry) to get partway to the checkpoints. The course is designed so that by applying good strategy and using their heads the smartest family team could beat the fastest athletic team. For more information see the event website www.citysafari.org.nz. Entries are open.

 
T'Walk 2016, Canterbury, weekend 21st-22nd May
              This is the 50th “24hr Walk” organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club, the pioneer of long distance foot navigation events in NZ. T’Walk differs from a Rogaine in that the order of checkpoint visits is somewhat predetermined but it is great fun and the moon promises to be full to cast light upon the shenanigans. Total entries are limited to 300 people. See the T'Walk website for all the detail and a video to give you some idea of what you might be in for. Entries are open; enter now so you don’t miss out.

Recent Results

Charwell Challenge - Inland Road (North Canterbury), Saturday 19th March.
            This inaugural event introduced 170 competitors young and old and from the northern part of the South Island and lower North Island to the farmland inland from Kaikoura. Participants were treated to some amazing country and local hospitality.

As organiser Grant Hunter reports:

“ …. the clear day enhanced the rugged Seaward Kaikoura backdrop and the glimpse of the distant ocean from high points in that (to most of us) ‘mysterious frontier’ called the Inland Road. It’s a landscape unmatched in my rogaining experience. Under our feet were grassed hills with pockets of native forest and scrub, rock outcrops, and pesky scrub filled gullies which were only for the brave or reckless …. “.

Continue reading Grant’s reflections of the day.

Wicked Autumn Series - Christchurch Port Hills, Wednesday 9th, 16th & 23rd March
           An average of about 160 people per event enjoyed this afterwork series of 2hr Rogaines on the Port Hills visiting locations which have been out-of-bounds since the earthquakes. For results and a great array of photos see the Wicked Rogaines website. The next Wicked series will be the mid-winter Night Series 1st, 8th & 15th June. Mark it in your diaries.
