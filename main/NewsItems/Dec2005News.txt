        Christmas Twilight Rogaine 3hrs - Wednesday 21st December
        Mt Vernon Carpark (Summit Road), Port Hills, Christchurch.
    ==================================================================

Organised by Emily Wall and Chris Forne, this will be a fun event, with a
Christmas theme, cryptic clues, and lots of yummy prizes. So come along and enjoy
the excitement. There will be prizes for the top three in each category, plus
numerous bonus and other spot prizes. Registration and maps from 5:30pm, start
6:30pm. For more information and entry forms (incl. early-bird registration
rates) see the Twilight Rogaine webpage, http://www.cutc.org.nz/twilight_rogaine
Enquiries: emily.wall@ccc.govt.nz




New Zealand Rogaining Championships, 18th-19th February
======================================================
The 2006 New Zealand Rogaining Championships (24hr), organised by the Hutt Valley
Orienteers, will be run the weekend of 18th-19th February in the Waiarapa (near
Wellington). In addition to the Championships there will be 12hr, 6hr and 3hr
events. For details, entry form, planners' report and other interesting bits and
pieces consult the event website, http://www.tararua24.co.nz.



The Baker's Dozen - Saturday 18th March
=========================================
6hr & 13hr options - for those who need one more hour! Organised by Bob
Cunninghame, family, and friends in the Dunedin/Strath-Taieri area. Further
details will be notified on the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz , as they
come to hand.
