Stoneyard Staggers
24hr (NZ Champs) and 7hr Rogaines
Eastern Otago, 27th – 28th February

           Entries for the Stoneyard Staggers close next Friday, 19th February. See the event website for all the details and on-line entry. The NZ Championships is the only 24hr Rogaine run in New Zealand so if you are up for a challenge then this is your chance. There is a 7 hour non-championship event on the Saturday as well.

The 24hr Rogaine will be suitable for everyone, not just experienced and elite rogainers. Remember you do not need to stay out for all of the 24 hours - you may make multiple trips back to the "hash house" where hot food, socialising, rest, and perhaps a bed, will await you. And for the 68 Rogainers heading to the World Championships in Alice Springs in July this is the chance to trim up your technique.


Other events coming up

MTB Rogaine Series, Rotorua, February/March, 90 minutes
            The annual series of 90 minute MTB rogaines, organised by Orienteering Bay of Plenty, will be run on Sundays 14th & 28th February and 20th March, with an additional novelty event on Thursday 18th February as part of the Rotorua Bike Festival. These are fun, family friendly events. All the information you need is on the Orienteering Bay of Plenty website. Entries close 11th February for the series and 15th February for the novelty event.


Massey Urban Rogaine, Massey University, Saturday 27th February, 90 minutes
               This is a family friendly fundraising Rogaine on the Massey University Campus and surrounding area. Organised by the Massey Child Care Centre it will also provide a challenge for the more athletically minded competitor. Information and entry form are available on the Childcare website.


Bouldergaine, Canterbury Foothills, Saturday 5th March, 3hrs
               The Bouldergaine, a combination of bouldering and Rogaining, is back in the scenic Castle Hill boulder-fields, an 80 minute drive from Christchurch. It is being organised by the Alpine Club in conjunction with Peninsula and Plains Orienteers. Take a look at the details and entry.


Wicked Autumn Rogaine Series, Christchurch Port Hills, 9th, 16th & 23rd March, 2 hrs
              The first event in this popular annual three week afterwork series of 1hr (family friendly) and 2hr Rogaines is on 9th March. See the Wicked website and the facebook page for more information. Don’t miss the chance “To get out there”.


Charwell Challenge, Inland Kaikoura, Saturday 19th March, 3 & 6 hrs
           Kaikoura Suburban School and the NZ Rogaining Association are collaborating on a Rogaine to be held on the 'Inland Road' between Mt. Lyford and Kaikoura, North Canterbury - a 2½ hour drive from Christchurch and 2 hrs from Blenheim. There will be 3 and 6 hour options. Expect stunning landscapes and an excellent mix of terrain for Rogaining. Overnight camping is available on-site. Download the information sheet. Entries are now open.

Recent Results

Christchurch Rogaine Series, Port Hills, Wednesdays 27th Jan, 3rd & 10th Feb
            The after-work Rogaine adventures hosted by Team Next Generation (Tim Farrant and Elisha Nuttall) were well supported with about 180 people turning out for the events. New areas on the Port Hills were opened up for exploration. The overall winner was the experienced team of Georgia Whitla and Greig Hamilton. See facebook or the website for results.


Akatarawa Attack MTB/Foot Rogaine, Sunday 31st January, 4 & 8 hrs
             Competitors in this year’s Akatarawa Attack could use MTB and foot sections in any proportion that appealed to them. This added another dimension to the normal route planning. Of course, teams had to bring their MTBs back with them to the finish. About 80 people tried their luck in the rugged terrain. See the results and stories on the Attack website.


WRC2016 positions still available
            There are still positions available in the 14th World Rogaining Championships (WRC2016) to be run near Alice Springs. Join the 68 Kiwis already entered in what will be a challenging Rogaine in very interesting terrain: semi-arid, rocky and technical, with complex gully systems. All the entry conditions have been relaxed so now is your chance to partake in a world champs close to home. Consult the event website for full information.


NZRA Annual General Meeting
               The NZRA AGM will be held in Christchurch on Monday 7th March at 6 Harrods Court, Christchurch. The Agenda will include:
  1. Minutes of the 2015 AGM and matters arising
  2. Annual reports from the President and the Association’s representative on the International Rogaining Federation (IRF)
  3. Financial report
  4. Election of officers
  5. Other business:  IRF event levies (Emma).

Additional agenda items and nominations for the positions of President, Treasurer, Secretary, and committee members should be lodged with the Secretary (g.hunter@clear.net.nz) at least five days before the meeting.

Our President Mondo Kopua has indicated he will stand down. Our Secretary, Grant Hunter would like to step aside, citing his analysis that either the Association deploys smarter administration tools for operating at a range of geographical scales, or it downsizes to a regional-scale operation. 

The Association may be able to help with travel costs if you strongly wish to attend in person. Or, if there is wider interest we may be able to establish a telephone conference link for those outside Christchurch. Please notify the Secretary, g.hunter@clear.net.nz, if you wish to take advantage of either option.

The minutes of the 2015 AGM are available at:  http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/NewsItems/AGM2015_minutes.pdf
