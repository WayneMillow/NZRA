
New Zealand Rogaining Association (Inc.)
8th December 2010


Dear Rogainer,

      The World Championships in the Cheviot region over the weekend of 20-21 
November was a great success due in no small measure to a willing and happy band of 
volunteers who pitched in to help. NZRA thanks all those volunteers who made the 
event such a pleasant experience for all participants.

We enjoyed amazing cooperation from over 70 landowners. The Cheviot Area School 
opened their complete facilities to us and the School and Home Association provided 
continuous meals during the rogaine as well as the post-event meal. Many people 
commented on how they enjoyed those meals. The Cheviot Lions fed us on Friday 
evening. And we need to give a big thank you to the whole Cheviot Community. Visitors 
from overseas remarked on how welcome they felt.

521 competitors in 251 teams from 22 countries fronted on the day. Kiwis Chris Forne 
and Marcel Hagener won by an unbelievable 850 points with a total of 4365. (Marcel is 
of German origin but has lived in NZ for many years). Second place on 3515 points 
went to Nicholas Mulder and Ryno Griesel from South Afria, and third was the team of 
Tonu Lillelaid, Karli Lambot, and Rait Pallo from Estonia on 3345 points.

Besides Chris and Marcel other Kiwis featured highly in the results with gold medals 
to Hamish Goodwin and Pete Swanson (MV), Rowan Nugteren and Carl Janssen (MJ), Trish 
Faulkner and Anne Kennedy (WSV), and Georgie Opie and Stacey McCoy (WJ). 
Congratulations to these people.

Detailed results are available at http://wrc2010.org.nz. There you will find a 
results breakdown with distance and time splits for every team. A plot of every 
team's route is provided via Google Earth and there are lots of pictures.


Rogaine on the Couch
====================
     Competitors at the World Championships were introduced to ROGO, a puzzle which 
has its roots in Rogaining. The puzzle was developed by Christchurch Rogainers Nicola 
Petty and Shane Dye. You too can ROGO - see the website, http://www.rogopuzzle.com/, 
for introductory games and more information. An app for iPhone, iPad, and IPod touch 
has just been released.

Christmas Twilight, Port Hills, Christchurch, Wednesday 15th December
======================================================================
     This fun event will once again be organised by Tim Farrant and Elisha Nuttall as 
a fundraiser for Junior Rogaining in Canterbury. Registration and maps available from 
5:30pm with a 6:30pm start from Victoria Park car park. For entry forms and further 
information see the event website http://www.cutc.org.nz/twilight_rogaine/. There is 
a $5 discount on entries received before 13th December.


Urban Series, New Plymouth, continues 2nd February
==================================================
     This series of eleven afterwork events began on the 10th November and runs 
weekly through until 9th March except for a break over the Christmas period. See the 
Maptalk calendar, http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine, for details. 


2011 Australian Championships, Tasmania, 22-23 January
======================================================
     To be hosted by Rogaining Tasmania in the northern midlands of Tasmania. The 
course will be set by experienced rogainers John Brock, Darryl Smith, Karen Pedley 
and Nigel Davies. The terrain is a mixture of open plains (farmland) and steeper 
lightly wooded hills. In addition to the 24hr Championship Rogaine there is a Roving 
15hr event (15hrs on course within 24hrs). See the event website, 
http://www.arc2011.rt.asn.au/. This is a qualifying event for the 10th World 
Championships, Czech Republic, 31st August 2012.


2011 New Zealand Championships, Marlborough, 19-20 March 2011
=============================================================
     Note that the date for the New Zealand Championships has been revised and is now 
on the weekend of 19th March, ie one week earlier than previously advised. This 
change is necessary because of farming operations by one of the major landowners. The 
change is unfortunate in that the Championships now clash with Adventure Race 
Coromandel and with the Mt Lyford Challenge. We regret the change but it is outside 
our control.

Mondo Kopua is chief planner and has most of the course organised already. Further 
information will be posted on the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz as 
arrangements are finalised. There may be a shorter associated event as well as the 
24hr championship.
