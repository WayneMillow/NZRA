Wellington Afterwork Series, Wellington, beginning 13th July
============================================================
      This series of 3hr afterwork events at intervals of 3 or 4 weeks in the
Wellington area begins 13th July and continues to early November. See the Maptalk
calendar, http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine, for details.

     
Lactic Turkey Riverhead Rogaine, Auckland, Sunday 8th August
============================================================
A 3hr event in the Riverhead Forest which is at the head of the Waitemata Harbour,
north-west of Auckland. Information and entry form are available on the Lactic Turkey
Events website, http://www.lacticturkey.co.nz/Rogaines.htm. Entries close 4th August.


Winter Classic, Wellington, Sunday 15th August
==============================================
The Winter Classic is an annual orienteering event that has a reputation of being both
physically and mentally demanding. There is a 3hr Challenge Rogaine run in conjunction
with the event. See the Classic website, http://www.winterclassic.co.nz/, for details.


Annavale Attack and Amble, Canterbury, Sunday 22nd August
=========================================================
A navigational adventure in the Canterbury foothills in support of Darfield HS Rural
College. All new terrain from 2009. The 4-hour rogaine (Attack) is for the competitive
and the 3-hour rogaine (Amble) is intended to be more recreational. Download a flyer
from the Darfield High School website,
http://www.darfield.school.nz/curriculum/special-programmes/rural-college/. An entry
form will be available soon.


E.P.I.C. Rogaine Returns, Otago, Saturday 11th September
=========================================================
Rogainers who have been around for a while will remember with some "affection" the
EPIC events run by Bruce McLeod in the Central Otago region. The last event was in
2003. The great news is the Bruce and Phil Wood are teaming to bring the EPIC back to
us. Keep your eye on the event calendar at E.P.I.C. Navigation,
http://homepages.ihug.co.nz/~Sbmcleod/Calendarframes.htm, for further information as
Bruce and Phil get themselves organised.
