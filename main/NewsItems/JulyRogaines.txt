                    ENTRIES CLOSE NEXT SUNDAY

PAPO is organising the popular Heights of Winter this year. The course is being
planned by reigning Junior (Mixed) World Champ, Tim Farrant and interesting terrain
is guaranteed. There are 12 and 6 hr options. Entries close 21st June. Get your
ENTRY IN NOW. Download an entry form from http://www.papo.org.nz/.
