Dear Rogainer,

Over the next couple of months there is a range of Rogaines to look forward to, with something for everybody, up and down the country.
  1. The first NZ Secondary  School Championships will be run in conjuction with the annual Great Forest Rogaine in Rotorua on Saturday 9th August. The Great Forest offers 3hr and 6 hr options on foot and MTB.

  2. The Winter Classic in Wellington on Sunday 17th August is chiefly an Orienteering event with a reputation for being tough in steep, well-vegetated, merciless country. This year’s terrain sounds more benign than usual. A 3hr Rogaine is included within the day’s entertainment.

  3. The next 3hr Welly Afterwork Rogaine is on 26th August in, and west of, the Cannons Creek area. Kelvin Thiele and family are the planners. 

  4. In the parks and reserves of Nelson there is the Wily Weka Challenge (3hr and 6hr) on Saturday 6th September. Should be good!

  5. On Saturday 13th September  Highland Events will provide their annual Spring 12hr, 6hr & 3hr family events plus a 6hr MTB Rogaine in Central Otago.
Details for each of these Rogaines are available on the individual event websites as above. For more Rogaines in the months ahead refer to either
  • the NZRA event calendar at http://www.rogaine.org.nz/ or
  • the OrienteeringNZ calendar.

The Golden Road Rogaine has been run
                 Based at El Dorado Station, Northeast Otago the Golden Road finally took place on Saturday 12th July with a good turnout of 101 people. Organiser Bob Cunninghame has commented on the day from his perspective and is obviously pleased that it went well after a couple of misfires.  Read what he has to say.

Greig Hamilton and Matt Scott cleaned the course with Matt Bixley and Martin Lukes hot on their tail, and Georgia Whitla and Tim Farrant not far behind. See the results and read Matt Bixley’s reflections on the event.

Dunedin action is hotting up. Bob is already planning the "Never Look Back" Autumn rogaine with 13, 6, and 3hr options, and Matt Bixley is planning a T’Walk-like Enduro in the February/March time frame. We will keep you informed via the NZRA calendar where you will find preliminary announcements for these rogaines.

 

Championship events

World Championship, Black Hills, South Dakota, 16th-17th August 2014
After a long build-up the 12th World Rogaining Championship is only two weeks away. While the goal of all the 37 Kiwis registered is to enjoy the immersion in a World Championship some have a good chance of a top placing. To see who is competing take a look at the list of entrants. Check out the blog and an article by Bud Laird on Rogaining in S. Dakota.

Good luck to all our representatives. Stay tuned for results which will probably be on the event website within 24 hours of the finish.

Australasian Championship, Victoria, 11th-12th October 2014
The Australasian Championship (Fossickers Dream) website and on-line entry is now active with lots of information. The event is set in a former goldfields area in the Castlemaine region, 1½ hrs drive from Melbourne. The terrain is a complex and challenging mixture of spurs and gullies. There will be a 6hr event as well as the championship 24hr rogaine. This promises to be an extraordinary event with a strong team of organisers.

New Zealand Championship 2014, Otago, 6th-7th December
Potential locations for checkpoints have been plotted and fieldwork for the 2014 New Zealand Champs has begun. Chief organisers are the experienced Highland Events team of Ed Stevens and Terry Davis who are well known for their twice yearly Wanaka Rogaines. There will be a range of shorter events in addition to the Championship. Look out for more information on the NZRA website.
