Snow Park Rogaine
 Pisa Range, Sunday 26th April
It is only 20 days to the Highland Events Autumn rogaine organised by Ed Stevens and Terry Davis. Based at the Snow Park, Pisa Range there will be 12hr, 6hr and 3hr family events, and a 6hr MTB option. This is classic Central Otago high country. For more information see the Highland Events website, http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/rogaine. Entries are open; get yours in now.

Note this Rogaine is on Anzac weekend. Monday is Anzac Day Observance public holiday for out-of-towners to travel home.

More Events in the next two months
             Two weeks after the Highland Events Snow Park Rogaine we have T’Walk in Canterbury and the Tutira Rogaine in Napier, and then a week later the City Safari in Wellington and the Autumn Muster in Geraldine.

T'Walk 2015, Canterbury, weekend 9th-10th May
              Organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club, the pioneer of long distance foot navigation events in NZ, this is the 49th running of this extremely popular event. It differs from a Rogaine in that the order of checkpoint visits is somewhat predetermined but it is great fun. This year T’Walk is 5 days after the full moon. Total entries are limited to 300 people. See the T'Walk website for all the detail. Entries are open; enter now so you don’t miss out.


Tutira Rogaine, Napier, Saturday 9th May; 3, 6 & 9 hrs
             Organised by George Christison, runner-up in the 2004 NZ Rogaine Championships, for the Tutira school. This event incorporates the Hawkes Bay Secondary schools champs. See the details and entry and follow facebook for updates. Entries are open.

 
Geraldine Rogaine Autumn Muster (GRAM), Geraldine, Sunday 17th May; 3 & 6hr
           The Autumn Muster in the foothills near Geraldine, South Canterbury, is a fundraiser for the local High School. Mike Harding is the chief course planner. The terrain is challenging farmed hill country with some scattered scrub and native bush. See the Geraldine High School website for all the details. Entries are open.


Metlink City Safari, Wellington, Sunday 17th May; 3 & 6hr
          The City Safari is a unique urban Rogaine that allows you to use public transport (bus, train, cable-car, ferry) to get partway to the checkpoints. The course is designed so that, by applying good strategy and using their heads, the smartest family team could beat the fastest athletic team. For more information see the event website www.citysafari.org.nz. Entries are open.

Recent Results
Taranaki Secondary School Rogaine Champs, Wednesday 18th March, 3hrs
 
 A full complement of 720 students, limited to that number by site works, competed in the 3hr Taranaki Secondary School Rogaine Champs on Wednesday 18th March. Nick Collins reports “Everything went off without a single problem. I think we’ve finally got the hang of it”. At least most students seem to be attentive at the briefing. Well done Nick.

NZRA is pleased to be able to support this event by sponsoring the Navlight scoring system.


Wicked Autumn Series, Christchurch, from 18th March
          Fine weather encouraged 170 people to get out and enjoy each of the three events in the Wicked series of 2hr afterwork Rogaines run at different locations in the Port Hills. Results and pictures are available on the event Facebook.


Never Look Back, Otago, Saturday 14th March
          The 90 people who turned out for this Rogaine organised by Bob Cunninghame 20km inland from Palmerston had a rewarding time running helter-skelter over great terrain in ideal weather. Not only did the Lawson family welcome us to their woolshed as base but they, and a neighbour, provided the scrumptious after-event food. Unfortunately Bob has had difficulty producing results but first place in the 13hr option went to Matt Bixley and Greg Thurlow, who cleared the course.

Why the event name? A prominent peak on the map named “Lots Wife” is topped with what appears to be a pillar of salt under certain lighting conditions. Google “Lots Wife” to see what happened to her when she looked back.

WRC2016 entry advice for Australia/New Zealand Rogainers
            The WRC2016 Organising Committee has provided preliminary information on the entry process for those planning to enter the 2016 World Championships in Central Australia. Entries will open in October and are limited to 800 persons. Since the event is very likely to be over-subscribed, it is important to understand how you can maximise the chance of your entry application being accepted. The key message is to enter early! Read the full advice.


Championship Events
3rd Australasian Championships, 10th – 11th October 2015
        The 2015 Australasian championships are to be held in the beautiful Capertee National Park about 3½hrs west of Sydney. This wonderful rogaining area offers open bushland, scenic creeks, river flats, magical vistas, rolling hills and one or two steeper hills. See the website for up-to-date information.

15th New Zealand Championships, 31st October 2015
              Subject to confirmation the 2015 New Zealand Championships will be in Marlborough on the weekend of 31st October. There will be shorter non-championship events in addition to the 24hr championship. Chief organiser is the experienced rogainer Mondo Kopua. There will be more information on the NZRA website in due course.
