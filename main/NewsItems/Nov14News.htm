Mt Evans Rogaine
Banks Peninsula, Sunday 9th November, 3 & 6hr options
    
Based at not-often-visited Camp Bay around the harbour from Purau Bay, the terrain is hilly farmland with a mix of open grassland, scrub, native forest, and some plantations. The springtime vegetation will be fresh and green, the air clean and refreshing, and people will be rewarded with great views out across the harbour and beyond.

See the Canterbury Rogaine website for details. Entries are open and close Thursday 30th October.

Organiser is Alastair McDowell, info@canterburyrogaine.com



14th New Zealand Championships
 Nevis Valley, Otago, 6th-7th December
 
This is the BIG one of the year – 24hrs of fun!!

The 14th NZ Championships will be run on the Ben Nevis and Craig-Roy Stations in the Nevis Valley, south-west of Cromwell. This interesting valley is quite remote but is readily accessible in summer – Google “Nevis Valley” for a glimpse of what the area offers. Chief organisers are the experienced Highland Events team of Ed Stevens and Terry Davis who are well known for their twice yearly Wanaka Rogaines.

Note: There will be a 12hr, 6hr, and 3hr Family events in addition to the Championship.

For more information, pictures, and online entry see the Highland Events website. Entries are open and close Monday 24th November. Remember you don’t have to be on the move for the whole 24hrs - you can return to base at any time for rest, food, and maybe sleep. There will be continuous food provided from 8pm to 8am. Sign up now.

Recent Results

EPIC Returns, Arrowtown, Saturday 18th October
On a day that started out fine but deteriorated to snow showers on the higher ground 40 rugged individuals in 14 teams enjoyed EPIC’s inaugural 12hr multi-loop, T’Walk style rogaine. In clearing 72 checkpoints Matt Bixley, Malcom McLeod and Glenn Kelly ran out the winners by only one checkpoint in a closely fought contest.

Numbers were much stronger at 240 in the 3hr and 6hr events with family teams making up the bulk of the 3hr event. Clear winners in the 6hr were the “Torpedo 7” mixed team (Richard Anderson, Bob Maclachlan, Simon Bowdan, and Jo Williams) whose other claim to fame is 2nd place in the last Godzone multiday adventure race.

Matt Bixley reports:
“Some fantastic terrain and history was visited. The weather up high wasn’t the most pleasant but the opportunity to go places I otherwise wouldn’t have far outweighed the discomfort .....

Having spoken semi–frequently with Phil over the last few months, he was adamant that the map was going to be accurate. He talked up the quality of the map at every opportunity he got, and by quality he meant the vegetation, features, tracks, controls, the lot, were going to be in the right place. It’s a bold statement to make, repeatedly, but the team delivered. Small depressions, extra contours for high points, water races, varying degrees of track were all mapped and all accurate”.
See the Epic website for full results and Matt’s report.


Ahuriri Equestrian Rogaine, Tai Tapu, Canterbury, Sunday 19th October 
            
             The 4th Equestrian Rogaine organised by Stuart Payne and Carol Fowler saw 40 riders and horses out and about enjoying the hilly terrain of the Ahuriri Farm. (We are reliably informed by riders that horses love charging uphill but are not so keen on the downhills). The farm was a fabulous venue and lovely country to ride over with its fresh spring growth. See the results, map and pictures on Facebook. The event raised $1000 for the GRACI Foundation (Gynaecology Cancer Research Trust).

Australasian Championship, Victoria, 11th - 12th October

             A relatively small contingent of 14 NZ Rogainers competed in the Australasian Championships in the Freyers Range and Upper Loddon State Forests, near Malmsbury, Victoria on 11th & 12th October. The course was in very complex spur/gully terrain and almost completely in bush; but the bush was quite open and relatively clean underfoot.

            Hearty congratulations to Kiwis Georgia Whitla and Greig Hamilton who were overall winners and the first mixed team. Finishing 4th overall Robert Jarvis and Phil Wood claimed both the Mens Open and Mens Veteran trophies in a repeat of last year. Other NZers on the podium were Vivienne Prince and the Oldies team of Pete Squires, Anne & Bill Kennedy. Further details and a full list of NZ results, with links to the map, are available.


13th World Rogaining Championships, 22nd - 23rd August, 2015

            It seems we have only just recovered from the 12th WRC in South Dakota and now it is time to consider the 13th WRC in Finland, August 2015. This will be the first WRC to be run in the Arctic; in the Saariselkä region of Lapland, about 200km north of the polar circle.

           While we associate perpetual daylight with that region continuous daylight ceases at the WRC2015 location about 21st July. By 22nd August the sun sets at 9.30pm and rises about 5am so, on that basis, there will be roughly 7½ hrs of darkness. However, twilight at those latitudes lingers on for about 1½ hrs so there will be only 4½ hrs of real dark. The sun will not rise far above the horizon even at midday.

           The exact details of the registration process have yet to be published but registration for pre-qualified entrants begins 1st December, and general registration begins 15th January. Entries are limited to 1000 people. Whether this limit will need to be invoked is a matter for conjecture but is likely given the event is in Europe.

           Bulletin 1 and course setter notes, including advice on weather, terrain, and map, are available on the WRC2015 website. Keep up to date via the event Facebook and Twitter.
