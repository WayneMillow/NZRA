Lactic Turkey Riverhead THIS SUNDAY 8th August
===============================================
      A 3hr event in the Riverhead Forest which is at the head of the Waitemata
Harbour, north-west of Auckland. Information and entry form are available on the
Lactic Turkey Events website, http://www.lacticturkey.co.nz/Rogaines.htm.
Pre-entries close today, 4th August. Late, on-the-day, entries will be accepted at
the organiser's discretion.


Winter Classic, Wellington, Sunday 15th August, 3hrs
====================================================
      The Winter Classic is an annual orienteering event that has a reputation of
being both physically and mentally demanding. There is a 3hr Challenge Rogaine run
in conjunction with the event. See the Classic website,
http://www.winterclassic.co.nz/, for details. Entries close 6th August so shake a
leg.


Tawa Night Rogaine, Wellington, Friday 20th August, 6hrs
========================================================
Tune up your night navigation with this 6hr event running from 6pm until midnight
- what a great way to start a weekend! Mark Hearfield is organiser. Entries close
13th August. See the Maptalk calendar,
http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine .


Annavale Attack and Amble, Canterbury, Sunday 22nd August, 4 & 3hrs
===================================================================
      A navigational adventure in the Springfield region of the Canterbury
foothills in support of Darfield HS Rural College. Organised by Stuart Payne and
Carol Fowler, the 4-hour rogaine (Attack) is for the competitive, and the 3-hour
rogaine (Amble) is intended to be more recreational. For more information and an
entry form see the Darfield High School website,
http://www.darfield.school.nz/curriculum/special-programmes/rural-college/.
Entries close 15th August.


Ophir Awesome E.P.I.C. Rogaine, Central Otago, Saturday 11th September, 12 & 6hrs
=================================================================================
      The E.P.I.C. is back with 12 and 6hr Rogaines in the historic Ophir area,
Central Otago. Organisers are Bruce McLeod and Phil Wood. For an entry form and
information see the E.P.I.C. Navigation website http://www.epicnavigation.co.nz/.
Entries close 7th September.


Wellington Afterwork Series, Wellington, next event 15th September
==================================================================
      The next event in this series of 3hr afterwork events in the Wellington area
is on 15th September. There are further rogaines on 7th October and 2nd November.
See the Maptalk calendar, http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine,
for details.


Katharine Eustace Fundraising Rogaine, Wanaka, Saturday 25th September
=======================================================================
      Fun family 3hr and 6 hr rogaines plus grab-a-map for those who just want to
go out and find out how Rogaining works. HQ at the Albert Town Tavern. This will
be great practice for the real deal on 23rd October.

This is a fundraising event for Katharine Eustace who is a keen Wanaka Rogainer
with many other athletic talents. Katharine will be representing NZ at the World
Champs, and competing on the World Circuit, in Skeleton Racing. So come along and
help Katharine reach her goals. There will be a fundraising auction after the
event plus other fun stuff. Highland Events organised a similar event last year
and it was alot of fun. For further details see http://www.highlandevents.co.nz


Highland Events Rogaine Heaven, Wanaka, Saturday 23rd October (Labour Weekend)
==============================================================================
      Organised by Ed Stevens and based at the Albert Town Tavern, the course will
be quite fast and also ideal for the beginner rogainer. The course features large
mainly flat areas with low open Kanuka scrub and there are some slightly hilly
parts going from 275m to 570m. There are 12 & 6hr options. See
http://www.highlandevents.co.nz for further details.


The Great Forest Rogaine, Rotorua, Saturday 6th November, 8hr
=============================================================
      An 8hr foot event and a 6hr MTB event with the same controls for both.
Utilising Rotorua's wonderful and right up-to-date Whaka Forest Map, this event is
perfectly timed for a pre-World Rogaine Champs blowout, and will also feature as a
part of the World Champion Team Orion's AR Training Weekend being held in and
around Rotorua. See the event website http://www.cdorienteering.co.nz. Contact is
Mark Copeland, copeland@copelandlaw.co.nz.


World Championships Assistants required
=======================================
      The organisation of the World Championships by a small, very active core
team of people is on target and proceeding at pace. The course planning is
complete, all the checkpoint locations have been taped, the course has been
approved by the vetters, so all that now remains is to put out the flags and the
Navlight electronic punches immediately prior to the event.

It is now time to expand the core team. There are tasks to complete leading up to
the event, and a large contingent of people is required during the event itself -
event base setup, registration and event centre attendants, camp monitors, parking
attendants, start/finish controllers, course patrollers and water drop
maintenance, event centre and catering roster during the event .... and on and on
it goes.

We are now looking for volunteers to assist us. If you would like to be part of
this World Championships and can offer some of your time please email Phil Bones
at admin@wrc2010.org.nz. At this stage we wish to accumulate a list of helpers.
Specific task lists and rosters will be produced closer to the event.


Lost Trophy - Heights of Winter
===============================
      The NZRA is trying to locate a trophy (silver cup) that was presented at one
of the last 3 or 4 Heights of Winter Rogaines to the first team which included a
female member. This could have been a mixed or an all female team.  The trophy was
originally awarded (engraved) for the Mountain Marathons which ran for ten years
back in the 1990's. If you know of the location of this trophy please contact Dave
Laurie at lauriefam@xtra.co.nz
