Canterbury Rogaine Series, Castle Hill, Saturday 8th October
           The final event in the Canterbury Rogaine Series starts at Castle Hill Village on Saturday 8th October. Three courses will be offered including a 3hr schools competition, a 3hr social grade, and a 4.5hr all-comers challenge - so there is something suitable for participants of all ages and abilities.  The school competition is a special feature of the series which seeks to promote rogaining and to further junior development through free entry to all high school students.

This final event incorporates a number of unique twists including a 250m, uninterrupted scree run and a “Sprogaine” (sprint-rogaine) through the alpine village. The series concludes with a barbecue and brief prize giving. Over $1200 in performance and spot prizes have been generously provided by Bivouac Outdoor.

For more information, and to enter online, see the website http://www.canterburyrogaine.com/. Entries close Thursday 6th October so don't delay in getting yours in.

Rogaining News

Nick and Annie prevail over the Territorians  
         Taranaki supervets Annie Sanderson and Nick Collins recently headed to the Australian Northern Territory to explore the Kakadu wetlands and wild places of the Arnhem Land escarpment. As a bonus they managed to schedule their trip to coincide with the NT Rogaining 24hr Championships. Nick’s cryptic comment says it all:
“Heat, humidity, escarpments like a moraine, grass up to Annies neck, plants that ripped your skin to pieces, green ants that fell out of trees and attacked you, wild pigs, high risk of grass fires affecting route choice, mosquitoes, rock climbing through waterfalls, spiders as big as your face, and fear of crocodiles on the swampy river crossings. Overall a great experience, but the locals struggled”.
The locals did indeed struggle. To the surprise of everybody, including themselves, Nick and Annie not only won the Supervets but were victorious overall. Read more about their enthralling experience in the News section of the NZRA website: http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/NewsItems/NickandAnnieNT2011.htm

Wounded bull derails Lactic Turkey Rogaine
          The Peak Road Rogaine in Auckland was postponed from 25th September to 2nd October due to a wounded wild bull running around the farm. From the Lactic Turkey website, http://www.lacticturkey.co.nz/RogaineInfo.htm
“The farmer has been trying to destroy this feral bull but as of this morning still couldn’t find it. Because the bull is wounded it is unpredictable so the risk of the bull charging someone is too great and the farmer has asked we postpone the event until next Sunday”.
The good news is that the bull was found and shot from a helicopter so the Rogaine is back on track a week late.
