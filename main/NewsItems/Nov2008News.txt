The WaikouWander - NZ Championships 2009
========================================
       Planning and organisation is well underway for the NZ Champs which will be held
in East Otago on the weekend of 7th/8th March. There will be an 8hr event in addition
to the championship. Currently the NZ Champs is the only 24hr rogaine in NZ so mark up
your calendars now. For a general information sheet take a look at the NZRA website
http://www.rogaine.org.nz.

Remember that you don't have to be a top team to enjoy the challenge of the longer
event. Many people get pleasure from a more relaxed approach returning to event
headquarters for food (supplied), rest and maybe a sleep before heading out again.
Everybody is welcome – come join us.

In addition to the usual gender and age categories a feature of these champs is the
introduction of the UniChallenge open to teams from Universities and Polytechnics. See
the information on the website for specific rules. So, you students out there, get
your teams together.



Silver Peaks Rogaine 2008, Saturday 18th October
================================================
As one participant put it, people in the Silver Peaks rogaine experienced, "4 seasons
in one day, with the extra bit of about 4 years worth of 4 seasons. We had bright warm
sunshine, bitterly cold winds, rain, hail, snow and sleet, and then it would begin the
cycle over again. There were some big hill climbs, but if you planned well you could
minimise the climb but maximise points".

Thanks to Bob Cunninghame, Laurie Kennedy and their support crew for organising the
event. Results are available on the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz.



Conferencing and Information Sharing - can you help?
====================================================
A recent meeting of the NZRA committee discussed ways to more actively involve the
regional representatives in meetings and decision making. Historically the NZRA has
been Christchurch-based. Currently there are 10 people representing NZRA throughout
the country (see the Contacts section of the website) and these people provide
valuable input and advice but they have not been regularly involved in meetings. It
was resolved that some form of teleconferencing and information sharing be
investigated.

Now this is where you come in. We are looking for advice, and help with setting
something up, from anyone with experience in simple, practical, easy to use
computer-based communication for information sharing and voice to voice, or video
communication, that might suit a national organisation such as NZRA for committee
meetings and other information interchange. Suggestions so far have included Skype
teleconferencing, an interactive 'Wiki' website, and a “committee” webpage to hold any
papers for meetings, agenda, and past minutes etc. If you can offer any suggestions
please contact Grant Hunter or Bill Kennedy - for email and phone details see the
"Contacts" list on the website.
