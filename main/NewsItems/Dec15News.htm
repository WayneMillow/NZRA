Next Event

Twilight Christmas Festive 3 hour Rogaine, Port Hills
Wednesday 16th December (this week)

The Christchurch Annual Twilight Christmas Festive 3 hour rogaine organised by Chris and Emily Forne, and maybe a few helpers, is on again!!! The start and finish will be in the Heathcote Valley Domain. All welcome! There will be lots of fun checkpoints to find, an exciting levitating twist in your planning options... and a yummy Christmas BBQ and nibbles at the finish line. More info on the blog, and on facebook facebook.

New Zealand 24hr Championships
February 27/28th

The 15th New Zealand Championships will be run in Otago north of Dunedin and inland from Palmerston and Waikouaiti the last weekend of February. This Rogaine will be suitable for everyone, not just experienced rogainers. There will be a shorter non-championship event or you can test yourself over the full 24 hours. However you do not need to stay out for all of that time - you may make multiple trips back to the "hash house" where hot food, rest, and perhaps a bed, will await you.

This championship will be a great opportunity for the 67 NZ Rogainers heading to Alice Springs in July for the World Championships to trim up their technique. Check out the event website for more information as it becomes available. Full details and on-line entry form will be available mid-January.

Other upcoming events

Akatarawa Attack MTB/Foot Rogaine
Sunday 31st January

This is a traditionally rugged MTB rogaine, in the wilds of the Akatarawas between Upper Hutt and Kapiti. Under new rules you can use your bike for all, some or none of the controls, which makes for more route choice, and also more teams. For more detail see the website.

Kaikoura Inland Rogaine
Saturday 19th March

Kaikoura Suburban School, local farmers, and the NZ Rogaining Association are collaborating on an event to be held on the 'Inland Road' between Mt. Lyford and Kaikoura, North Canterbury. There will be 6 and 3 hour options. Expect stunning landscapes and an excellent mix of terrain for rogaining. Overnight camping will be available on-site. Details will be on the NZRA website in the New Year.

New Zealanders dominate Adventure Racing World Champs

Congratulations to the Seagate team of Nathan Fa'ave, Chris Forne (current NZ Rogaining Champion), Sophie Hart, and Stu Lynch who completely dominated the recent Adventure Racing World Champs in Brazil. It is difficult to calculate their winning margin since all the other teams traversed a shorter course but it would have been about 24hrs had all teams completed the same 7 day course. The Swedish team Haglofs Silva captained by New Zealander Aaron Prince finished second. More on the Endurance Sport website and read Nathan’s report on SleepMonsters to experience the team’s highs and lows.


We wish you all a Merry Christmas and don’t forget to keep your eye on the NZ Champs website for information as it comes to hand. We hope to see you all at a Rogaine in the New Year.
