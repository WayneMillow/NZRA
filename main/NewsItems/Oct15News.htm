New Zealand 24hr Championships Postponed
Due to a combination of land access challenges for such a large area, and work commitments for the planner the difficult decision has been taken to postpone the 2015 NZ Championships which were to be run  31st October in Malborough.

This decision has not been taken lightly but we rely very much on the good will of rural communities and the time of voluntary organisers to be able to put on these events. We apologise for the lateness of this notice.

We are hoping to provide a Championship event early next year, most likely in Otago. Keep your eye on the NZRA website for up-to-date information.

Events in the next couple of months
3rd Australasian Championships, 10th – 11th October, near Sydney
             Now that there is no NZ Champs in the immediate future maybe you should consider getting your 24hr fix at the Australasian Championships, which will be a worthy navigational challenge with more than 10hrs of darkness and no moon. The Championships will be run in the Capertee National Park about 3½ hrs west of Sydney on the weekend 10th – 11th October. The area is wonderful for rogaining with mainly rolling hills, river flats and open bushland providing easy travel. See the website for full information. Be quick though, entries close tomorrow Friday 2nd October.


Wicked Rogaine Spring Series, Port Hills Christchurch, Wednesday 14, 21 & 28 October
            The first event in this popular three week afterwork series of 1hr and 2hr Rogaines is on 14th October. See the facebook page or the Wicked website for more information.

 
EPIC, Arrowtown, Saturday 17th October, 3, 6 & 9hrs
            The EPIC (Enigmatic Pseudo Insane Challenge) organised by Bruce McLeod and Phil Wood is on again this year. There will be 3hr, 6hr, and 9hr options. For detailed information see the EPIC website. Entries close Monday 12th October.


Wellington Afterwork Rogaine Series, 21st October
            The next 3hr event in this monthly series of Rogaines will be organised by Ann Kendon and Jo McKenzie. See the ONZ website for details.


Winter Classic, Wellington, Sunday 8th November, 3hrs
               This will be the 29th Winter Classic (except November is not Winter, but the annual event retains its name for old times sake). The main event is the Classic which is a physically and mentally demanding 2hr (or more) endurance orienteering event. The Rogaine run in conjunction with the Classic is a more relaxed Family friendly course. You are invited to join in. See the details on the Classic website.


Big Trig 2015, Saturday 28th November, Eastbourne, 4hr and 7hr
              Entries are open for the 2015 Big Trig organised by Orienteering Hutt Valley. It will be based in Eastbourne, will feature 7hr and 4hr Rogaines, and will take in the East Harbour Regional Park. See all the detail at the event website.


Ross Rummage 24hr Rogaine,Tasmania, 28th November
           You are all invited to Rogaining Tasmania’s “Ross Rummage” 24 Hour State Championship and concurrent 8 Hour event. The events will be held near the town of Ross in the Midlands region of Tasmania - roughly halfway between Hobart and Launceston. The terrain is a mixture of farmland and open Eucalypt woodland. Event entries are now open. More detailed event information can be found at http://rt.asn.au/rossrummage.



14th World Rogaining Championships (WRC2016), Australia 23-24 July 2016
              Please remember that registration for the 14th World Rogaining Championships in the MacDonnell Ranges east of Alice Springs begins 23rd October 2015. It will be at least another 5 years before the World Champs are this close to home so consider taking the opportunity to join in. Entries are limited so read the WRC2016 qualification tips if you are keen to be there. See the event website for more information.

Recent Events
Mt Outdoors Spring Rogaine, Saturday 12th September, 3, 6 & 12hrs
            There was a good turnout of nearly 300 people for the regular Highland Events Spring Rogaine run this year on the Bendigo Station of Shrek fame, just up HW8 from Cromwell.

    


Organiser Terry Davis reports:
           “A beautiful Central Otago day greeted participants of the Highland Events Rogaine on Bendigo Station. An extra 100 people turned up to make almost 300 participants on the day. There was some fearsome competition in the 3hr families and beginners category - Matt Bixley (Dunedin) was pretty stoked to win that one with his son. I think Family Faavae (Nelson/Queenstown) coming in second and the Fairmaids (Wanaka) in third were actually quite relaxed about it. Over 100 in this category which is great to see!

           The 6hr was taken out by Open mixed couple Phil Wood and Ange West from Queenstown with 1850 points. There were over 100 people in this category too. Quite a few women’s teams were using this as good training for the Spring Challenge a couple of weeks later. 

            The 12hr was officially won by George Whitla and Katie Bolt from Christchurch with 3120 points. Unofficially Chris and Emily Forne, also of Christchurch, scored 3140 but due to niggling injuries they chose to go unofficial and use MTBs on the 12hr course. There were only 50 odd competitors in the 12hr which was a shame because the day was so perfect and feedback about the course and the placement of the controls was very positive.

           Terry had a wee bit of excitement with one team of teenagers taking an extra couple of hours to finish the 6hr course after getting bushed with their MTBs in a particularly nasty gully. Luckily the boys did have cell phone coverage so there was good communication and no search was necessary though they did appreciate getting picked up from a road several kms from finish. Then another team in the 12hr got themselves turned around in the dark and were quite late coming out. Headlights could be seen way up the hill so a vehicle was sent on a retrieval mission and all ended happily. 

           Over $4000 was paid out to the local Tarras School for their catering services so that’s a worthy wee fundraiser for them too”. 

See the Highland Events website for complete results.
