T'WALK 2010, Canterbury, weekend 29th - 30th May
================================================
     Entries are open for the annual 24hr walk organised by the University of Canterbury
Tramping Club, the pioneer of long distance foot navigation events in NZ. This will be
the 44th running of T'Walk which is guaranteed to maintain the tradition of providing
24hrs of continuous fun. T'Walk differs from a Rogaine in that teams follow predetermined
loops of 5 to 8hrs duration, returning to the Hashhouse for food and a short rest before
setting out on the next loop. For details and online entry see the T'Walk website,
http://www.cutc.org.nz/twalk/. Shake a leg - T'Walk is less than 2 weeks away.


Mitre-10 Wanaka Rogaine, Bendigo Station (Wanaka region), Saturday 29th May
===========================================================================
      Ed Stevens and Terry Davis are organising one of their regular Rogaines, this time
in the stunning terrain of Bendigo Station, the home of Shrek the sheep. In addition to
the 12hr and 6hr foot events there will be a 6hr MTB/Foot rogaine and a "Grab A Map"
beginners event. See the website http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/rogaine for more
details. Get you entry in NOW - a late fee applies for entry after the 24th May.


Heights of Winter 2010, Canterbury, Saturday 26th June
=======================================================
     Entries are now open for the annual Heights of Winter - see the NZRA website
http://www.rogaine.org.nz/ for complete information. As usual there are 12 and 6hr
options. The area selected is just 45 minutes drive from Christchurch and features
rolling and gentle hills with height variation over the course of about 400m. Pat Bodger
has completed the course planning. Event organiser is Grant Hunter
(g.hunter@clear.net.nz).


Wellington Afterwork Series, Wellington, beginning 13th July
============================================================
      This series of 3hr afterwork events at intervals of 3 or 4 weeks in the Wellington
area begins 13th July and continues to early November. See the Maptalk calendar,
http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine, for details.


The Great Forest Rogaine, Rotorua, Saturday 6th November
==========================================================
       There will be an 8hr foot event and a 6hr MTB event utilising Rotorua's wonderful
and right up-to-date Whaka Forest map. This event is perfectly timed for a pre-World
Rogaine Champs blowout. See the event website http://www.cdorienteering.co.nz/.
