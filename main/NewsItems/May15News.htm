New webmaster
         We welcome Wayne Millow (wayne.millow@gmail.com), an active Christchurch rogainer, as  manager of the NZRA website (http://www.rogaine.org.nz/), taking over from Bill Kennedy. Bill established the website immediately after the formation of the Association in 2002.  The Committee is grateful to both Bill for his magnificent work and to Wayne for enabling Bill to take a break. Please address future website matters to Wayne. Bill continues as Newsletter editor and maintains the Membership list.

Events coming up
There are lots of events in the pipeline for the next couple of months with the first being T’Walk next weekend and the last being Heights of Winter on 27th June. Entries for T’Walk are full at 300 people. See the T'Walk website for all the detail.

Tutira Rogaine, Napier, Saturday 9th May; 3, 6 & 9 hrs
             Organised by George Christison, runner-up in the 2004 NZ Rogaine Championships, for the Tutira school. This event incorporates the Hawkes Bay Secondary schools champs. See the details and entry and follow facebook for updates. Entries will be accepted right up to the start of the event.

Geraldine Rogaine Autumn Muster (GRAM), Geraldine, Sunday 17th May; 3 & 6hr
           The Autumn Muster in the foothills near Geraldine, South Canterbury, is a fundraiser for the local High School. Mike Harding is the chief course planner. The terrain is challenging farmed hill country with some scattered scrub and native bush. See the Geraldine High School website for details. Entries close midnight 11th May.

Metlink City Safari, Wellington, Sunday 17th May; 3 & 6hr
          The City Safari is a unique urban Rogaine in which you can use public transport (bus, train, cable-car, ferry) to get partway to the checkpoints. The course is designed so that, by applying good strategy and using their heads, the smartest family team could beat the fastest athletic team. For more information see the event website www.citysafari.org.nz. Regular entries close 10th May but some late entries may be accepted.

The King - Shake Rattle and Roll Rogaine, Marlborough, Sunday 24th May, 3 hrs
             This 3hr Rogaine is hosted by the Marlborough Orienteering Club on classic rolling South Marlborough farmland featuring a mixture of pasture, scrub, forest and pines. There are great views from the higher points on the course with the highest elevation being just over 200m.  See The King - Shake Rattle and Roll website for details and entry.

Canterbury Secondary Schools Rogaine, Port Hills, Sunday 7th June, 3 hrs
           The Canterbury Secondary School Rogaine organised by Peninsula and Plains Orienteers (PAPO) is on Sunday 7th June. See the PAPO website for details.

Equestrian Rogaine, Canterbury Foothills, Sunday 14th June, 3 hrs
            Carol Fowler and Stuart Payne are organising another equestrian rogaine on Brooksdale farm 7km west of Springfield. See the information and entry form on Facebook.

Heights of Winter, Blythe Valley, Saturday 27th June, 12 hr & 6 hr
            The annual Heights of Winter Rogaine, organised this year by NZRA, is on Saturday 27th June. Entries will open on Thursday 28th May and will be limited. Detailed information will be available on the NZRA website shortly.

Recent Results
Snow Farm Rogaine, Pisa Range, Sunday 26th April



Organisers Ed Stevens and Terry Davis report: 
“Despite the very inclement weather, made more challenging by the altitude of 1500m, only two teams didn't show in the 12hr – quite amazing. Everyone came home with big smiles as they had the ultimate navigation experience with only brief glimpses of the features around them. There were some very good scores despite the conditions. A very big thumbs up for all those that endured the event.

Many six hour scores were right up there too with the highest score of 1490 by team "Where's Willy", Malcolm McLeod and Mark Williams. One of the most interesting scores was achieved by team "Look a Likes", Jenny Ferguson and Misa Klasoa, with 1240 points. Before they went out they asked me how to use the compass. Well done girls, 6hr female winners.

Unsurprisingly given the weather conditions numbers were down in the three hour family event but the teams that went out really enjoyed the challenge.  Well done to you all. This is as hard as it gets.

We had some every good feedback about the course.  All controls were in the right place and well placed over the course making planning a challenge. 

Terry and I have much fun planning and organising these events and really value your participation. Our next Rogaine is on 15 September and likely to be on Bendigo Station, the home of Shrek.  We will keep you well informed”.
There were 21 teams (50 people) in 12hr event, 32 teams (80 people) in the 6hr, and 11 teams (40 people) in the family 3hr Rogaine. For full results see the Highland Events website, http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/rogaine.

Championship Events

3rd Australasian Championships, 10th – 11th October 2015
        The 2015 Australasian championships are to be held in the beautiful Capertee National Park about 3½ hrs west of Sydney. This wonderful rogaining area offers open bushland, scenic creeks, river flats, magical vistas, rolling hills and one or two steeper hills. See the website for up-to-date information.

15th New Zealand Championships, 31st October 2015
              Subject to confirmation the 2015 New Zealand Championships will be in Marlborough on the weekend of 31st October. There will be shorter non-championship events in addition to the 24hr championship. Chief organiser is the experienced rogainer Mondo Kopua. There will be more information on the NZRA website in due course.

WRC2016 entry advice for Australia/New Zealand Rogainers
            The WRC2016 Organising Committee has provided preliminary information on the entry process for those planning to enter the 2016 World Championships in Central Australia. Entries will open in October and are limited to 800 persons. Since the event is very likely to be over-subscribed, it is important to understand how you can maximise the chance of your entry application being accepted. The key message is to enter early! Read the full advice.
