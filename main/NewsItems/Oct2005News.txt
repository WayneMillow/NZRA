New Zealand Rogaining Association (Inc.)
1st October 2005


Dear Rogainer,

Some coming events. For details and links to organisers' websites see the
NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz .

==================================================================
      CPIT SPRING MYSTERY MADNESS - Saturday 15th October
      Port Hills, Christchurch (from the Redcliffs School)
==================================================================
This is the first event of its kind to be organised by the CPIT's School of
Recreation. There will be 12hr, 6 hr, 3hr and family courses on a new
1:25000 map. Entries close Monday, 10th October. Download an entry form and
details from the CPIT website - http://www.cpit.ac.nz/subjects/recreation/
.




Terawhiti Rogaine - Saturday 29th October
=========================================
Organised by the Hutt Valley Orienteers in the spectacular windswept and
beautiful Wellington south-west coast area. There will be 6hr and 3hr
options and a 1:25000 map will be used. See the NZOF Rogaine calendar for
details. Entries close on 23rd October.


Hot Rocs Rogaine - Saturday 5th November
=========================================
8hr and 4hr events organised by the Rotorua Orienteering Club in the
Rotorua area. An entry form is available from http://www.rogaine.org.nz .
Entries close on Saturday 29th October.


Wellington Afterwork Series
============================
This series of afterwork 3hr events in the Wellington area started on the
17th August and runs through to 9th February. See the NZOF Rogaine calendar
for details.


Afterwork Series in Taranki
============================
A series of afterwork 3hr events starting Wednesday 19th October. See the
Egmont Orienteering website, http://www.geohire.co.nz/eoc.htm , for
details.


New Zealand Rogaining Championships
=====================================
The 2006 New Zealand Rogaining Championships (24hr), organised by the Hutt
Valley Orienteers, will be run the weekend of 18th-19th February in the
Waiarapa (near Wellington). Keep your eye on the event website,
http://www.tararua24.co.nz , for further details.

NZRA.
