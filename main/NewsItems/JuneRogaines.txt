            The Gorges Rogaine, Marlborough, Sunday 14th June
            =================================================

The 7th Marlborough Rogaine organised by the Marlborough Orienteering Club is "on stunning iconic
Marlborough hill country and farmland". There are 6 and 3 hr options. Entries close 9th June. Have
a look at the pictures, http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/Events/MOC2009Pics/index.html, and download
an entry form from the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz


             MORE ROGAINES IN JUNE
             ======================

(1) The "Lactic Turkey Nowhere Near Piha Rogaine" is on Saturday, 13th June near Helensville
(Auckland Region). Entries close Thursday, 11th June. For more information and an entry form go to
the Lactic Turkey website, http://www.lacticturkey.co.nz/ .

(2) The "Otari Rogaine" is an afterwork 3hr event in the Wellington region on Monday 15th June.
Check it out on the maptalk calendar, at http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine .

(3) "Belmont Rogaine" - again in the Wellington region. This is a 6hr weekend event on Sunday 28th
June. More information via the maptalk calendar,
http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine .



             New on the Calendar
             ===================

The "Annavale Attack and Amble", 4 and 3hr events in the Canterbury foothills, Springfield area,
on Sunday 16th August. More details will be announced shortly.
