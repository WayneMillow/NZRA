Christmas Twilight 2014
Port Hills, Christchurch, Wed. 17th December, 3hrs
Ho Ho Ho! It's time for Emily and Chris Forne’s annual Christmas Rogaine, after work from 6 - 9pm next Wednesday 17th December. Starting from a new exciting location up on the summit road near the Sign of the Bellbird the course will include some relatively unexplored areas recently opened post earthquake. For more information and entry details see http://christmas-rogaine.blogspot.co.nz/. There will be a brief prize giving with yummy cakes and other Christmas fare.


Early next Year

Hamels Haunts Enduro, 24hrs, Dunedin, 24th – 25th January 2015

Organised by Matt Bixley, matt.bixley@agresearch.co.nz or 021 046 9255, and based at the Waiora Scout Camp. Team size from two to “as many as you want”. See Matt's blog for the details and entry. And a bit further out prepare for:

Never Look Back, 13, 6 & 3hr, East Otago, Saturday 14th March
             Organised for Dunedin Rogaines/NZRA by Bob Cunninghame and team, there will be 13, 6 and 3hr courses, with the latter catering particularly for families and novices. Inland from Palmerston and Waikouaiti the terrain is similar to that for the "Golden Road" run in July 2014 -  relatively open farmland and tussock with rocks and scrub in places, plus some forest blocks. Schist and volcanic (basalt) outcrops and several distinct volcanic cones, with some steep gullies and deeply incised valleys, characterise the area. Download and distribute the event flyer. Contact: Bob Cunninghame, signalhill@xtra.co.nz, ph. (03) 473 8330.

Recent Results

Big Trig 2014, Western Akatarawas, Saturday 29th November
             Close to 100 Rogainers were out and about enjoying a fine day on the fringes of the Akatarawas Ranges near Wellington on Saturday 29th November. The rugged 9 hr option was won by the team of Jamie Stewart and Casper Harmer, while Gillian and Malcolm Ingham took out the less rugged 6 hr. Shane Wilson and James Watson were first in the 3hr with a score that challenged most of the 6hr teams. The event was organised by O Hutt Valley. Full results are available on the Big Trig website.


14th New Zealand Championships, Nevis Valley, 6th-7th December
              The remote Nevis Valley, south-west of Cromwell, was a magnificent venue for the New Zealand Champs hosted on the Ben Nevis and Craig-Roy Stations.

Two teams, Chris Forne/Tim Farrant and Greig Hamilton/Georgia Whitla, cleared the course. Competition was hot amongst the top four teams which were separated by only 120 points. Further down the pecking order, 5th to 10th places were even closer with only 90 points covering them.

There was general agreement amongst the participants that the terrain was awesome even if the hills were unrelenting. One competitor commented:

“.... there was heaps of climbing and the controls were further apart and big tussocks made it hard work some of the time, but not always. Really, compared to some other rogaines it was pretty good going.  .... navigation at night was good with the moon a big help even behind clouds.”


Have a look on Facebook for further chatter and a few photos. Full results, and the routes taken by each team on a map overlay in Google Earth, are available on the NZRA website.

NZRA President Mondo Kopua who competed with his wife Lisa in the 12hr event commended the Highlands Event team for hosting the NZ Championships which is a massive undertaking. “I was impressed with the location, logistics, the courses (only saw the 24 and 12hr ones) and it was obvious that the team had put in a major effort to host the champs and other supporting rogaines. Well done to everyone and I hope you all enjoy some deserved R&R over the next few weeks.”

Thanks to all who played a part in organising this great event. Particular thanks to Ed and Terry of Highland Events for arranging the venue and doing most of the fieldwork. And thanks to Pete Squires who was the NZRA representative and the course vetter.
