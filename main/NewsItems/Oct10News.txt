
New Zealand Rogaining Association (Inc.)
10th October 2010


Dear Rogainer,

     As you all must be aware by now (unless you have been hiding under the 
proverbial) the big event coming up is the World Championships in Cheviot 
20th-21st November. Preparation is tracking along nicely although there seems to 
be an ever growing list of things to do in the leadup to the event. We'll get there!


Remember the 8hr Great Forest Rogaine takes place in Rotorua on Saturday 6th 
November. This is being billed as an ideal event for pre-WRC training but we hope 
that many non-WRC people will see it as a challenging event in which to partake. 
Website is http://www.madeventz.co.nz/orionadventure.


Maybe even more challenging will be the longer 12hr Highland Events Rogaine in 
Wanaka over Labour weekend. The course for this is relatively flat and fast with 
elevation variation only 300m to 550m. Enter now at 
http://www.highlandevents.co.nz. There is a 6hr option as well. On the Friday, at 
the Albert Town Tavern from 7pm to 8pm, there will be a navigation and map reading 
tutorial conducted by Phil Wood. This is a great opportuntity to find out how the 
experts like Phil navigate so accurately.   


These and other events coming up are listed below. 

And, by all accounts, the Ophir Awesome E.P.I.C. was indeed awesome. Results are 
at http://homepages.ihug.co.nz/~Sbmcleod/

R&R Sport Taylors Mistake Series, Port Hills, Christchurch, from 20th October
=============================================================================
     This series of 3 x 3hr afterwork Rogaines on successive Wednesdays is being 
organised once again by experienced adventure racers Nora Audra and Ian Edmond. 
There will be a 1hr Family event as well. Don’t miss the opportunity to be out 
there experiencing the highlights of the Port Hills. An entry form is available at 
http://www.madeventz.co.nz/tmrogaine


Highland Events Rogaine Heaven, Wanaka, Sat 23rd Oct (Labour Weekend), 12 & 6hr
===============================================================================
     Organised by Ed Stevens and based at the Albert Town Tavern, the course will 
be quite fast and also ideal for the beginner rogainer. The course features large 
mainly flat areas with low open Kanuka scrub and there are some slightly hilly 
parts going from 275m to 570m. See http://www.highlandevents.co.nz for further 
details.


Wellington Afterwork Series, Wellington, final event 2nd November
==================================================================
     The final event in this series of 3hr afterwork events in the Wellington area 
is on 2nd November. See the Maptalk calendar, 
http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine, for details. 


The Great Forest Rogaine, Rotorua, Saturday 6th November, 8hr
=============================================================
     An 8hr foot event and a 6hr MTB event with the same controls for both. 
Utilising Rotorua's wonderful and right up-to-date Whaka Forest Map, this event is 
perfectly timed for a pre-World Rogaine Champs blowout, and will also feature as a 
part of the World Champion Team Orion's AR Training Weekend being held in and 
around Rotorua. The event website is now up and running and provides online entry 
at http://www.madeventz.co.nz/orionadventure. Contact is Mark Copeland, 
copeland@copelandlaw.co.nz.


Urban Series, New Plymouth, begins 10th November
================================================
     This series of eleven afterwork events begins on the 10th November and runs 
weekly through until 9th March except for a break over the Christmas period. See 
the Maptalk calendar, http://www.maptalk.co.nz/events/default.cfm?t=Rogaine, for 
details. 


World Championships, Cheviot, weekend 20th-21st November, 24hr
==============================================================
     The organisation of the World Championships is on target and proceeding 
steadily. There are participants from 22 countries  (http://wrc2010.org.nz) but, 
as you would expect, Australian and New Zealand entries dominate. Entries are closed


2011 Australian Championships, Tasmania, weekend 22nd-23rd January, 24hr
========================================================================
     To be hosted by Rogaining Tasmania in the northern midlands of Tasmania. The 
course will be set by experienced rogainers John Brock, Darryl Smith, Karen Pedley 
and Nigel Davies. The terrain will be a mixture of open plains (farmland) and 
steeper lightly wooded hills. See the event website, 
http://www.arc2011.rt.asn.au/. This is a qualifying event for the 10th World 
Championships, Czech Republic, 31st August 2012.
