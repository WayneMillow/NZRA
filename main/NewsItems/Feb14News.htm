NZRA restructuring – note from Secretary, Grant Hunter
            At its general meeting on 2 December the Association’s committee considered how it can better meet future needs. While the Association has achieved much for rogaining since it was formed in 2002, it wants to better build and support rogaining more evenly across the country, at least where there is a demand. See the minutes of the December meeting (near the top left of the Home page on the NZRA website).

Immediate requirements include (1) a more effective way of communicating, at a range of scales (regional to national), as an Association than our rather tired and limiting mix of committee meetings and linear emails,  (2) a framework for investing Association funds to promote rogaining on a sustainable, pan-regional basis. 

An obvious next move is a proposal to the AGM in February that the Association commits to a series of changes along the lines outlined in the December minutes. Watch the NZRA website in the lead-up to the AGM for details of this proposal.

However, making change depends on having people in the regions willing to offer leadership, preferably with rejuvenation of energy and ideas from younger rogainers.  Please think how you may engage.  Until we set up a better way of communicating, I encourage you to pass on your views and ideas to either myself (g.hunter@clear.net.nz) or the Association’s President Pete Squires (petersquires@ihug.co.nz), or start a conversation on our Facebook page.

Grant Hunter, Secretary


Events to end of March

Waitangi Day Rogaine, Christchurch Port Hills, Thursday 6th February
For the second event in this season’s Canterbury Rogaine series, organised by Tim Farrant and team, there will be 3hr and 90min options. For further information and online entry see the Canterbury Series website http://canterburyrogaine.com/.

Whangamata Waitangi Rogaine, Whangamata, Coromandel, Saturday 8th February
           Ryan Thompson and friends are organising a Rogaine in Whangamata for the Saturday following Waitangi Day. With 3hr and 90 minute options the Rogaine will be based around the beach, parks, coastal tracks and urban pathways of Whangamata. For detailed information see the event website at http://www.whangamataevents.co.nz/rogaine/.

Suburbs to Surf, Dunedin, Sunday 2nd March
            Organised by Tim Jowett and located on the rural-urban fringe of the Otago Peninsula with about a third of the map in a suburban area. There will be 6hr & 3hr foot and 3hr MTB options. This is a "shoe-string" rogaine so for controls competitors will write down answers related to features at each control site visited. The event base will be at the Guthrie Pavilion, Bayfield High School, 2 Shore Street, Andersons Bay. For more information see the information sheet on the NZRA website. Contact is Tim Jowett (Jowti231@gmail.com, ph. 027 656 7251).

Equestrian and mini-Foot Rogaines, Whitecliffs, Canterbury, Sunday 9th March
         The Equestrian event for teams of 2 to 4 on horseback will be 2.5 hours, registration from noon. Pre-entry is necessary for the equestrian rogaine. Check out the flyer. More information and an entry form will be available soon at http://www.ecess.org/.
         The mini-Foot rogaine for teams or individuals will be 90 minutes, registration from 9 am. Enter on the day, but an email of number entering to Stuart Payne at oldhat@actrix.co.nz will guarantee map(s). Organisers: Stuart Payne & Carol Fowler for Eyre Community Environmental Safety Society.

The Golden Road Rogaine, Otago, Saturday 29th March
          Organised by Bob Cunninghame and team, there will be a 13-hour “Classic”, a 6-hour “Introduction” and a 3-hour “Family Special”. The terrain, west of Waikouaiti and about an hour’s drive from Dunedin, is relatively open farmland and tussock with rocks and scrub in places, plus some forest blocks. Some valleys are steep sided but reasonable routes that minimise the ups and downs are possible. Mark the Golden Road in your calendar now. Look for more information on the NZRA website soon.
    General Enquiries:  Bob Cunninghame, signalhill@xtra.co.nz, ph. (03) 473 8330.

Geraldine Rogaine Autumn Muster (GRAM), Geraldine region, Sunday 30th March
        Organised by the Geraldine High School PTA in the South Canterbury foothills at or near the location of the 2013 Heights of Winter event. There will be two options (3 and 4½ hours), with categories for school children and adults. Planner is Mike Harding & Controller is Stuart Payne. More information closer to the event. Contact is mikeharding@ihug.co.nz

Championships coming up

12th World Rogaining Championships
Black Hills, South Dakota, 16th - 17th August 2014
There are many places still available. As of mid-January the total of 383 entrants is well short of the 1000 limit. Thirty-one (31) Kiwis have registered their interest so far. The event website and blog is at http://peakassurance.net/. See the list of entrants.

Australasian Championships (Goldfields Bonanza)
Victoria, Sat-Sun 11th - 12th October 2014
           Set in an old goldfields area in the Castlemaine region, the terrain is a complex and challenging mixture of spurs and gullies only 1.5hrs drive from Melbourne. There will be a 6hr event as well as the championship 24hr Rogaine. Website is at http://vra.rogaine.asn.au/arc14/.

New Zealand Championships
Pisa Range, Otago, Sat-Sun 8th - 9th November 2014
          The 1st New Zealand Championships back in 1995 was organised by Ken & Anitra Dowling at the Snow Farm in the Pisa Range near Wanaka. We are set to return there for the 2014 champs. Chief organisers are the experienced team of Ed Stevens and Terry Davis (Highland Events) who are well known for their twice yearly Wanaka Rogaines. Pencil this date in your calendars and look for more information on the NZRA website further down the track.
