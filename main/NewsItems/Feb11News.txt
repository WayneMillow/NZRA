Annual General Meeting, 21st February, Christchurch
===================================================
The NZRA AGM will be held Monday 21st February, 7.30 pm at 6 Harrods Court, 
Christchurch.

The Agenda will include:
       1. minutes from AGM 2010 and matters arising
       2. financial report
       3. election of officers
       4. transitioning to a more truly national organisation (recap of findings at 
committee meeting held 7 February)
       5. other general business

For those living outside of Christchurch and having a compelling interest in attending 
this meeting, NZRA will consider helping with travel costs. Please raise this with the 
Secretary, g.hunter@clear.net.nz.

If anyone has items to add to the agenda please send them to the secretary, Grant 
Hunter, g.hunter@clear.net.nz

"Eve of the Harvest"
2011 New Zealand Championships, Marlborough 19th - 20th March
=============================================================
Entries  for the 2011 New Zealand Rogaine Champs open on 12th February. The event will 
be in Marlborough with Mondo Kopua as chief planner so a great event is assured. We 
hope to see you there wandering around the country-side under a full moon.

In addition to the 24hr championship there will be a "15 in 24 hours" event. In this 
format, very popular in Australia and offered for the first time in New Zealand, 
participants can spend any time they like on the course up to a maximum of 15 hours 
within the 24 hour event duration. For the other 9 hours (or more if desired) people 
rest, socialise, eat and sleep.

For the 2011 Champs the Junior grade will be for people under 23 on the first day of 
competition (under 20 in previous championships). The other grades of Open, Vet, and 
Supervet are unchanged. Remember, though, that Rogaining is as much about having fun 
and socialising as about all-out competition.

For further information and to enter see the website: 
http://rogaine.org.nz/nzchamps2011/. Entries close 5pm Thursday, 10th March.


Australian Champs 2011, Tasmania, 22nd/23rd January  - results
==============================================================
Tim Farrant and Elisha Nuttal placed 1st U23, 1st MO and 2nd Overall in the recent 
Australian Championships in Tasmania. Aged 18 and 21 they are the youngest team ever 
to win the Mens Open. See Tim's account of the event in the results section of the 
NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz/ . 

Other teams in the New Zealand contingent also performed with distinction. The 
complete list of NZ placings is:

    Tim Farrant, Elisha Nuttall         1st MO, 1st U23, 2nd overall
    Emma and Guy de Lacey               2nd XV, 11th overall
    Julie Gordon, Debbie Mansfield      1st WV, 18th overall
    Mark Struthers, Dennis Litt         4th MSV, 23rd overall
    Colin Dixon, Douglas Woods          12th MV, 28th overall
    Pete Squires, Anne & Bill Kennedy   1st XSV, 30th overall
    Jim Nugteren, Helen Nugteren        2nd XSV, 33rd overall
    Dennis de Monchy, Rob Jessop        24th MO, 40th overall – bad blister problems
    Chris Dixon, Caroline Elliott       4th WV, 52nd overall

There were 97 teams in the event. For full results see the Tasmania website, 
http://www.arc2011.rt.asn.au/ .

R&R Sport Taylors Mistake Rogaine Series, Christchurch, begins Wednesday 2nd March
==================================================================================
    The popular R&R Sport Taylors Mistake Rogaine Series organised by Nora Audra and 
Ian Edmond, and which runs for 5 successive Wednesdays, begins Wednesday 2nd March.  
The series relocates to a different spot around the Port Hills each Wednesday. So grab 
some friends and get a team together for some super-fun Wednesday evenings! A 1hr 
Family event will be run too.

Information and an entry form are available at http://www.madeventz.co.nz/tmrogaine . 
Teams can enter on the night, but they must pre-enter to be guaranteed a map!
