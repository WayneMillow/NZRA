Annavale Attack and Amble, Canterbury, Sunday 14th August – entries close 9th August
            Entries for the Attack and Amble close next Tuesday, 9th August. This event is a navigational adventure in the Springfield region of the Canterbury foothills. It is organised in support of Darfield HS Rural College by Stuart Payne, who brought you Heights of Winter this year, and Carol Fowler. The 4-hr Attack is intended to be for competitive people and the 3-hr Amble for the more recreational, but who cares – both will offer a delightful romp in the winter sun (we hope). A flier and an entry form are available on the Darfield High School website, http://www.darfield.school.nz/curriculum/special-programmes/rural-college/.
 
 
More Events
 
Canterbury Rogaine Series, 1st event Coopers Creek, Saturday 27th August
            The Canterbury Series consists of three events planned for 27th August, 17th September and 8th October. On each occasion rogaines of 4.5hrs and a 3hr school/social event will be offered.

The events are suitable for participants of all ages and abilities but the school competition is a special feature of the series. The series seeks to promote rogaining and to further junior development through free entry to all high school students. Organisers Tim Farrant, Jess Collingwood and Rowan Nugteren will be running training workshops at local schools leading up to the rogaines. Email Jess at info@canterburyrogaine.com to nominate your school.

The Canterbury Series is a fundraiser for junior rogaining in Canterbury. Proceeds from the events will fund travel grants for local juniors to compete at domestic and international rogaining events.  For more information about the series, and to enter online, see the website http://www.canterburyrogaine.com/
 

25th Winter Classic, Wellington, Sunday 21st August

Two new Rogaines run in Auckland and Dunedin
 
The Bethells 12hr Rogaine organised by Alastair McDowell, and the Hamel’s Haunts organised by Matt Bixley, in the Auckland and Dunedin regions respectively, have been successfully run.
 
Alastair tried an “upside down” format starting at midnight Friday and finishing at midday Saturday. The “upside down” format is unusual in New Zealand but is more common in Australia. Alastair reports “… all the teams seemed to arrive back at base looking shattered, but exhilarated by the experience of going hard all through the night, and walking into the new day”. Everybody, including the organisers, had a lot of fun and all are looking forward to more of Alastair’s rogaines in the future.
 
Matt utilised a more tried and proven format with 3hr and 6hr events in daylight hours. Again he had an enthusiastic bunch of participants with some subjecting their bodies to gross punishment. As Matt says “… blood and carnage galore. Pretty sure I said at the briefing that no bush bashing or fighting gorse was required. Most blood was seen on those who have links with Athletics!!!!! One was even seen up a tree and I know I didn’t place one [a checkpoint] up there. Anyone going out with Kevin Duggan, make sure you’ve got the map”.
 
See Alistair’s and Matt’s interesting reports available at the Results page on the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz
