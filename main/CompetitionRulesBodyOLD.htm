Details of the rules of Rogaining can vary from event to event. The common theme is that teams stick together, find checkpoints quickly and efficiently by good map reading and navigation, and must return to base by the event finish time or be penalised. Within this concept very specific rules have been developed for major championship events, as can be seen by the Rules of Competition.

For most rogaines in New Zealand we need more flexible rules often varying from event to event. For our major events we will encourage the adoption of the Rules of Competition but perhaps with more tolerance of teams that become split through injury or tiredness, who instead of being "disqualified" will become "informal" and may continue the event.


The Rules of Competition are part of a set of Technical Regulations that applies to both competitors and event organisers. The regulations are composed of three parts

  • Preamble
  • Rules of Competition
  • Technical Standards
The regulations have been developed to introduce a standard based on wide experience that will enhance the sport in general and allow for a competitive element to the sport that is based on fairness. They may however be adjusted for specific events.
