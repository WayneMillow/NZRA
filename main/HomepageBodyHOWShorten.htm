Heights of Winter Change Alert

It may not surprise that after days of flood-inducing rain in North Canterbury, capping an already wet season, we are making changes to Heights of Winter this Saturday. The specific concerns are (1) saturated soils both in the car parking/catering area and on the course, and (2) flood flows in Blythe Stream. We expect neither of these to self-correct by Saturday.

Rather than cancel, we will go ahead with both events, but please note the following changes. Firstly to start times and duration – in each case these are (1) reduced duration, (2) delayed start, (3) earlier finish.

As well as this we expect to (1) delete some check points, related to flood risk, and (2) limit crossings of the main stream that bisects the map to motor bridges only. Details on the day.

You should also be aware that much of the course will be very boggy underfoot. We are now unlikely to erect a marquee, so that covered space will be more limited. We will continue to offer primo food in the woolshed, and there is a prospect of a bonfire!

If you are able to double-up with cars (e.g. two teams) and/or come in a 4wd, these would help to keep parking compact.

If you decide to withdraw your whole team because of these changes, we would look at a modest good-faith refund, depending on how finances look.

While we regret these changes, we consider it a durable response to the conditions – all part of the challenge of rogaining!

Thank you, organising team.

The Golden Road Rogaine
Eastern Otago, Saturday 12th July, 13, 6 & 3hr family options
      Organised by Bob Cunninghame and team, there will be a 13-hour “Classic”, a 6-hour “Introduction” and a 3-hour “Family Special”. The terrain, west of Waikouaiti and a one-hour drive from Dunedin, is relatively open farmland and tussock with rocks and scrub in places, plus some forest blocks. Schist and volcanic (basalt) outcrops and several distinct volcanic cones, with some steep gullies and deeply incised valleys, characterise the area. See the information sheet for further details.
General Enquiries:  Bob Cunninghame, signalhill@xtra.co.nz, ph. (03) 473 8330.
