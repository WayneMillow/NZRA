Australian Events
Rogaining originated in Australia where clubs have regular events and generally welcome Kiwi participation. For a list of all events in Australia see the Australian Rogaine Association website. You will find contacts for all clubs in Australia there too.
Orienteering Events
Orienteering offers a great way to get in some map-reading practice. Events are much shorter (about 1 hr) and you follow a fixed course on an enlarged map (scale typically 1:10,000) with individual start times. Events are regular and cheap, and are run by a number of orienteering clubs throughout the country. Check the contacts in Relevant Links. Most events have someone with the specific task of explaining the sport to beginners, and you will be made most welcome.

For a schedule of events in USA and Canada see the North American Rogaine Calendar and Sage Orienteering & Rogaining Club.
