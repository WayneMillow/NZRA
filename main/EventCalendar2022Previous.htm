Sat 12 & Sun 13 Mar
Duration: 90 minute
Location: Kaiapo Faultline extended		 Katoa Po 2022 night relays and "team explorer"
Katoa Po is a Maori translation for "all night" and the event is the annual interclub night relay hosted by the Taupo Orienteering Club. The first relay was held at the end of January 1981 but nowadays is usually in March when dusk is earlier but the weather still generally good. Teams are made up of seven members, of mixed ages and abilities who contest for the Owl trophy or five members who compete in the first five legs for the Po Kerukeru trophy.
The Katoa Po Explorer is a teams event that has been designed for adventure racers wanting to practise their night navigation and teamwork. This event will comprise a three hour rogaine on Saturday night and a 90 minute rogaine on Sunday morning for teams of two or four. The course has been designed by experienced adventure racers, but will be set for a range of abilities, from beginners to expert. See Taupo Orienteering for more details.

Wednesdays 16, 23 Feb, 2, 9, 16, 23 Mar
Duration: 60 minutes
Location: Locations around Bay of Plenty		 Summer Nav Series
A great series of early evening rogaines in Tauranga and Rotorua for you to enjoy. Check out the link below for locations OBOP Summer Nav is a series of 60 minutes rogaines set in local parks and streets. In a rogaine you try and visit as many controls as you can in the time allowed, in this case 60 minutes. You will receive a clip card when you register and you punch in the box which corresponds to the number on the control, proving that you have visited the control. When you register you will also receive a map which will have the controls marked on it. If you have never done an orienteering event before ask one of the event officials to give you some pointers. You will be able to start within a time window. There will be no mass starts this year with the Covid situation. You will be responsible for recording your start time and finish time, and we also ask you to tally up your points on the bottom of your clip card before hanging up your clip card on the results line or handing it to the event official. You can do the rogaine individually, as a family, or with other friends. You can go as easy or hard as you like. See Orienteering Bay of Plenty for more details.

Sat & Sun May 14th-15th
Duration: up to 24 hrs
Location: Shhhh! Secret		 TWALK
If you talk to anyone who has been rogaining for a few years they will be able to tell you about TWALK. It is legendary.
TWALK is a twenty-four hour orienteering event. It is split into five legs, each around 15-20 kilometres long. Each leg has around 10 - 20 controls, which will be hidden on features marked on a map and described by a cryptic clue. You will be given an event map at the beginning of leg one and a list of cryptic clues.
The first leg is compulsory, as it leads from where you are dropped off to the Hash House. From there, competitors may choose to participate in as many or few legs as they choose, leaving when they like (there is a compulsory 30 minute break between legs). Legs two - five form loops which end up back at the Hash House, and can be cut short if required.
A key tradition of TWALK is dressing up! Come up with a costume theme for your team. The best dressed team gets a prize! Details on TWALK - None of this silly sleeping as it becomes available.
