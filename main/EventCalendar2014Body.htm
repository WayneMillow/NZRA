Sunday 23rd February
Duration: 2hr
Location: Rotorua		 MTB Adventure Quest
The MTB Adventure Quest rogaine is back again this year as part of the Rotorua Bike Festival. The event will start and finish at the main Waipa State Mill Road car park, just out of Rotorua, and you can also check out the Waipa Bike Fair which will be happening at the same time.

The MTB Adventure Quest is a fun bike-based navigation challenge where teams of 2 to 5 people try to collect as many check points as they can within 2 hours. Teams can earn bonus points by completing special mystery activities along the way - think Amazing Race style! The event is suitable for families with kids (including the littlies under 5 years old) through to Olympic cyclists. Every child who competes will receive a special certificate. There will also be lots of spot prizes from Macpac to give away.

To enter and for more information check out the Orienteering Bay of Plenty website. Online entries close on February 19th.

Sunday 4th May
Duration: 3hrs
Location: Marlborough
 "King of the Castle"
Located within 30 minutes drive from Blenheim Town Centre the King of the Castle Rogaine will be held in classic rolling South Marlborough landscape. Controls will be located from sea level to a max of 280m. Lots of open land with gullies, defined ridges and spurs, and some scrubby areas and plantations to offer a challenge for all skill levels. Beaches and great views! See the event website for details and entry. Late fee applies for entries after 8am Thursday 1st May.
      Newly elected NZRA President Mondo Kopua looks forward to people coming to Marlborough for this event in another stunning location and notes, "We are fortunate to have so many areas that are ideal for rogaining and with the support and generosity of the landowners and local communities we get great pleasure out of hosting rogaines in our region."

Sat/Sun 17th/18th May
Duration: 24hrs
Location: Canterbury
 T'Walk 2014
On yet again!! This will be the 48th running of this iconic event, organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club, the pioneer of long distance foot navigation events in NZ. It differs from a Rogaine in that the order of checkpoint visits is somewhat predetermined. This year T'Walk is two days after the full moon, it is great fun and a "not to be missed" event. See the T'Walk website for details. Entries open Monday 28th April and close Monday 12th May.
Sunday 18th May
Duration: 3 & 6hr options
Location: Wellington		 Metlink City Safari
In the City Safari teams will be racing by foot, train, bus, cable car or ferry to find checkpoints spread at unique and interesting spots throughout the Wellington region. The course has been designed to reward smart use of public transport which adds a whole new level of strategy into the sport of rogaining. Live scoring will enable you to know how you are doing during the event (using your smartphone), and friends and family will be able to watch your progress from a big screen at the start finish line. For more detail see www.citysafari.org.nz.

Saturday 12th July
Duration: 13, 6 & 3hr family
Location: Otago		 The Golden Road Rogaine
Organised by Bob Cunninghame and team, there will be a 13-hour “Classic”, a 6-hour “Introduction” and a 3-hour “Family Special”.
