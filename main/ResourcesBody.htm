We have separate sections below for participants and for the organisers of rogaines. We hope to continually improve these resources over a period of time. Please let us have your suggestions for additions or changes.

For the record: World Champs 2010 (Cheviot) website.


Resources for Participants

  • How Rogaining Works

  • A beginners guide to least painful rogaining by Grant Hunter.

  • Novice Pack - this is an Introduction to Rogaining that can be downloaded from the Victorian Rogaining Association (Australia). Although it is written for Australian conditions much of the content is applicable to NZ.

  • Using Navlight - our very own electronic scoring system developed by Pete Squires (100KB PDF).

  • Rules of Rogaining - these are the official rules which may be varied for particular events.

  • Code of Conduct - governing the conduct of participants.

  • WRC Generic Entry Criteria (June 2013) - the generic entry criteria and process for the World Rogaining Championships. Specific details may be altered somewhat for a particular World Championships.

  • Equipment Chat - in draft form with lots more to come later. Includes discussion on clothing, torches, and compasses for now.



Resources for Organisers

If you are thinking of organising a Rogaine, and you wish to take advantage of the advice and assistance that NZRA can offer, then the first thing to do is to talk with the NZRA Regional Contact for your area.

We have assembled a range of resources and suggestions to assist you in running your own Rogaine. We hope to continually improve this resource over a period of time. Please let us have your suggestions for additions or changes. If any item requires further explanation then please consult the author whose name is noted on the particular article.

Currently available are:

  • RogaineSCORE (Navlight software) - is being updated. The latest version will be available from here when it is ready.

  • Promotional Pictures - Photos by Mike Chappell

  • HOW2012 planning guide - compiled by Peter Squires (34KB PDF)

  • Checkpoint planning and placement - compiled by Grant Hunter (26KB PDF).

  • How to Organise a Rogaine compiled by Peter Squires.

  • Land Access and Community Relationships - Grant Hunter.

  • Rules of Rogaining - these rules include Technical Standards for organisers.

  • Job List for Volunteers compiled by Peter Squires.

  • Travel Expenses Claim Form
