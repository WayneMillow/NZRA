A series of afterwork 3hr events at roughly fortnightly intervals, starting 13th February, in the Wellington area. See the NZOF Rogaine calendar for details.
Sat 3rd - Sun 4th March
Duration: 24hrs
Location: Marlborough		 New Zealand Rogaine Championships
The 2007 championships will be held in Marlborough with Alan Holdaway as the chief organiser assisted by Mondo Kopua. Both Alan and Mondo are experienced rogainers who have competed internationally, and they both have a history of organising rogaines. We are assured of a quality event. Details, pictures and entry form are available. Entries close 24th February. See the Entries so far.  Transport is available from Picton & Blenheim.

Sat 5th - Sun 6th May
Duration: 24hrs
Location: Canterbury
 T'Walk
This will be the 41st running of this event, organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club, the pioneer of long distance foot navigation events in NZ. It differs from a Rogaine in that teams follow a fixed route. A "not to be missed" event. Consult the T'Walk website for information and entry form.
Sunday 20th May
Duration: 3hr & 6hr options
Location: Wellington		 Wellington City Safari
The City Safari allows you to use Public Transport: buses and trains! You can use your head instead of your legs to get partway to the checkpoints. For more information see the event website www.citysafari.org.nz.
Wednesdays 23rd May,
     30th May & 6th June
Duration: 3 hrs
Location: Christchurch		 Taylors Mistake Rogaine Series
This is an after-work series organised by Lara Prince and others. Each event will focus on a different part of the area bounded by Godley Head, Lyttleton and Sumner. The series will as always feature lovely moonlight nights, running on wild hills way above the city, mysterious pathways down to seaside coves, and exploring around in giant wartime bunkers. Download details and an entry form (150KB PDF). Registration from 5:30, start time 6:30. Entries Monday before each event.

No Wairewa Rogaine this year
Kia ora koutou,
For all those of you who may have been looking forward to the Maori Language Week Rogaine at Wairewa happening again this year, I have to unfortunately say that it won't happen this year. As you probably heard last year the wharenui (meeting house) is getting a major overhaul. This will not be completed in time for this year. However, we will be keen to run the event again next year. So, look out for it during Maori Language Week 2008 (usually the last week in July).
Na, Bruce Meder

Sunday 2nd December
   (Note the new date)

Duration: 7hrs
Location: Canterbury high country		 Korowai Office Hours Rogaine
Come and join the New Zealand Rogaining Association for a pre-Christmas rogaine on the fringe of the Canterbury high country. You’ll get to visit your Korowai-Torlesse Conservation Park. This is a seven-hour event, just like a short day at the office. There are no age or gender grades – all teams are created equal. If you can’t get a leave pass so close to Christmas, make up a family team and bring ‘em all along. Further details and entry form are available. Entries close 23rd November.
