Saturday 28th and Sunday 29th April
Duration: up to 24 hours
Location: TBA		 TWALK 2018
Twalk is back again for 2018! The annual event which is run by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club is a twenty-four hour orienteering event in Christchurch, starting on Saturday, 28 April 2018. The event is split in to five legs, each around 15 to 20 kilometres long. Each leg has around 10 to 20 controls, which will be hidden on features marked on the map and described by a cryptic clue. The first leg is compulsory, as it leads from where you are dropped off to the Hash House. From there, competitors may choose to participate in as many or few legs as they choose, leaving when they like (there is a compulsory 30 minute break between legs). Legs two to five form loops which end up back at the Hash House, and can be cut short if required.

Orienteering Hutt Valley are looking for a new event manager. Is this you? Orienteering Hutt Valley have been designing fun family navigation events for over 30 years. Sunday 23rd September
Duration: 2 & 3 hours
Location: Paraparaumu		 Kapiti Crews Mark II
Following the crewsy MTB rogaine in May, the rest of the Kapiti Coast is crying out to be explored! We've mapped the zone from Kapiti Road to the Waikanae River, and included a brand new area for which park proposals are now out for comment. So another 2hr MTB rogaine for bikes on the flat Kapiti coast. 3hrs for feet, or slower riders. Shorter options by arrangement. This is a team event, but solos are fine. Approx 50 controls chosen, we're just honing the score values now! Expect to ride quiet streets and lanes. Leafy lifestyle block culs-de-sac. The shared path along the new expressway. The Waikanae River trail. The proposed Otaraua Park. Tracks through the dunes. (Check the tide tables, you may be able to short-cut along the beach.) Ordinary bikes will be fine, hard-core mountain-bikers won't generate any adrenalin whatsoever. Kids with a "road sense" welcome, under Y9 must be in a family group. Pre-register by Wed 19 Sep at the website above using the "Laid-back Daytime Rogaine" form. Pay on the day, adult rates $20 less $5 for pre-registering and another $5 off for orienteering club members. (You can join OHV on the same website.) Juniors Y9 to age 20 half price. Under Y9 free. Enter on the day subject to map printing. Put it in your diary. We don't have a publicity or a training officer - use this to recruit your friends. If you plan to do 3hrs, maps will be available from 9:30 for a 10am start. For 2hrs or less maps will be available from 10 for a 10:30am start. Note that we envisage a fast rider might mop up all the controls hence 2hrs for bikes; but no objection to slower riders (and footies) doing 3hrs. This is a laid-back event after all.:-)) See Orienteering Rogaine Calendar.

Wednesday 5th December
Duration: 2 hours
Location: Christchurch, Victoria Park		 Christmas Twilight Rogaine
Ho! Ho! Ho! The original Christmas Twilight Rogaine returns in 2018! Anybody that has a moderate level of fitness can participate. A high level of navigation ability is not required. Teams travel at their own pace and anyone from children to grandparents can experience the personal satisfaction that comes from cross-country navigation at their own level of competition and comfort. Team members stay within earshot of each other. The best thing about rogaining is the social aspect. Everyone starts at the same time, and generally finishes at the same time, it is just the amount of ground that has been covered by teams that differs. Rogaining can be held in city environments or in farmland and bush, and you get to visit parts of the country you never knew existed. Check out facebook for more details.
