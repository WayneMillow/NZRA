What is a
Rogaine?		 A Rogaine is a cross-country navigational and strategy race on foot, designed for people of all abilities. Each participant is given a map with about 40 - 50 circles marked on it showing where "checkpoints" are located. You may visit as many or as few checkpoints as you wish, in any order and by any route, within your course time limit. Visits to checkpoints are recorded on a score punch card.

Some experience with a map and compass is helpful. Much of the pleasure and challenge of a Rogaine is the strategy of planning an optimum route to suit your team's ability.

Scoring points depends on the number of checkpoints visited. Each checkpoint is worth a number of points depending on distance and difficulty. There are stiff penalties for returning after the time deadline with 10-point deductions for every minute late.

Detailed rules exist, but basically:

You must travel together, within 50 metres of one another at all times. A team shall demonstrate compliance with this requirement to any official or other team on request.

All team members must visit the checkpoints and shall simultaneously approach to within 20 metres, and within sight, of each checkpoint for which points are claimed.

You must respect the landowner’s property. Cross fences at posts or climb through the wire. Re-close any gates that are shut.

You must be responsible for your own wellbeing by having suitable food, water, clothing and equipment with you. Competitors shall not discard any litter.

Plan your course well. Rogaining is as much about careful route choice and navigation as it is about physical endurance. For fit, first-time people, plan on about 2 to 2.5 kms per hour average over the whole time you are on the course. If you find you travel faster or slower, adjust your course as you go.

Note: Neither the organizers nor the landowners accept liability for any accident or injury to participants during the event. The ‘Team Contact’ on the entry form must ensure all team members are aware that they participate at their own risk.
Event Information
Location:Allow 30 minutes travel from Blenheim to the event centre. Orienteering signs will be placed at key locations for directions. Final travel details will be sent to the team contact closer to the event.
Terrain:Undulating farmland to moderately steep hill country, ranging between 300 and 900 mtrs. Relatively clean with tracks and some scrub, knee high grass and vegetation in places. Hazards include electric fences (which will be easy to cross) and under-runners. Gaiters recommended.
Map:Pre-marked A4 size, 1:25,000 scale with 20m contours. Out of bounds areas will be overprinted. One map per competitor. Maps are not waterproof – bring a water proof bag or map case.
Clothing &
Equipment:		We recommend that every competitor carries at least the following minimum extra clothing and equipment:
- Waterproof parka
- Gloves & Hat (polypropylene or wool)
- Upper and lower body clothing (polypropylene or wool)
- Basic First aid (including sunscreen)
- Whistle
- Adequate food and water for the duration of the event
