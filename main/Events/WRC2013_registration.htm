5th December 2012

 

Registration for 2013 World Champs in Russia, 26 & 27th July 2013
 

Note the date change of the event (advanced by one day) since the initial announcement.

Registration will open 15th December at 00.00hrs Moscow time on the event website http://wrc2013.com/. Entries are limited to 1200 people. Some people will pre-qualify for entry by reason of performance in past WRC and regional championships and to exercise this right of entry their registration must be in by 28th December. (See http://wrc2013.com/reg/entry-criteria for the list of qualifying events).

Registration proceeds in three phases which determine the process and priority by which registrations are accepted. (See Bulletin1 for the timing of the phases). It appears to be a complicated process as specified in http://wrc2013.com/reg/entry-criteria but the long and short of it is that to maximise your chances of gaining entry, whether by pre-qualifying or not, make sure you enter in phase 1, i.e. by 28th December.

Notwithstanding the above, the organisers think it unlikely that the limit of 1200 entrants will be exceeded in phase 1 so we expect later registrants may gain entry – but no guarantees.

Note that a new UltraVet category, with automatic pre-qualification right of entry (must enter by 28th December), has been introduced for this WRC. See Bulletin1 and the entry criteria.

Please pass this message on to all your Rogaining mates and contacts who may be interested in WRC2013.
