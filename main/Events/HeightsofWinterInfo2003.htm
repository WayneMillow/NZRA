A rogaine is a cross-country, endurance, navigation event on foot, using a map and compass. Teams (of 2 to 5 people) are given a standard topographical map with about 40 checkpoints marked on it. The checkpoints have differing points values. Teams collect points by visiting as many check points as possible over the 12 hours, two-and-a-half of which are in darkness. The team with most points wins.

As a team you choose your own route and distance within the mapped area. You decide which checkpoints to visit and in which order. Hence rogaining is as much about careful route choice and navigation as it is about physical endurance. You can be as competitive or as social as you choose. A team can go out ‘on course’ for the full time, or just a few hours. And then there’s an evening meal after the event, included in the entry fee.
