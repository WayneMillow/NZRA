‘2nd Port Hills Twilight Rogaine’
3 hours
14th March 2004

A Few Details


Download:  This Information sheet (6KB PDF)


Registration:opens at 5.00pm. Maps and control descriptions issued at that time. Plastic bags will be available for the map.
Control card:issued just prior to the start at 6.00pm. (You might need something to tie it to).
Parking: is tight at the Sign of the Bellbird. Could teams that are bringing more than one car please meet somewhere and come up in one car. Takahe and Kiwi are spots to leave cars.
Toileting:One toilet is available at Start/Finish. Others are at the Sign of Kiwi and Victoria Park.
Finish: After passing through the finish shoot teams will be asked to total their scores. These will be checked so results can be given out as soon after the Finish as possible.
Penalty:Teams will loose 20 points for each minute late or part thereof.
Supper:A supper will be available in the main building after you finish
Navigation:The area covers some quite rugged country with many bluffs, rock outcrops, native forest and scrub. Teams will need to travel on the network of tracks for sections of the course. These may be steep, wet and slippery in the shaded sections.
Dark:It is planned that the final ¾ - 1hour will be in darkness so you will need to carry torches if you propose to stay out for the full 3 hours.
