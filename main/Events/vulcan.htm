Mt Vulcan Station, North Canterbury
From Christchurch. Travel north on SH1, past Waipara to Omihi School.
Turn right into Reeces Road and follow for approx. 10 km, then follow O-signs. Assembly area will be on left just past a sign that says “The Rock”.
Allow 75 mins.


Terrain: Open hill farmland to intricate spur-gully, from sea level to 411m. Many gullies have native bush; also stands of pines, coastal edge with magnificent views; plus a geologically fascinating area of ‘moving’ land.

Something for every one                 Enjoyment guaranteed
