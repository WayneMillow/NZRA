YODA Naseby Rogaine
Preliminary Information
Bob Cunninghame, 1st January 2004


Preparations for the YODA Naseby Rogaine are progressing well. Once the orienteering is finished there (about a week or so from now) they can start putting out the "controls".

It will be a cryptic clue - but not too cryptic - event.

  • Twelve hours option - 9.00 am to 9.00 pm
  • Six hours option - 1.00 pm to 7.00 pm

A meal will be provided (included in entry fee) and catering will be by/behalf of Naseby Ice Rink Fund Raising Committee.

Date as previously advised - Saturday March 13th; entries will close two weeks before this date - Saturday February 28th.

Entry fee (same fee for 6hr and 12hr options) -

  • $40.00 over 18yrs
  • $25.00 18 years and under.
NOTE entry fees are probable, but depend on final cost of meal.
