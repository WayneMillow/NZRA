27th January 2007

Alan Wilson will be offering transport between Blenheim/Picton and the event. He has a 30 seater coach and anyone wishing to use this needs to contact him directly. Phone 03 579 3290 or 027 223 2578, e-mail: AKWilson@xtra.co.nz

Program

Friday March 2nd

Meet the Interislander at Picton that arrives at 9:15pm and travel to Blenheim (30 minutes)

Saturday March 3rd

Depart Blenheim 7am to be at Rogaine site around 8am for Registration.

Sunday March 4th

Depart Rogaine site 2pm for Blenheim & Picton (Interislander departs 6pm).

The coach will remained at the Rogaine site during the event, excess gear can be left on the coach.

Cost

$35 – Picton and return to Picton

$28 – Blenheim and return to Picton or vice versa

$20 – Blenheim and return to Blenheim

Please book with Alan Wilson (contact details above), to make sure of a seat and pay when you get on the coach.
