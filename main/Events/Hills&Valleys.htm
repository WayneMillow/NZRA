WHAT IS A ROGAINE?

A rogaine is a cross country navigation event, in which teams travel across varied terrain using a topographical 1:50000 map and a compass. Teams choose their own route to visit various check points in any order. The check point is located from the map and either the cryptic clue fixes the final location of a small coded tag, or there is a conventional orange and white flag marking the control site. The team with the most points wins. NZRA rules apply except that the maximum number per team for this event is four. Navigation is by compass, watch and the course map only. No GPS receivers, altimeters (including watches with altimeters) or other maps. Cell phones may be carried, but are for use only in emergencies.

For further information about rogaines see What is Rogaining on the New Zealand Rogaine Association website.
