Registration for 13th World Rogaining Championships
Saariselkä, Northern Finland
22nd & 23rd August 2015
 

Registration for the 13th World Rogaining Championships in Northern Finland (Lapland) will open 1st December 2014.

Entries are limited to 1000 people and the entry selection criteria and process is available on the WRC website. Whether the limit will be reached is a matter for conjecture but is likely given that the event is in Europe.

Some people will pre-qualify for priority entry by virtue of their placing in previous world, regional (European, Australasian, North American), or national championships. Specific events and placings that satisfy the pre-qualification criteria will be elaborated on the WRC website prior to the opening of registration. All Pre-Qualifying Entrants wishing to take advantage of their Pre-Qualifying Entrant status MUST register before 15th January 2015.

Even teams who do not pre-qualify should also register before 15th January. These teams will have priority over teams entering after 15th January.

Please pass this message on to all your Rogaining mates and contacts who may be interested in WRC2015. Keep your eye on the event website, http://wrc2015.rogaining.fi/, for any further information.

Note: the U20 age-group category in the World Championships has been replaced with a “Youth” U23 category.
