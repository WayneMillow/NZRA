New Zealand Rogaining Association

Dunedin Rogaines

 

Saturday 23rd September 2006

 

Silver Peaks and Mt Allan Forest

 

A long-distance navigation event in conifer forest, tussock country, native beech forest and regenerating scrub.

Download your own copy of this notice (30KB PDF).

 

 

General information

•         Teams of 2 minimum and 5 maximum

•         Anyone 16 or under MUST be accompanied by an adult  (21 & over)

•         Twelve hour and six hour options

•         Entry forms available shortly on the NZRA website and at local outlets

•         Entries close Tuesday 12th September

 

Entry fees :    Seniors (over 18 yrs) $25.00 per person

                        Juniors (18 yrs or less) $20.00 per person

                        Family (2 seniors maximum) $50.00 per family

 

A discount of 10% applies for entries received by Saturday 2nd September.

The closing date for all entries is Tuesday 12th September.

Late entries may be accepted at the organisers’ discretion.

 

The entry fee includes basic light refreshments at the completion and drinks throughout the day at the start/finish area.

Registration for the 12 hour rogaine will be in Mosgiel (exact location to be advised) from 5.45 - 7.15am. The start will be at 8.00am at the start/finish area, which is a 35 minute drive from Mosgiel.

 

Registration for the 6 hour rogaine is at the start/finish area from 12.15 pm and the start is at 1.30 pm.

 

Directions to the registration (12hr) and start/finish locations, along with general information and comments on the map and terrain will be advised to all team contacts – the earlier you enter, the earlier you will receive this information!

 

Special Offer: There will be a combined discount of $4.00 per person for those who enter both the Rogaine on Saturday and the DOC orienteering on the Sunday. Discount vouchers will be available at the Rogaine for those who want an easy potter on Sunday to loosen up after the Rogaine!
