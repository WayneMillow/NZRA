 		 Korowai Office Hours Rogaine
Canterbury high country

Sunday 2nd December 2007

Organisers - New Zealand Rogaining Association

Come and join the New Zealand Rogaining Association for a pre-Christmas rogaine on the fringe of the Canterbury high country. It’s a seven-hour event, just like a short day at the office. Your team, of 2 to 5 people, will use a topographical map to navigate around a series of check points. While the underlying aim is for you to visit as many checkpoints as possible in the seven hours, you can either make a race of it, or use the event as an opportunity to develop your map reading skills. Or, you may do both of these, or go somewhere in between. Which ever you choose, all teams from elite to beginner/trampers use the same map and set of check points, but some will cover more ground than others. Remember, rogaining is as much mental challenge as a physical one.
