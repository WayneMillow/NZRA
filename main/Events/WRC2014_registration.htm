Registration for 12th World Rogaining Championships
The Black Hills, South Dakota
16th & 17th August 2014
 

Registration for the 12th World Rogaining Championships in the Black Hills of South Dakota will now open 1st November 2013. Entries are limited to 1000 people.

Some people will pre-qualify for entry as stated in the WRC Entry Preface on the WRC website. To exercise this right of entry they must register within the first 14 days of registration opening.

Even people who do not prequalify should register within the first 14 days of registration opening. Registration proceeds in three phases which determine the process and priority by which registrations are accepted. For details of the process and the phases see the full entry criteria document (PDF version). It appears to be a complicated process but the long and short of it is that everybody, including those who do not prequalify, should register in the 14 days of phase 1 to maximise their chances of gaining entry.

It is unlikely that the limit of 1000 entrants will be exceeded in phase 1 so we expect later registrants may gain entry – but no guarantees. Remember registration begins 1st November. Keep your eye on the event website, http://peakassurance.net/, for any further information.

Please pass this message on to all your Rogaining mates and contacts who may be interested in WRC2014.
