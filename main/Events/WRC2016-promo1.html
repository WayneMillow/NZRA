Make Your Plan To Visit Australia

Preparations ramping up

Dear Rogainer,

It is now just over 12 months until Australia hosts the world's best - and our preparations for the 2016 World Rogaining Championships in Central Australia are well underway.

The course is already set, as you can read in the report below. We've also just launched a new website which will give you a real flavour of the location and our event.

We encourage you to plan your trip to Central Australia for July 2016 now!

Best Wishes

2016 World Rogaining Championships team

Setting the 2016 Championships

The World Rogaining Championships course setting team has just returned from a fantastic week at the event site. We were continually awestruck at the ever-changing rugged natural beauty of the area and the technical challenge of the navigation. This will be a WRC for both the elite rogainers who are prepared to back their physical capabilities and navigational skills, and the social rogainers who enjoy a navigational challenge in some of the most majestic and attractive terrain on the planet.

Over the week we taped, checked and vetted all of the checkpoint sites on the WRC course. We also set up a ‘model’ course of 21 checkpoints that will be available for competitors to try in the days before the World Championships, helping overseas and interstate visitors acclimatise to the amazing central Australian landscape.

The map quality will be superb, thanks to brand new mapping data of the full course area from aerial mapping company, Aerometrex. We now have a map that lives up to the quality of the terrain, and it will be used for the first time at the World Championships in 2016.

Every keen rogainer should plan to attend this event. It will be a rogaine that all who do it will look back on as one of the great experiences of their lives. Check out the photo gallery on the event website to see the treat you will be in for. The checkpoint sites are now ready and waiting, they just need you!

Course Setter – David Baldwin
