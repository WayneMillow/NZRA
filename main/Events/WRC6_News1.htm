6th World Rogaine Championships
IRF Newsletter No. 1
Gordon Birch, IRF Event Manager


As many of you will be aware I visited the USA last May and was shown where the world championships are to be held. My general impressions of the region are:-

  • The area which was selected by the setter John Maier has been chosen with a navigation challenge in mind rather than difficult terrain. John is looking to use about 65 controls to try to ensure that there are lots of route choices, be you a tourist or a serious competitor.

  • The region itself seems to have two distinctive features within the terrain -

    • Firstly it has, for the lack of a better phrase, rolling grass prairies.
    • Then it has old open forests in which you can easily become lost.

    Overall I reckon that about 30% of the course is in open country and about 60% in forest. I also noticed that there are lots of hand rails (tracks).

  • The animal life of the region seems to be birds, ground squirrels, and deer that have white rear ends, which I reckon are caused by the cold when they sit down in winter. I also believe that there is lots of other wildlife that I did not see but you may see.

  • The climate for the event will probably be what is better described as spring - fresh, cool, and with a little bit of a chill in the air. When I was there which is about the time the event is being held there was still a little bit of snow in the gullies.

  • Finally I must say a word about the organising club's (Tucson Orienteering Club) attitude to catering. What the club does is ask a local caterer to advise how much food they think is required then they say double it, and the club reckons that works very well.

Lastly here is my commercial which may be helpful for anybody going to the USA for the first time. I am using a travel agent in Tucson to book the American content of my trip. His name is Michael Baker and his Email is notme1989@hotmail.com. I have found him very good at finding the best deals for car hire, hotels etc. In case you ask, no I am not on a “spotters fee”. I just believe that he does his best. If you mention that you are a rogainer he knows where the event is to be held so will book you in within a 30 minute drive of the event. (He is sworn to secrecy so will only tell you about 2 weeks in advance of the event where you are staying). As a matter of interest I am staying in Las Vegas which is about 7 hours drive from the event.
