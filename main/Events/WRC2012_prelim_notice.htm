2nd November 2011

 

Registration for 2012 World Champs expected to open 1st December

The entry criteria and registration process for the 10th World Rogaining Championships in Czech Republic, August 2012, are currently under vote within the IRF and should be announced about 12th November. We will publish the criteria document here on the NZRA website, http://www.rogaine.org.nz, as soon as it is available. Any change to the process indicated below will also be notified via the website.

It is expected that registration will open 1st December on the event website http://www.rogaining.cz/wrc2012/. Entries are limited to 1000 people. Some people will pre-qualify for entry by reason of performance in past WRC and regional championships and to exercise this right of entry their registration must be in by 14th December. (The qualifying events will be specified in the mid-November announcement).

There should be a significant pool of places available for non-prequalifying teams which may be subjected to a ballot. To be considered in the ballot non-prequalifying people must register by this same date (14th December). Teams registering after this will have a diminished chance of gaining entry.

Each IRF member country is allocated 6 wildcard teams for people who record a significant performance not accounted for in the criteria, for people who are playing a major role in the development of the sport and/or the organisation of rogaines, or for people who miss out on a place via the routes above. Subject to confirmation, the New Zealand wildcard selection panel and selection process will be announced by mid-February 2012 and the last date for wildcard nominations is likely to be 1st March. Note that to be eligible for a wildcard, teams must register for WRC2012 in the normal way as above.

The principal message to note is that the first phase of the entry process is expected to follow a tight timetable. People registering after 14th December, whether they prequalify or not, will have a diminished chance of gaining entry.

Please pass this message on to all your Rogaining mates and contacts who may be interested in WRC2012 and keep your eye on the NZRA website for the mid-November announcement.
