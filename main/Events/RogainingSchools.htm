Rogaining School

Port Hills, Canterbury from 3rd February


18th January 2009

The following announcement is from Tim Farrant, current Junior World Champion.

"A newly established under 23 group will be running a series of public trainings (open to all ages) around the Port Hills during the weeks leading up to this year's New Zealand Rogaine Champs. The series will cover a number of Rogaine specific skills that should prove handy for novices and the experienced too!

The trainings will run at 6:30pm on Tuesday evenings, starting Tuesday 3rd February, at Sign of the Kiwi. Cost will be $3 for a map. Please register your interest with Tim Farrant at tjf35@student.canterbury.ac.nz

Also:
Peninsula and Plains Orienteering club will be running a series of street score events on Wed nights, starting on the 21st of Jan. The first event will be held at Hansens Park in St Martins. Check out www.papo.org.nz for more details."
