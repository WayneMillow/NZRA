The "Hope"- full Shoestring Rogaine

A Marlborough Orienteering Club event
Download:  This Information sheet (8KB PDF)

When:Sunday 4 August 02
Where: Somewhere between the Wairau and Awatere Rivers, Marlborough. Registration/Start venue will be announced 27 July 02.
Courses: Long - 6 Hours, Short - 3 hours
Starts: Long - 8:00am, Short - 11:00am Maps and control description available for planning 1 hour before start. Controls will be pre-marked on maps.
Registration: Long - 6:30 to 7:00am, Short - 9:30am to 10:00am.
Cost: $4.00 Orienteering Club Members, $8.00 Non-members.
Entries:Entries and entry fees need to be received by the MOC Treasurer, PO Box 787, Blenheim by the end of the day, Wednesday 31 July 02. Absolutely no late entries!!!

Contact Barry Hope (Planner) 03 578 7168 or email king.hope@wave.co.nz for an entry form and further queries.

Classes: Individual or Teams (2, 3, 4 or 5). There are no categories i.e. Open, Mixed, Vets, Juniors etc.
Other: BBQ sausages, onions & bread available for purchase. Bring along any of your own refreshments, a seat, change of clothes and positive vibes.

Dogs are not permitted at the event/venue.

There are no prizes.

Standard Rogaine rules apply. Please refer to the NZ Rogaining Association website www.rogaine.org.nz for a copy of these.
Finally: The MOC "Hope"-full Rogaine is a casual event primarily to provide people with an introduction to rogaining while still offering a challenge and further rogaine experience for previous rogainers as well as adding a bit of variety to the clubs orienteering calendar. The event will appeal to the complete novice through to the experienced rogainer. See you there - somewhere!!!

Note: Southern Traverse teams - come to Marlborough for a weekend of training and incorporate this event into your schedule.
