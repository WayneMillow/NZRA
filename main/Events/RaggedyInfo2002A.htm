Bruce's Comments

Organisation for the Raggedy EPIC is progressing. In fact I spent an awesome day putting out checkpoints in the mist this weekend. Above the mist the sun was shining and the views were awesome. In the mist the navigation was challenging and interesting. I'm pretty excited about the area. Lots of similar looking rocks, gullys and spurs so it pays to stay in contact with the map as relocation isn't that easy !!
