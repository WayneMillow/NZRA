Rogaining Explained

Rogaine:(1) A proprietary hair restorer for discerning gents; (2) a cross-country, navigation challenge for discerning outdoorsy folk

A rogaine is a cross-country endurance and navigation event similar to orienteering but larger-scale. Teams of 2 to 5 are given a topographical map of the event area, overprinted with a network of (in this case) 30 checkpoints. The checkpoints, spaced about 1 km apart, are located on landscape features such as hilltop, distinctive tree, stream junction, or prominent rock outcrop, each marked in the field by a small flag.

Your team’s challenge is to visit as many checkpoints as possible in the activity (in this case 7 hours). Each checkpoint is worth a number of points and the team that accumulates the most points is the winner. Course setters place emphasis on creating navigational and route choice challenges to make teams think – the event is as much a navigational challenge as a physical one. All teams start together, and use the same overall course, but select a route and number of check points that suits their ability and interest. (even elite teams are unlikely to visit all checkpoints). Many teams are not interested in competition and regard a rogaine as a day tramp with a novel navigation element. Rogaines are great for building awareness of landscape and natural surroundings, and practicing how to read a topographical map and to navigate. Come along and try ….
