Location

            West of Waikouaiti and East of Middlemarch; about a 1hr drive from Dunedin. Detailed travel instructions will be emailed to competitors a week before the event.

 

Terrain

            A variety of rolling land and steep hills with a mix of clean open grassland, tussock grassland, scattered scrub, and some native bush in the gullies. There are several old volcanic cones in the area (part of the Dunedin volcanic shield) interspersed with Central Otago schist as the underlying rock. Both volcanic and schist, rock outcrops and cliffs are scattered across the map. The lowest and highest points are 200m and 700m and the average relief over the map is 200m.

 

Map

            The map is based on the topo map NZMG260 series, is A3 in size at a scale of 1:50 000, and has 20m contours. It is pre-marked with 59 checkpoint locations and there are 4 water drops. Out of bounds areas are overprinted. The map will not be waterproof so bring your own waterproofing or use the A3 ziploc plastic bags that will be supplied. See the planners report for more information about the course.

 

Weather and astronomical

            The event is 4 days before the full moon which occurs on Wednesday March 11. The moon will be 79% illuminated.

 

Sunset:             20:16, Saturday

Sunrise:            7:23, Sunday

 

Moonrise:         18:07, Saturday

Moonset:          03:17, Sunday

 

The weather in Otago is normally settled at this time of the year. However, as usual the weather will be unpredictable and competitors will need to be equipped for anything from very hot to cold blizzard conditions. In some weather conditions low cloud and fog can cover the high ground very rapidly.

 

Hashhouse

The hash-house is based at a large woolshed. There will be some shelter but in strong winds robust tents are advisable. Camping and parking will be available at the hash-house site on Friday, Saturday and Sunday nights. Toilets will be available. Shower facilities will not be available.

There is plenty of under-cover shelter in the sheep yards, but limited space in the wool-room where administration and meals will be managed. You should be prepared to do your planning in your tent or car if necessary.

During the event food will be provided at the hash-house from 6:00pm on Saturday, and will be available until 1:30pm on Sunday. Note that there will be no food provided on Friday evening so those who camp at the hash-house that evening will need to supply their own.

 

Transport

            Depending on demand, buses or mini-buses will be organised from Christchurch, Dunedin airport and Dunedin city, to arrive at the hash-house site about 7pm on Friday evening. Departure from Christchurch is at 1pm Friday, 6th March from outside Science Alive in Moorhouse Ave. The Dunedin bus will depart from outside the main airport terminal at 4.30pm and from the Gardens in North Dunedin at 5.30pm (shopping will be available at a nearby Supermarket from 5pm to 5.30pm).

People travelling by bus are advised to bring all their food and supplies with them; however, the bus from Christchurch will stop for 30 minutes in Oamaru for takeaways and shopping. The Dunedin airport bus will stop for 30 minutes in Dunedin as noted above.

The return buses will depart from the event site at 2pm Sunday. These buses should reach Dunedin airport by 4pm and Christchurch city by 8pm.

The bus fares are $98 return from Christchurch city and $35 return from Dunedin airport or Dunedin city.

The bus coordinator is Dave Laurie, lauriefam@xtra.co.nz, ph. (03) 332 4455. Please contact Dave if you have any queries about the bus transport.

 

Accommodation

            Camping will be available at the hash-house site on Friday, Saturday and Sunday nights. Alternatively, there is a variety of accommodation available at Waikouaiti, Palmerston, Middlemarch and Dunedin.

 

Categories

Men – Open,    Junior (under 20), Veteran, Superveteran

Women – Open, Junior (under 20), Veteran, Superveteran

Mixed – Open, Junior (under 20), Veteran, Superveteran

University Challenge (24hr event only) – genderless (unisex) teams. Details of scoring are given in the notes below. Teams in the University Challenge also qualify for other categories.

 

Notes –

        i)      in a Veteran team all members must be 40 or over on the first day of competition; in a Supervet team all members must be 55 or over on the first day of competition; in a Junior team all members must be under 20 on the first day of competition.

       ii)      Teams to consist of 2 to 5 people.

     iii)      All members of a team must fall within the team category

     iv)      Any team with a person under 15 must also have a person over 20.

      v)      The University Challenge is open to full-time students enrolled in a programme of a minimum of 3 years at any University or Polytechnic. Teams consist of 2 to 5 students from the same institution. A team’s points are calculated by multiplying the team score by the number of members in the team. All students from a given institution contribute to the points so the bigger the teams, and the more teams, the higher the overall score.

            These events will be run according to the standard championship rules available at http://www.rogaine.org.nz/main/CompetitionRules.htm . A copy of the rules will be included in the information package emailed to team contacts about one week before the event.
