Sat & Sun May 22nd & 23rd
Duration: 24 hours
Location: To be revealed on the day		 TWALK
None of this silly sleeping business. Come and see why people keep coming back year after year. Or even just come once as a "bucket list" event. TWALK is a twenty-four hour event. It is split into five legs, each around 15-20 kilometres long. Each leg has around 10 - 20 controls, which will be hidden on features marked on a map and described by a cryptic clue. You will be given an event map at the beginning of leg one and a list of cryptic clues. The first leg is compulsory, as it leads from where you are dropped off to the Hash House. From there, competitors may choose to participate in as many or few legs as they choose, leaving when they like (there is a compulsory 30 minute break between legs). Legs two - five form loops which end up back at the Hash House, and can be cut short if required. See TWALK for more details.

May 29th & 30th 2021
Duration: 24 hrs
Location: Burra - SA, Australia		 Australasian Australian Rogaine Championships
"Deceptive Lands" is the name for the Australian Championship near Burra on 29-30 May 2021, hosted by the South Australian Rogaining Association. This will also be the Intervarsity Championship. The event map covers some beautiful Mallee country, with a complex drainage system and deep erosion gullies and spurs. Visitors can plan addtional time to enjoy some of the local attractions such as the historical township of Burra and the wine regions of Clare and Barossa Valleys. This event was planned to be the Australasian Rogain Champsionships but with the current Covid-19 status, travel is likely to be restricted. The event will still continue and everyone will be very welcomed to enter. See ARC 2021 for more information.
