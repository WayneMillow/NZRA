Location: Canterbury
 T'Walk 2013
It is on yet again!! This will be the 47th running of this iconic event, organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club, the pioneer of long distance foot navigation events in NZ. It differs from a Rogaine in that teams follow a fixed route. It is great fun and a "not to be missed" event. See the event website.

Saturday 8th June
Duration: 10hr & 6hr
Location: Geraldine area

 Heights of Winter 2013
In a break from tradition the annual Heights of Winter rogaine heads south of Christchurch to Woodbury, near Geraldine. This year there will be a 10hr option (replacing the usual 12hr event), a 6hr option, and a 2.5hr event for local schools. The organising/planning team (Stuart Payne, Martin Etherington, Mike Harding and Dave Laurie) have found some fantastic country. The terrain includes rolling farmland to more rugged hills with streams and a small river. The height variation over the course is approx 450m. Vegetation is variously open farmland, tussock country, and some wooded areas. Entries close Wednesday 29th May. See the PAPO website for complete information.
9th thru 30th June
Duration: 3hrs
Location: various NI
 Navquest Series
The NavQuest is a series of four 3hr rogaines aimed at secondary school students, their families and teachers. Members of the public are more than welcome to join in by entering in the open category. Go to http://www.opc.org.nz/navquest/ for more information and to register a team online. The NavQuest is organised as a partnership between The Sir Edmund Hillary Outdoor Pursuits Centre of New Zealand (OPC) and NI orienteering clubs in Rotorua, Auckland, and Wellington. Coordinating organiser is Darren Ashmore.

 Australian Rogaining Championships 2013 (incorporating the 1st Australasian Champs)
Rogaining North Queensland will host the 2013 Australian Rogaine Championships on the Atherton Tableland, a 40 minute drive from Cairns International airport.

Sunday 28th July
Duration: 3hrs or so
Location: The Bull Ring, Flagstaff, Dunedin		 Hamels Haunts III
Organised by Matt Bixley, this years rogaine is a TRAINING event and MTB friendly. It’s on because there are some adventure races and multisport events coming up and this Hamels Haunts is meant to give people a chance to practice navigating, route finding, riding, transitions, team-work etc. in a non competitive environment.
       You choose your start time (between 1pm and 3pm), you’ll be handed your map and go from there. Map reading, Navigation, Trekking and Riding will be at levels expected for the Dunedin Mazda Adventure Race and for the Spring Challenge. Checkpoints will vary from very easy to some more difficult ones for those that want more of a challenge. Coaching/Advice will be given to groups that would like it before they head out.
       Cost $10 (under 15 free), there will be soup/bread at the finish, no prizes so it’s only bragging rights, and I’m not even sure if there will be results, but there probably will be. See http://runlongergeek.wordpress.com/rogaine/ and https://www.facebook.com/events/269102113232051/ for up-to-date information. Matt's contact is matt.bixley@agresearch.co.nz or 021 046 9255.

Saturday, 10th August
Duration: 90mins
Location: Broken River Ski Field		 Broken River Ski Rogaine
Calling all orienteers who can ski or skiers who like a map challenge with their skiing! The annual Broken River Ski Rogaine is on Saturday 10th August at 2.00pm (backup Sunday if closed). Join in a race to visit as many controls to gather maximum points as you can without going overtime – choose from the 90 minute ‘open’ class (that will entail a bit of hiking) or the 45 minute ‘family’ class, team or solo. Entry just $2 per map, with some spot prizes at 3.45pm. You only need a watch and a pen; compass may be handy but not essential. Normal lift fees apply $70 adult, $56 for CSA (other ski club members), $50 student with ID, $35 youth 11-17 years, under 11 ski free, $175 for family.

If you’re new to Broken River ski field check out http://www.brokenriver.co.nz/; sing out when you arrive on the field and we’ll arrange a local to take you on a tour around or contact the organiser and PAPO club member Sally Dickson for more info before the day membership@brokenriver.co.nz, or ph 338-0801. Hope to see some new faces joining in this year!.

Duration: 10hr, 6hr options
