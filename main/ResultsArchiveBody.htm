  • NZ Rogaine Championships - November 7th & 8th - 24hr, 15hr & 6hr - Hanmer Springs
    Over 80 teams of over 200 competitors lined up at the start. They were promised an honest course that would challenge even the best and it did. They were also warned of an impending southerly front. In the first nine hours everyone enjoyed the challenging terrain. Route choice was important. Often travelling "cross-country" involved fighting through thick scrub (matagouri, hebi, kanuka ETC). Gaining height was rewarded with good points and great views but at an energy cost. After about 9 hours, the first mist arrived together with the darkness. This was followed by the rain building in intensity and an accompanying lowering of temperature. Many teams decided to visit the warmth of the "bush cafe" in the north-west of the course , while others returned to the event HQ to dry off and even grab a couple of hours sleep. Despite all of this there were many tired but happy faces at the finish with great stories to tell. Yet another NZ Champs for the folklore.
