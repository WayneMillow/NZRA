Calendar of Events 2004

6th - 7th March
Duration: 24hrs
Location: Wairamarama,
near Auckland.		 New Zealand Rogaine Championships 2004
This event, organised by the Auckland O Club in conjunction with NZOF, will double as the 2004 NZ champs. There are 16hr and 6hr non-championship options. Wairamarama is close enough to Auckland to drive to the event start/finish on the day. Camping will be available at Wairamarama itself for those who are interested. The Wairamarama area is a lovely bit of country. Read the event information here and download your own copy (28KB PDF). Download an entry form (20KB PDF).

8th - 9th May
Duration: 24hrs
Location: Canterbury

This will be the 36th running of this event, organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club. It differs from a Rogaine in that teams follow a fixed route. A "not to be missed" event. Consult the T'Walk website.
