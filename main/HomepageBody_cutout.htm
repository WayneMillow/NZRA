Australasian Rogaining Championships
May 9th & 10th 2020
  Northern Ranges - Goyder Council, South Australia The event map covers some beautiful Mallee country, with a complex drainage system and deep erosion gullies and spurs. In addition to the 24-hour championship, there will be an 8-hour event commencing and midday on Saturday 9th May. World Rogaining Championships
August 1st & 2nd 2020
  These will be held in Sierra Nevada, California, USA. The competition area is about 200km2 in size, located in the Sierra Nevada mountain range. The average altitude in the area is around 2300m, the highest point is 2665m, and the lowest point is about 1900m. Slopes are mostly moderately hilly with some steep areas and deep ravines. Snow covers the area for 7-8 months of the year, which contributes to the forest being very open and runnable with just small patches of thick vegetation. The forest ranges from open grassy areas to mainly pine forest and some rocky granite outcrops. Streams in the area will be mostly low during the summer. See WRC 2020 for more information.
For those keen, there will be a weeks of Orienteering available prior to the event. See California Orienteering Festival

Series of Short Rogaines - Break up the week.

      Summer Series Wednesdays Mar 18th & 25th, April 1st - 60 minutes - Rotorua
OBOP Summer Series - Rotorua - 60 minute rogaine. Do as individual or as team. Go as easy or hard as you like, collecting as many controls as you can in the time allowed. Simply turn up on day to enter. Check out www.obop.org.nz for more details.

      Summer Rogaine Series Wednesdays Mar 18th & 25th - 2hrs - New Plymouth
Compete in teams of 2-5 people. Navigate to landmarks (eg signs, buildings, sculpture) which are used as 'controls' Record the answer to the question about the 'control'. Points are allocated to each 'control'. Teams collect as many points as possible in 2 hours. See orienteeringtaranaki

      Wicked Autumn Series Wednesdays Mar 25th, Apr 1st & 8th - 2hrs with family options - Christchurch Port Hills
These events are always well patronated. What better way to break up the working week. Put these on your calendar. For a wicked time check out Wicked Rogaines for more details or on facebook.

      Monthly Afterwork Rogaine Series Thursdays - April 16, May 5, Jun 10, Jul 7, Aug 11, Oct 14, Nov 12 - Wellington, Hutt Valley area
All at different locations in the Wellington, Hutt Valley area. Put these in you calendar and break up the week. More details at Orienteering Hutt Valley.

      Don't Get Lost Rugged Rogaine Sundays Apr 19, May 17, Jun 14, Jul 12 - 2 & 4 hours - Different locations in the Waikato
The Don't Get Lost Rugged Rogaine Series is a fun way to explore the beautiful Waikato countryside, experience an adventure sport, practice map reading and navigation, and improve your fitness. Run, jog, or walk - you set the pace. By yourself, in a team, or as a family. Fun adventure or a competitive challenge and race For more details see orienteeringwaikato.org.nz.

      2020 Rogaine Series Sundays Apr 19, May 3, May 17, Jun 7 - 90 minutes - Different locations in the Auckland area
This year's series of 4 Foot Rogaine Events will be held in April, May and June 2020, with 90 minute rogaines only. They will be suitable for all levels, from total beginners to experienced rogainers. This will be a great opportunity to improve your navigation over a series of fun events. For more details see Northwest Rogaine Series Auckland.
