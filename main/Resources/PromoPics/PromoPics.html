The NZ Rogaining Association engaged Canterbury sports photographer Mike Chappell to take a series of photographs suitable for promoting rogaining in New Zealand. These were taken at the 2016 Heights of Winter rogaine in the Ashburton Lakes basin, organised by Peninsula and Plains Orienteers. The images cover course planning and field navigation aspects of the event.

A selection of these photographs is available to rogaine organisers, participants and others for bona fide use in publicising and promoting rogaining.

Please acknowledge the source in any public use as 'Mike Chappell/NZRA'. You may also wish to credit the event as Heights of Winter, Ashburton Lakes Basin, 2016.
