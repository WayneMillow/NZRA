World Rogaining Championships Entry Criteria
 

The IRF has established Entry Criteria for World Rogaining Championships (WRCs) in recognition that there is always likely to be a finite limit on the number of entries available for any given WRC and the popularity of our sport is continuing to grow. We have already had WRCs where the number of people wanting to enter has exceeded the available places and this situation is expected to become more common in the future.

In establishing these Entry Criteria the IRF has been mindful of the following:

  • Ensuring that well performed teams in all age/gender categories from Regional Rogaining Championships will be able to compete in the WRC
  • Ensuring that top performing teams from the National Rogaining Championships of IRF Members will be able to compete in the WRC
  • Recognition that the make-up of rogaining teams is not constant
  • Maximising the national and geographical diversity of competitors at any WRC
  • Providing an opportunity for regular rogainers, rogaining administrators and key volunteers who are not amongst the most successful competitive teams to compete in a WRC
  • Allowing access to WRC entry for a limited number of people who are not regular rogainers but whom, by virtue of their performance in related sports, could reasonably be expected to be competitive
