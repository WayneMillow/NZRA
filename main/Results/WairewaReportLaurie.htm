A Long Walk to the Hangi
Wairewa Rogaine
Little River,  23rd July 2006

As experienced by Dave Laurie


What a way to start a rogaine with a special welcome followed by tea and biscuits. I saw a few slipping the odd chocolate biscuit into their pocket for later on. The day was warming up nicely as we took off down the road towards Akaroa. It seemed just about everyone else was going the same way. As we walked briskly along we were soon at the tail end as most teams jogged past. In a few minutes we had the first control in hand, and we knew the meaning of the word as well. It was only a short distance down the side road before we were over the fence and up the hill. What a haul. The views slowly improved as we gained height, and took our mind off the grunt up to our second control (32). We could see teams on the other side of the valley slowly gaining height as well so clearly they didn’t all come our way. We then contoured a bit to 72 before we started climbing again to 70. Luckily we passed teams coming back from the 100 pointer so that gave us a guide into the cliff. Didn’t know that word. Around the hill to the fallen log, but which one, there were many, an obvious one stood out but no luck there, so keep going - finally found it closer to the stream. Almost missed the next one at the stream junction (62) as we split up to check each side. Caught up to the young guns (Tim & Rhys) who were out to beat us oldies this time. Around the hill to 90 and then retraced our steps across to 80. Deer fences galore in this bit; I think we climbed over 5 before we got to the pond and 42.

Out to the road and there parted company with the young guns. A quick glance at the watch told us we were behind schedule and so might have to give away 30 and 52. Over the footbridge and into the backyard of a great old homestead. Out the back gate and things didn’t look good so back through the gate and down past the veggie garden. Tried a couple of routes here and finally pushed our way through blackberry and scrub to the clear spur on the other side for 53. At 63 we had to make a decision as we were now an hour behind our planned schedule. Luckily Andy came up with an alternative low route and so we gave away the top ridge. Round the hill we contoured to 44, enjoying the great views of Little River township and the cars speeding past below. We became bushed on our way around to 91 and so began to descend into the river with the aim of crossing over. Fortunately we came across a farm track (Bruce did mention that you could be lucky) only a little below us which took us nicely round past the first junction. We then had a short descent into the river and contoured up along the other side to the waterfall. It was a slow haul from there up to the ridge and 82. It didn’t look promising to contour around to 64 so a further climb was needed. We then decided that as we were within 1-2 contours of 100 we might as well get it and perhaps drop out 61. As we climbed the last few metres to the ridge the young guns were roaring down the ridge towards us. They had come around the ridge from 71 and were heading down to 82 and 91.

It was certainly well worth the grunt up to the ridge for the view over Lake Ellesmere and the southern coast. That’s the great thing about rogaines you get these gems that come out of the blue. Well no more climbing as we headed along the fence towards 64 & 50, giving 43 away to our right. The snow under foot seemed to hold and allowed us to jog for a while. As we sidled around the rocky knob we realized that we were part way to 61 and had time to collect that as well. A tricky bit of rock climbing was needed to get us up the last few metres to the top. Luckily the control was back on the trig thanks to Mondo who had retrieved it earlier in the day.

Well just 40mins to go and we were still 400m up on the hill. Around to the gully where 50 should be. Luckily we spotted what could be a clump of cabbage trees below a new road and followed a disused fence line to them. Out onto the spur for a fast run to the road. Nearing the bottom I misjudged getting over an electric fence and ended in a heap on the ground getting a good belt in the process. At 40 we meet up with the young guns again and we all did a slow jog down to 10 and 21. Back to the main road to pick up another 20 pointer at the railway station and followed Chris and Michael in with 15sec to spare.

Finally the hangi which was well worth the walk and capped off a great day.

Dave Laurie
