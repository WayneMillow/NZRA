Using Google Earth to display map and routes

Google Earth must be installed on your computer.

  • Click on the Map and Routes link above. The next step depends on which browser you are using and the settings within it but select either
    • "Open file" to start Google Earth directly from the browser or
    • "Save" or "Download" the file, note where the file is saved to, and load it into Google Earth by double-clicking on the file or using File Open... within Google Earth.
  • Select a team (or teams) in the sidebar to view a route. Zoom the map in and out with the mouse wheel as necessary. Highlight a route by pointing at a segment of it. Click on a route segment to identify the associated team.
  • You can switch the map and controls on/off in the Google Earth sidebar to see the underlying terrain.
  • Display the Elevation Profile of a team's route by right-clicking on the team in the sidebar and selecting "Show Elevation Profile". Slide the mouse pointer over the Elevation Profile for more detail and to display a large red arrow on the map showing the position of a particular elevation point and the direction of travel.
         Remember, though, that the distance of the route, and the corresponding elevation gain/loss, shown by the Range Totals in the Elevation Profile are for a straight line between checkpoints and as such have doubtful usefulness. What Rogainer will go over a hill, or down and up through a valley, if a longer route around is judged more efficient?
