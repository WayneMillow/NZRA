Australasian Championships 2014
Fossicker’s Dream
Freyers Range, Victoria, 11th & 12th October

 

A relatively small contingent of 14 NZ Rogainers competed in the Australasian Championships in the Freyers Range and Upper Loddon State Forests, near Malmsbury, Victoria on 11th & 12th October. The event was extremely well managed by a team of organisers whose experience was clearly apparent. The course was in very complex spur/gulley terrain and almost completely in bush, but the bush was quite open and relatively clean underfoot. The weather was fine and stable which meant that people could travel light but the moon was a few days past full so teams were challenged by about 4 hrs of complete darkness between sunset and moonrise.

In a remarkable performance Kiwis Georgia Whitla and Greig Hamilton defended a spirited challenge from the seasoned Aussie campaigners Julie Quinn and Dave Baldwin to take out the overall title by 20 points. Equally inspiring was the Veteran pairing of Robert Jarvis and Phil Wood who claimed both the Mens Open and Mens Veteran trophies in a repeat of last year. Phil and Robert finished 4th overall.

Viv Prince teamed with her usual Ozzie partner, Richard Robinson, to take out the XSV title, while the evergreen oldies, Pete Squires, Anne and Bill Kennedy, won the XUV - by default since they were the only XUV team. More significant for the Oldies was that they were 2nd XSV and 3rd XV.
