Mt. Outdoors Earnscleugh Rogaine

17th September 2011

 

Report by Pete Squires

 

The Highland Events 12hr Rogaine on 17th September was a classic. For anyone who has memories of “hidden checkpoints” or “cryptic clues” or “matagouri bash”, forget it. This event was as good as it gets.

 

The checkpoints were clearly visible, accurately placed, and unambiguous. The terrain was varied and beautiful, but sometimes navigationally demanding. No problem if you had good skills. Travel varied from “fast” to “Hmmmmm”  –  but this was well indicated on the map.

There was plenty of route choice, and enough checkpoints to challenge even Chris Forne. Everyone was forced to make choices and decisions. There was no obvious “best route”. The area close to the start/finish had an abundance of options, allowing for a wide range of finishing alternatives.

 

The after-match cuisine was very well managed and very appropriate and delicious, and with Ed’s handicap system nearly everyone got a spot prize.

 

For anyone who is serious about rogaining, and improving their National and International skills, events like this are perfect. I urge you to attend the ongoing 12hr events put on by Highland Events (Ed Stevens and Terry Davis). They often fill a void in the event timetable and they are worth travelling to! Watch the Highland Events website http://www.highlandevents.co.nz/rogaine or NZRA website http://www.rogaine.org.nz/ for future information.

 

Pete Squires
