The Nenthorn Nemesis
26th & 27th February 2005

Course-setter’s and Organiser’s Report
Download PDF version (12KB)


At the end of the first, hot afternoon and with only five checkpoints marked, I wondered if I would ever finish in time for the event. It seemed a very daunting task! Checkpoints are not so easy to find when there is nothing to mark them and no tracks leading through the long grass! However with each day you know the map better and it all goes more smoothly. Help from Laurie Kennedy also made a big difference. Early on it was necessary to spend time reassuring some landowners that rogaining is a low impact sport and all of the phone calls and visits do take up time and involve a lot of driving. It all worked out well and their cooperation is much appreciated.

There were a large number of checkpoints, but this was necessary on relatively fast country to ensure that the top teams were out for the full time. As it turned out, the winning and other high-scoring teams were out for almost 24 hours and achieved very high scores; my congratulations to them.
