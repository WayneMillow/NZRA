Organiser's Thoughts

Peggioh Merry-Go-Round
New Zealand Rogaine Championships
Marlborough,  3rd & 4th March 2007

Alan Holdaway


Relief first comes to mind. No one in hospital, no missing teams and no land owners with complaints.

Gratitude to my extended family who are always available to help.
 To all the landowners for their graciousness and support for the event.
To Barry Hope and Susan King who did most of the initial contacting of landowners (their neighbours).
For the weather!

Satisfaction that competitors were positive about the course and that many have taken happy memories from the weekend even if it took a few days for the pain to go.

Enjoyment from the challenge of planning a course for the NZ Champs.
From many hours spent driving and walking the properties in the planning process.

Amusement at how old some people look after 24 hours on the go and no sleep.

Highlights
 The close race for 1st place with the winning team only taking the lead on Sunday morning.

The success of HH2 – it was really there for safety but we had rave reviews about cheese toasties and soup in the middle of the night being the highlight of the course.

"The best lunch I have ever seen at a 24 hour rogaine".

Low points
 Putting my ute into a creek and having to ask the farmers to get it out (their help was greatly appreciated).

Being completely disoriented on the Peggioh Ridge while placing the controls in 20m visibility. I walked off on a spur that headed down into Blue Mountain Stream and was saved only by my GPS from a cold miserable night lost up there. Thank God it was fine for the event.
