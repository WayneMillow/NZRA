Could   "Morrissey & Christison (38)"   have beaten   "Fa'avae & Ussher (45)" ?
Analysis by Janette Holdaway



The teams followed practically the same route for two thirds of the event: Team 38 did 102-88-78 in a different order, and got 70 extra points at control 70.

However by 2am team 45 got to control 100 sooner, and after 4am they took different routes.

Team 45 finally got in front in points after 10am. Team 38 scored last at 10:16am, and Team 45 at 10:56. So the ability to keep going and collecting checkpoints to the end made the difference.
