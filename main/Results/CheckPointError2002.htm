‘HEIGHTS OF WINTER’ 2002

Checkpoint Error



OOOps. We stuffed up. Sorry.

The circle marked on your maps for Checkpoint 70 was definitely in the wrong place. The right place for the circle was where the checkpoint actually was, on the sharp ridge. The grid reference is 763 006.

How we stuffed up is not quite clear. We have several theories:

  1. A pig shooter shifted the circle.
  2. A GPS reading got translated to a different number.
  3. Aliens from outer space put a crop-circle on the map in the wrong place.
  4. We stuffed up.
  5. A pig shooter shifted the flag.
  6. All of the above.

We will go through the check cards and credit the 70 points to any card where a query has been raised. Watch for updated provisional results on the website. When these are available, if you think your score is still wrong, contact us within a week. We will then publish the final results.

We know that no amount of point adjustment will compensate for the effects a wrong checkpoint has on teams. All your strategy planning and timing is altered, sometimes with considerable detriment. Again, we apologise, and will look at ways to double-check this problem in the future.

Pete Squires.
