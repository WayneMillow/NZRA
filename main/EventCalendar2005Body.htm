The 24hr event incorporates the New Zealand Rogaining Championships for 2005. Event information, entry form and publicity poster are available. see who has entered. Sunday 10th April
Duration: 3hrs
Location: North Canterbury
 MT VULCAN
A 3hr Rogaine in open hill farmland with intricate spur-gullies, from sea level to 411m. Planner: Stuart Payne, Controller: Dave Laurie. More information.
14th - 15th May
Duration: 24hrs
Location: Canterbury
 T'Walk
This will be the 37th running of this event, organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club, the pioneer of long distance foot navigation events in NZ. It differs from a Rogaine in that teams follow a fixed route. A "not to be missed" event. Entry forms, T'walk rules and other details are available on the T'Walk website. Entries close 6th May.

Thursday 9th, 23rd June
     & 7th July
Duration: 2 & 3 hr options
Location: Christchurch
Start-time: 6pm		 Taylors Mistake Rogaine Series
This is a fundraising series organised by MADeventz (Mental Adventure Discovery) for New Zealand's World Champs orienteering team. Each event will focus on a different part of the area bounded by Godley Head, Lyttleton and Sumner. This is a fascinating area with an interesting diversity of landforms, from native bush to rolling hill tops, rocky coast line and pleasant beaches.
