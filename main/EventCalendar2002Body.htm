Calendar of Events 2002

3rd Feb 2002
Duration: 3hr & 6hr
Location: Taranaki		Billygoat Rogaine
See Organiser's story and results on the NZOF website.
13th April 2002
Duration: 8hrs
Location: Lower Hutt		 Eastbourne Rogaine
The Hutt Valley Orienteering Club continues its tradition of organising rogaines with this great event in April. See the story and results on the NZOF website.
25th - 26th May 2002
Duration: 24hrs
Location: North Canterbury
Entries close: 17th May.		 T'Walk
This is the 34th running of this event, organised by the University of Canterbury Tramping Club. It differs from a Rogaine in that teams follow a fixed route. Organiser: Chris Forne. For details see the T'Walk website. Check out the Results for this year's event.
22nd June 2002
Duration: 12hrs
Location: North Canterbury		 Heights of Winter
This popular event will take place again this year. See Event Details for information and to download an Entry Form. Error in Checkpoint 70 is confirmed. Check out the Results and Organisers' report.
26th - 27th July 2002
Duration: 24hrs (1pm - 1pm)
Location: Czech Republic		 5th World Rogaining Championships
This is (obviously) a championship event run under the rules of the IRF. For more details see the Czech website. See the report by Pete Squires.
27th July 2002
Duration: 3hrs & 6hrs
Location: Waitakeres, Auckland		 Multisport Bikes Piha Rogaine
Organiser: Phil Wood, Lactic Turkey Events. Email: phil@lacticturkey.co.nz or Ph. (09) 634 8104. Entries close 22nd July or on the day with your own map. Results and pictures on the Lactic Turkey website.
4th August 2002
Duration: 3hrs & 6hrs
Location: Marlborough		 The "Hope"- full Shoestring Rogaine
Organised by the Marlborough Orienteering Club, this is a casual event with appeal to the complete novice through to the experienced rogainer. Planner: Barry Hope. See Event Details for more information. Results and comment are available.
24th August 2002
Duration: 12hrs
    (9:30am to 9:30pm)
Location: Ophir, Central Otago
Entries close: 9th August.		 RAGGEDY E.P.I.C.
A brand spanking new event around one of the coldest places in NZ, and at the coldest time of the year!! Organiser: Bruce McLeod. Email: bmcleod@es.co.nz or Ph 03 441 4447. Rogaine style route choice with Twalk style cryptic checkpoints replacing the normal control flags. More details and organiser's comments. Results on EPIC website.
12th October 2002
Duration: 3hrs & 6hrs
Location: Waitakere ranges, West Auckland		 Multisport Bikes HUIA Rogaine
Organisers: Phil Wood, Ph. (09) 634 8104 and Shaun Collins, Ph. (09) 817 7621. Email: phil@lacticturkey.co.nz. See the Lactic Turkey Events website for more information. Results and pictures are now available on the Lactic Turkey website.
2nd November 2002
Duration: 12hrs
Location: near Queenstown
Entries close: 18th October		 The Queenstown EPIC
Its back!! Held twice before, and having missed last year, this popular event is back with a vengeance!! Organiser: Bruce McLeod. Email: bmcleod@es.co.nz or Ph 03 441 4447. Rogaine style route choice with Twalk style tags and clues replacing the normal control flags. More details and entry form are available on the EPIC website. Results on EPIC website and impressions from Dave Laurie.
