Maori Language Week Awards 2006

WAIREWA ROGAINE AWARD WINNER


On 14 September the NZ Rogaine Association and Wairewa Runanga were declared winners of the "Sports" category of the 2006 Maori Language Week Awards.

The Awards night and dinner were held at the Westpac Stadium (a.k.a. the Cake Tin) in Wellington. Bruce Meder (representing the Rogaine Association) and Maatakiwi Wakefield (representing Wairewa Runanga) accepted the award and thanked all those that had put work into making the Wairewa Rogaine such an unique event.

"It is certainly an honour to be acknowledged in such a way at a national level" said Bruce, "especially as rogaining is not a well-known sport". Of the 14 awards presented that night, this was the only one to come to Te Waipounamu (the South Island).

On Sunday, 3rd December, the trophy (a carved mere) was formally taken on to Wairewa Marae and blessed. Bruce, the proud Dad, is pictured with the mere.

Congratulations to Bruce on his initiative. See Bruce's report, E WHAKAHOKI ANA NGA KUPU I ROTO I NGA PUKE (PUTTING THE WORDS BACK IN THE HILLS) and also the results with winner's route, pictures, and other narratives.
