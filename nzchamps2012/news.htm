Results (25th November)

The results and a map of the winners' route are available. You can download a pdf version of each from the results page.

Final Instructions to Competitors (17th November)

Download comprehensive instructions covering directions to the event centre and organisation at the event. All competitors must read these instructions, particularly the sections on the course and safety.

Entries now open (5th October)

Entries for the Moonlight on Mahinerangi Rogaine (incorporating the New Zealand Championship 24hr event) are now open via the online entry form.

Transport (5th October)

Transport will be provided from Dunedin city and Dunedin airport for people requiring it and booking it with their entry. The schedule is under "Transport" on the event information page.

Promotional Poster (10th August)

Please let as many people as possible know about Moonlight on Mahinerangi: download and distribute a promotional poster.

Entry Form (28th July)

The entry form will be available when entries open in early October

Age Restrictions (28th July)

Please note that, for safety reasons, in the 24hr event any team with a person 16 or under must also have a person 21 or over. For the 7hr event any team with a person under 14 must also have a person 18 or over.
