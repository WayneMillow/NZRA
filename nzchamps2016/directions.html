Directions

The event center is at El Dorado, end of Mount Watkin Road which runs off Ramrock Road out of Waikouaiti

Based at El Dorado woolshed  end of El Dorado (Mt Watkin) Road. From Waikouaiti travel west on Ramrock Rd for 3km to Mt Watkin Rd, then north/north-west 14km to the end of El Dorado (Mt Watkin) Rd.
Mt Watkin road is mainly unsealed however it is perfectly suitable for a road vehicle.

  • Dunedin Airport to Waikouaiti: 68km
  • Dunedin to Waikouaiti: 40km
  • Oamaru to Waikouaiti: 72km
  • Waikouaiti to El Dorado woolshed: 16km
