Accomodation

Come and stay in Akaroa and enjoy the magnificent scenery of Banks Peninsula. The Top 10 Holiday Park has five cabins and good campsites, and there are some Air BnB's also. Have a relaxing harbour cruise on Sunday - see penguins, dolphins, outstanding landforms, historical places, and much more.

Checkout Stay in Akaroa for some options.
