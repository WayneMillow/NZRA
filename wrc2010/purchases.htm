Purchases
(Updated 21st September 2010)

 

Southern Hemisphere Compasses

The MAPsport Shop will be operating at the event center with a range of orienteering and rogaining equipment. It is run by rogainer Michael Wood who will be competing so pre-ordering is encouraged. You can see what they provide at www.mapsport.co.nz.

In particular, visitors from the northern hemisphere will need a compass made for the southern hemisphere. Your compass from home will not work properly. The MAPsport Shop is offering a special deal to 9WRC participants on the Silva Ranger 3 compass, for orders placed by 20th August. Check it out, and place your orders at the MAPsport Shop.

The MAPsport Shop will post a compass to you anywhere in the world provided you order it soon - allow at least 2 weeks for delivery. Limited stocks will be available at the Rogaine.

Chartered Bus Information (Christchurch to Cheviot and return)

Buses will be provided from Christchurch to Cheviot on Friday afternoon and Saturday morning. The return buses will run on Sunday afternoon. The last date for ordering a bus seat was 20th September 2010.

Pickup Locations –

  • City (Worcester Street) – People wanting to travel from the centre of the city should wait at the coach stop on N side of Worcester Street, between Oxford Terrace and The Square.
  • University – People wanting to travel from the University of Canterbury (Ilam) should wait in the carpark of the Students Association, E side of Ilam Road, between University Drive and Kirkwood Avenue.

Buses will be identified by a large “Rogaine” sign in the front window beside the driver. A WRC2010 representative will travel on each bus to supervise organisation and departure of the bus. Please be at the pickup point at least 5 minutes before the departure time.

Note: It is the responsibility of people flying into Christchurch on Friday to ensure they allow adequate time for flight delays and finding their way to the pickup location. The bus will not wait.
