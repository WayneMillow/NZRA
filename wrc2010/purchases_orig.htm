Southern Hemisphere Compasses

The MAPsport Shop will be operating at the event center with a range of orienteering and rogaining equipment. It is run by rogainer Michael Wood who will be competing so pre-ordering is encouraged. You can see what they provide at www.mapsport.co.nz.

In particular, visitors from the northern hemisphere will need a compass made for the southern hemisphere. Your compass from home will not work properly. The MAPsport Shop is offering a special deal to 9WRC participants on the Silva Ranger 3 compass, for orders placed by 20th August. Check it out, and place your orders at the MAPsport Shop.

 

Online Shop (all ordering has been disabled)

The following items are available via the on-line shop -

  • Apparel (Fleece top, Cap, and T-Shirt)
  • Bus transport
  • Registration of accompanying persons
  • Meal at the Hash-house on Friday evening
  • For each item ordered please enter the appropriate team Registration ID and person's name so that we can provide the service or item to the correct person. Apparel will be available for pickup at the Registration desk at the event.
  • To purchase more than one of a given item just add it more than once to the cart.
  • Payment is by secure credit card transaction via Paypal. (At checkout select the credit card option unless you have a Paypal account).
  • Full payment must be made with order. All prices are in NZ dollars.
  • The last date for ordering any of the items from the online shop is 20th September 2010.
  • For purchases cancelled before 20th September 2010 there will be a 75% refund, less bank charges; later than that no refund.

If you have any difficulty with ordering the items below please notify registration@wrc2010.org.nz the organisers by email.
